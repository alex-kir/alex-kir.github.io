Without a past, without a future.

The present Wan Tiansheng was the one and only Wan Tiansheng.

He was a ninth-stage Sunmoon. Not an Invincible, and not a Pseudo Invincible. Yet he was also in an incredibly unique state. He had no past and future, yet he constantly threaded the River of Time, remaining at his peak at all times.

He was as strong as he could have been in the past. He was also as strong as he could be in his future.

Such was the dao he walked. A dao nobody had walked before, and a dao nobody else could comprehend.

The traitor had appeared. It was only a future body, but this was enough. After losing this future body, the identity of the traitor would be exposed.

The human Invincibles were currently gathered in groups to protect the human Pseudo Invincibles who were attempting their dao affirmations. The moment one of them lost a future body, the other Invincibles in the group would be able to sense it. The only way the traitor could remain hidden was if the others in his group were also traitors.
