Chapter 2203: Su Yu's Fury (1) (Teaser)

Rather than relying on others, it was better to be self-reliant. That had always been Su Yu's belief. This belief was mostly shaped by the environment he grew up in.

Since he was weak, he only had himself to rely on. First, he relied on the Book of Time. Liu Wenyan was crippled, and Bai Feng was unreliable with his obsession with research and nothing else. At first, Bai Feng's strength was still sufficient as a backer. But the moment Su Yu grew into a Skysoar himself, Bai Feng hadn't been able to catch up.

In fact, Bai Feng had been incapable of backing Su Yu even when Su Yu was still at the Mental Tempering Stage. Even back then, Su Yu was already facing Sunmoons. And then, Nan Wujiang and Yun Chen appeared to back him, but he felt distant from them. He could never make himself rely on them.

Liu Wenyan and Bai Feng were his teachers. His family. Wan Tiansheng and Lan Tian were his fellow daoists. His friends. They were very close to him, but they were still different from Liu Wenyan and Bai Feng.

These days, Su Yu rarely visited Bai Feng and the others. He was happy to see them focus on research.

At Su Yu's current level, Bai Feng's talent was clearly insufficient to keep up anymore. Su Yu couldn't get too involved with Bai Feng, as that would probably result in a funeral for Bai Feng...
