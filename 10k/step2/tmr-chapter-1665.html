Chapter 1665: Collapse (2) (Teaser)

Among the myriad race guests.

Mo Duona was frowning as he stared at Great Zhou King and the others before looking at Su Yu. Finally, he looked at those from Great Yue. At the moment, the Great Yue prefect was sitting there silently with his head lowered, as though he was trying to hide into the ground.

But since the prefect was hiding his face, Mo Duona couldn't see anything unusual about the prefect. After thinking about it, Mo Duona looked at Topsoil Spirit. At the moment, Topsoil Spirit's eyes were flickering.

He could sense the atmosphere slowly turning stern.

And it was growing in intensity.

Su Yu was a tad bit too overbearing today. He was also a tad bit too rude toward the founding kings. He had always been overbearing, but he was not the type to act so disrespectfully. Had he gotten too full of himself after becoming the holy lord?

It was hard to say.

However, Great Xia King and Great Ming King were among his strongest supporters. Yet he had still been so rude toward the two.

Topsoil Spirit sank into deep thought.

Did something happen?

What was it?

The biggest matter to happen today should be Su Yu's crowning, right?...
