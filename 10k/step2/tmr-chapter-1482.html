Chapter 1482: Who's Silly? Who's Dumb? (2) (Teaser)

Before long, Su Yu attached his own willpower onto the 109th golden stripe. The Frost Divine King noticed nothing.

The Frost Divine King had only left a small amount of his willpower at a single spot in the weapon to maintain his control over it. Su Yu was uninterested in what was happening outside. He couldn't care less about all the schemings going on out there.

It also wasn't his place to worry about the humans. He shouldn't be biting off more than he could chew. With four human Invincibles present, there was no need for him to worry that the humans would sink into too big of a disadvantage.

It was also likely that there were more hidden allies of humanity among the other races. Thus, Su Yu was completely unconcerned about them. He refused to believe that humanity would be doomed without him. After all, the human race was filled with cunning old foxes. They would be fine.
