Chapter 1277: Center Of Attention (1) (Teaser)

Once again, Su Yu visited the inner hall.

Grandstar opened his eyes.

He noted that Su Yu would grow noticeably each time he took his attention off the kid for a few days.

He would usually not say anything about it, but he decided differently this time as he said, "Your heaven acupoint is incomplete. You can't stop it from absorbing death qi. If this continues, you will fully transform into a death spirit."

Wow. Such a knowledgeable old fellow.

Heaven acupoint. So that was the name of that acupoint. Su Yu was impressed at the breadth of knowledge this old fellow possessed.

"Heaven acupoint?" Su Yu said, "I named it the yang acupoint, with its counterpart being the yin aperture. Together, they are called the source soul acupoint."

Grandstar considered that name and said, "That is a suitable name."

"Do you know this acupoint, Lord?"

"Yes." Grandstar explained, "I have only heard about it, so I don't know too much. Even during my era, this acupoint was rarely opened."

Su Yu nodded and asked, "Lord, since I've yet to fully open the acupoint, is there a way to shut it again?"

The acupoint had been getting more and more annoying to handle. Each time he fed it with death qi, he would drift dangerously close to becoming a real death spirit....
