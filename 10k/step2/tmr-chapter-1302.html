Chapter 1302: Nine Leaf Skylotus (2) (Teaser)

At the moment, Wan Mingze was facing an old Skysoar in one of the rings. Unfortunately for the Skysoar, he was fully sealed right at the start of the match. This was what Wan Mingze had always been known for.

All his characters were related to sealing. With his numerous apertures and characters, the Skysoar was completely helpless against him.

Wan Mingze did not even bother to attack. He merely sealed his opponent for a short moment before releasing the seal. Just as his opponent was about to attack, he sealed his opponent again.

After several repetitions, the Skysoar was forced to surrender. Ji Hong and the other higher-ups watched silently.

Meanwhile, Wu Yuehua frowned as she raised her head to look at Wan Mingze. Suddenly, she asked, "It feels like this kid...lacks something."

When the others looked at her, she explained, "From that youth army he formed, it is clear he has great ideals. And he is also quite capable. However, in the path of cultivation, can one truly win the acknowledgment of everyone with such soft methods? Compared to the likes of Su Yu or even Huang Teng, Wan Mingze seems to lack a certain decisiveness and ruthlessness."
