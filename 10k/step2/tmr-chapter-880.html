Chapter 880: Thinking About You (2) (Teaser)

Inside the Skyrend Valley.

Inside Su Yu's sea of willpower, the tribulation character pulsed again. The pulse was weak, signifying that the threat wasn't too big. But there was still some danger. What was going on?

Su Yu was confused. The character had pulsed out of nowhere. He waited a while, but he saw nothing.

"Was it broken?"

But that wasn't possible. In that case, at that exact moment, something related to him must have happened. And that something was capable of bringing him danger.

"Whatever. I'll stop thinking since I can't figure it out for now."

What could he do if he had zero clue? He just had to stay cautious. His current form, a broken sword, was wrapped in a layer of heavenly source qi. Both saber qi and divine power were gathered around him.

He was on the verge of completing his thirteenth body forging. This place was too good for body forging.

Suddenly, he stopped all action. Even the layer of gold around the broken sword faded away, revealing a rusty sword. Accompanying the rusty sword were several piles of bones and some other broken weapons. This blade blended perfectly with its surroundings.

A short while later, a figure appeared noiselessly. This was not someone from the shadow race. Rather, this person was using some unique concealment ability. That was Su Yu's conclusion since he was quite familiar with the shadow race. Sure enough, the newcomer turned into a large bug resembling a grasshopper.

"Dark locust!"

Su Yu recalled something. This was a sub-race in the greater locust race. They possessed excellent concealment abilities, but this race wasn't considered a major race due to a lack of Invincibles.
