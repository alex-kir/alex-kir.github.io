Chapter 1417: Death Follows (3) (Teaser)

"So this is the second level?"

After killing his welcoming party, Su Yu started looking around. The second level was different from the first level. Or to be precise, it was very different. The second level felt like a world of flame. Fire and lava filled the level.

Yellow Nine said, "The second level is known as the Flame Mountain. In the legends, a blood fire devil and fire elemental had battled here during the ancient past. This world was the aftermath of their battle."

Su Yu laughed and said, "The so-called lava here is merely the blood vessels on the neck. Why do you believe in such fairy tales?"

What nonsense was that? Unlike the first level, the second level did not resemble anything on a human body. This was indeed a world of flame. But equipped with the knowledge he had gained from the first level, Su Yu was able to easily determine that the rivers of lava were essentially the blood vessels on someone's neck.

Yellow Nine did not argue. If Su Yu's theory were correct, this could indeed be the neck. However, the thought of that was still so ridiculous she did not dare to even entertain it.

That would be a fact far more terrifying than the legends about this place.

If Su Yu was right, it could only mean that an incomparably powerful ancient expert had been killed by the Human Emperor before being slammed head-first into the ground. The hair of that ancient expert had stabbed right into the Death Realm while the rest of his body had been forged...
