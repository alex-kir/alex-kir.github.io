Invincibles had fallen.

Fiery Sea King did not count. He was a traitor. Nobody would mourn his death. But this was different.

Inside the square of Nanyuan mayor's residence.

Silence descended.

Everyone was shocked. Everyone was confused. Some even started exclaiming in panic.

"The myriad races are attacking!"

Three Invincibles had fallen. This could only happen if the myriad races were attacking! Humanity was finished! The Human Realm was finished!

The myriad race guests were greatly alarmed as well. They all gathered warily, but they didn't dare to flee.

Just what was going on? Three Invincibles had died.

While sipping his liquor, Su Yu smiled and stepped onto the stage. Overlooking the gathered crowd, he shouted, "Silence."

Some obeyed. But some continued running around in chaos. Su Yu's eyes lit up as a burst of aura erupted from his body.

At that, all the people who were running around in chaos coughed blood and collapsed.

Finally, the world was silent.

Su Yu coldly said, "This sky above us. It has yet to fall. Why are you shouting? Pieces of trash."  

His calm was infectious as some calmed significantly after looking at him. He continued, "I'm not dead. I'm still here. Why panic?"
