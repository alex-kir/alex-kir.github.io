Chapter 1921: Upper Realm Iron Devourers (1) (Teaser)

The iron devourer territory was located in the south of the Upper Ream.

In truth, the Divine Fire Mountain was also located in the south. The Heavenly Hole Range, where South Suppression Marquis was hiding at, was also located somewhere in this region. 

In fact, the southern region of the Upper Realm was a rather complicated area. The iron devourers, a race friendly to humans, were based there. Several human Daofuses were hidden there. The human survivors were also relatively active in the south.

The iron devourer race could also be considered a powerful race. Their dao center was not a city. Rather, it was a large mountain range known as the Bamboo Mountain. The south was a mountainous region. The iron devourers might not have a large number, but they lived a free and unfettered life in their mountain range.

***

In the Upper Realm, a dao center was generally a place of habitation backed by a Daofuse.

There, the Daofuses would spread their powers of laws in the air to make it clear that the territories belonged to them. Outsiders could not intrude upon these locations...
