Silkwyrm Realm.

This was a massive world.

Suddenly, the massive silkwyrm was roused awake. He instantly transformed into a humanoid form. Clad in a white outfit, Silkwyrm King walked out of the palace. As he whirled his fingers in the air, streams of incorporeal power converged around him from all over the realm.

His expression changed slightly as he started looking around. One Sunmoon after another appeared before him.

"King, what task do you have for us?"

"..."

"Is Wang Hu here again?"
