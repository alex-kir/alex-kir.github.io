Chapter 1439: First Cloudbreach Transformation (1) (Teaser)

Inside a random valley, Su Yu released his Mark of Civilization and covered the heavenly rewards he had received. The saber qi in his 144 acupoints was fully dispersed. Before this, he could be considered a pseudo Cloudbreach. One whose transformation had stopped at 144 acupoints.

He had planned to complete his transformation through multiple cultivation methods. However, Grandstar had claimed that this would cause an imbalance of his acupoints. That was why he had started his pursuit of the grand cycle cultivation method. He aimed to transform all 360 acupoints together. 

Since the unique source qi from the heavenly rewards contained the power of the heavenly laws, his transformation was made simpler and stronger. Depending on one's foundation, each transformation would provide a different level of boost in strength.

That was only fair since building one's foundation was hard work. The unique source qi started tempering his acupoint one after another. His acupoints grew brighter and brighter. Each acupoint was as dazzling as a star.

His transformation began.

Su Yu felt so touched that he almost teared up. Finally, he could become a proper Cloudbreach. It had been almost two full years since he first joined...
