Chapter 1940: Weird Human Emperor (2) (Teaser)

Su Yu hesitated. His desire to study the dao was strong. If he were alone, if there weren't anyone relying on him, he would have jumped down unhesitatingly.

"Responsibility..."

Suddenly, a certain concept appeared in his mind.

Responsibility.

He still had numerous responsibilities on his shoulders. He once said that the position of the human lord represented burden and responsibility. It was no reward.

He struggled with his desire to learn more. The burden on him was too heavy. A neccessary risk could be taken, but this was not necessary. This was merely curiosity. What would happen to the people he had brought to the Upper Realm if something were to happen to him here?

"It is said that one's dream is worth dying for."

Lan Tian had said something similar before. He envied Lan Tian for being so carefree and unconstrained.

He, too, was a cultivator. He, too, wanted to be so carefree in his pursuit of the dao.

To cultivate the dao. To seek...
