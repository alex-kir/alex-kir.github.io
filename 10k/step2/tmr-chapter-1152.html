Chapter 1152: Nanyuan Ruin (2) (Teaser)

Wu Ji snorted before looking at the blood fire devil with contempt, "Count yourself lucky. The blood fire devils are my favorite devils to kill." 

The blood fire devil expert did not seem to care. He said, "It's fine. You're free to kill more. My race does not lack experts."

Yes. His answer was surprisingly simple. His race did not lack experts. Losing some here and there did not matter to them. Excluding the Invincibles, they had just as many experts as the original devils.

How did they produce so many experts? Combat. That was the most straightforward way to produce experts. Of course, one was not guaranteed to grow just by fighting. But the blood fire devils were special as they simply needed to fight, win, loot, and go back home. The devil race as a whole was left to clear up the mess caused by them.

In a way, that was the price for having the blood fire devils as the vanguard of the devil race. They had lost a lot of people over the years, but they had also profited massively. At this time, a different group of youths started stepping out of the realm entrance, accompanied by their dao protectors.

The humans had complicated expressions when they saw these people. The lower-ranked individuals did not know too much about these people, but the higher echelons...
