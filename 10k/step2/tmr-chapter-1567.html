Chapter 1567: Suppressing Myriad Races (1) (Teaser)

Chaos was unfolding in the Immortal Realm.

A ninth-degree Eternal was intricately linked to the fortune of his home realm. With Wisdom King's death, natural disasters started erupting all over the Immortal Realm. This wasn't even the first time this had happened today.

The death of each immortal king represented the exhaustion of the accumulation of the immortals. Each exhaustion would weaken the power of the realm. When enough died, the realm barrier might weaken, allowing their enemies to invade through the barrier instead of the entrance.

The three experts facing Heavendoom were distracted momentarily, allowing Heavendoom to land a hit on the Wind King. Blood splashed everywhere as the Wind King transformed into wind to flee.

Immediately after, the Fire and Lightning Kings blocked Heavendoom's pursuit as a storm of lightning and fire raged around them.

Right at that moment, another mournful wail rang out.

Far away, Empyrean Cloud pulled her city from the ground before turning it into a sword and stabbing it through Han Xiang's eye. With a snort, she said, "This is your punishment for seducing my mayor, slut!"
