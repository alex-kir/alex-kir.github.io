Venerable Moon said nothing, but he acted immediately and charged toward the Hell Gate. At the same time, Venerable Nether pulled back and faced the ancestor with Heavenly Fate Marquis. Four powerful Venerables were joining hands against the ancestor.

Meanwhile, the ancestor was in a terrible mood. This was his debut, yet he was faced with the encirclement of four Venerables. Not even Hundred Battle King had enjoyed such a treatment back then. He was greatly annoyed.

An intense battle had erupted out of nowhere, surpassing all his expectations. They had lost five heavenly kings in the short battle.

On top of that, they had lost thirty Daofuses. His heart ached as he thought of their heavy losses. Their lineage had remained in seclusion for many years, yet they had lost six heavenly kings as of late. The sixth was none other than Zi Yan, who had been killed by Su Yu not long ago. This was not a loss any race could shrug off.

 As the two sides fought, more and more ancient beasts arrived. There were about forty of them, and even more were arriving in all directions. The encirclement was almost complete. At this rate, they would need to face another battle to the death before long.
