"Yes."

"Assistant Liu Hong was the one who made you the class monitor?"

"Yeah." Su Yu answered one question after another. The old man frowned and asked, "As the class monitor, you can get three merit points per month. Do you know that?"

"Teacher Liu mentioned that."

"The more you do, the more you can gain. That is the rule of the academy."
