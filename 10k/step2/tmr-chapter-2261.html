Chapter 2261: Kill A Few Chaos Beasts For Fun (1) (Teaser)

Human Emperor had separated his spirit from his body. His physical body was heavily injured. It was likely that his sea of willpower was injured as well.

He was practically an empty shell.

Su Yu felt quite helpless upon learning that. He had been placing his hope on Human Emperor, but clearly, Human Emperor had also been waiting for his help. How disappointing. That guy was so good at acting tough in front of others, but deep inside, he was crying for help.

How sad.

Su Yu had thought that with the addition of ten or so Masters of Laws, Human Emperor could probably defeat the myriad races. After all, he had been able to keep the balance for so many years. But nope. It had been one big bluff all along.

And thus, Su Yu wallowed restlessly despite the major victory he had just won. Just when would this end?

Thanks to the new information, he realized that he couldn't go upstream rashly anymore. Things would get very troublesome the moment the myriad races felt desperate enough to trigger the final battle in advance. That empty shell of Human Emperor must remain standing.

"After getting screwed by Culture King and the others, you're doing it to me. Is that it?"

"Sigh."

A bleak sigh rang out.
