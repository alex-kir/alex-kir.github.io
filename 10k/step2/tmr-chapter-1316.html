Outside Liu City.

Su Yu stepped through the gate to enter the city. This was his first time here, and his first impression was that the city was quite a busy place.

It was a decent place.

There were quite a lot of people in the city. The biggest difference between this place and his city was the lack of death qi. Generally, one would not enter an ancient city unless there was no other option.

That wasn't the case for Liu City. Thus, there was a lot more life to this city. Humanity was the main population in this city.

The few non-humans in the city would keep a relatively low profile since this place could be considered a part of the human territory. This was the same for the territories of the divines, devils, and immortals. The members of the other races would also maintain a low profile when they were there.

Some people noticed Su Yu, but they ignored him since they didn't know him. Some recognized Liu Hong's appearance he was using, so they were quite surprised. Then again, they did not have that big of a reaction either. With the recent deaths, the grudge between the single and multiple character...
