Chapter 1946: Gather Up, Let's Kill Some Heavenly Kings (2) (Teaser)

Boom!

When Su Yu appeared outside, he blanked out. The previous battle was still ongoing.

Even the combatants were still the same.

Wait, no, the immortals were gone. It was unknown if they had died or fled. However, the masterless dao from before was still there. It was in the hands of a devil. Su Yu could feel it. There were two devils present, and they were faced with two divines, one phoenix, and one nether.

The so-called allies were fighting among themselves. They blanked out momentarily when Su Yu appeared.

A devil?

About seven or eight hours ago, an immortal had appeared before running away. As a result, the two immortals were forced to abandon the masterless dao and flee.

And now, a devil had appeared. From his aura, this seemed to be an extremely powerful expert. A peak expert of the devils? Did the dao attract him? But this was merely a regular, incomplete dao. Could such a dao attract such an expert?

"Lord!"

The two devils exclaimed in joy.

They were slightly doubtful since this was an unknown face, but they weren't too surprised since the Dao Origin Land was supposed to be filled with unknown old fossils. Some of these old fossils were not known to even those from their own races.

For example, not even humanity knew if there were slumbering humans in the Dao Origin Land.

"Six..."

Su Yu looked around and noted that there were six Daofuses in the area. He could probably kill all the others by joining hands with the two devils. But after checking around...he cursed inwardly.

He could feel that they were all holding back. Nobody was fighting with all their strength. Were these people playing around?

Upon thinking about it, understanding dawned on Su Yu. Such masterless daos would appear frequently here. If the myriad races were to fight seriously...
