Chapter 1787: Five Elemental Submits (3) (Teaser)

Topsoil Spirit said, "All of them are seventh-degree Eternals. It is too hard for them to match even one Daofuse. Perhaps this will be feasible if they are at the ninth degree. Back then, even Great Qin King, Great Zhou King, Great Xia King, and Great Ming King could only handle one Daofuse together.

"And they are incredibly powerful. To be frank, they are much stronger than the five ancestors of my race. And even people like them can barely match a Daofuse upon joining hands. And you're telling me that you can let five of our seventh-degree Eternals match a Daofuse?" 

Was Su Yu trying to send those people to their deaths? This was absolute nonsense. It was too dangerous to bluff in such matters.

Su Yu narrowed his eyes and said, "Topsoil Spirit, this is your second time doubting me."

Su Yu would not tolerate something happening three times. If Topsoil Spirit were to doubt him one more time, he wouldn't let it slide any longer.

Topsoil Spirit said awkwardly, "I'm not doubting you. I'm just worried about them. After all, I need to be responsible toward the five elemental race. I understand what you're suggesting. You wish to create two teams, each capable of handling a Daofuse. But...the cost of failure is too high."

They would lose eight Eternals to the ancient cities, and on top of that, they had to provide ten more to face Daofuses. That would leave them with only one to guard their realm. It wasn't exactly a bad plan. But it was far too risky. Failure...
