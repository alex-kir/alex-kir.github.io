Chapter 837: First Venture Out Of The Human Realm (1) (Teaser)

Su Yu remained in the room and did not try to leave.

After some time, a maid walked in and dropped a chest filled with books onto the ground and coldly said, "These are the books about the Allheaven Battlefield. The madam wants you to read them all."

Su Yu nodded and hurriedly asked, "Can I know...the surname of my martial mother?"

He knew her given name, but he didn't know her surname.

The maid looked at him like she was looking at a fool. She said, "Don't you know Ye Batian?"

"..."

Su Yu was momentarily stunned before nodding blankly. Yes. He was such an idiot. Ye Hongyan. That was her full name.

After shooting Su Yu another look of disdain, the maid left. Was this fellow slow in the head? She was nearby when they spoke about Ye Batian. Since that fellow was aware of Ye Batian's relationship with the madam, why was he asking about her surname?

Su Yu tossed the matter concerning the maid out of his mind and picked up one of the books. Books introducing the Allheaven Battlefield could also be found in the academy, but evidently, the Allheaven Prefecture had a much bigger and complete collection of books.
