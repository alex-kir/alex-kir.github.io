The aftereffects of the massive battle engulfed the world.

Even now, the numerous fireworks in the sky remained, giving the Chaos Mountain a dreamlike atmosphere. However, such a beautiful sight was created through the deaths of numerous Daofuses, more than ten heavenly kings, and one Venerable.

Deep inside the Chaos Mountain.

This was an unimaginably vast region dotted with towering mountains. 

At the peak of a certain mountain.

A figure stood with his hands clasped on his back while overlooking the world beyond. The blooming fireworks in the sky sank him into recollection. "Your Majesty!"

Several individuals appeared behind him. All of them stared at his back with both sorrow and joy.

Cloud Water Marquis looked up with boundless admiration on her face and spoke, her voice shuddering, "Shadow and the others...are dead."
