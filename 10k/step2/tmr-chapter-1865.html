Chapter 1865: New Domain (2) (Teaser)

Su Yu thought about it and said, "How about this? I'll use it for now, but I'll return it when you're needed outside. Or we can wait until humanity's fortune grows stronger and I can stabilize this place with the human lord's token." 

The human lord's token had already grown into a divine weapon. Naturally, its offensive strength was still nothing impressive since that wasn't its main function in the first place. But a token of fortune would serve nicely to suppress the laws as well. In a way, it was quite similar to that piece of stone.

"Fine."

This was an acceptable arrangement for the white dog. He sniffed Su Yu and remarked oddly, "You have so many different smells on you."

Su Yu nodded, "Yes. I am searching for my own dao."

Su Yu smiled and asked, "Fat Ball, I have a question. Why did Culture King separate himself from the pen dao instead of growing it back then?"

"I don't know." The white dog shook his head and said, "I don't ask about such unimportant matters."

"Unimportant?"

"Yeah." The white dog nodded and said, "Nothing is more important than what to eat for lunch, what to eat for dinner, and when the master will return."

Fine. Su Yu couldn't argue...
