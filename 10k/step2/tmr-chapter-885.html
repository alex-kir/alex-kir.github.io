Chapter 885: Swaggering Into The Earth Index (2) (Teaser)

Su Yu completely ignored Lei Jue as he attacked the group.

The little furball attacked as well, creating an opening for Su Yu to cut a massive demonic beast into two. After collecting the corpse, he heard the peng yelling, "If we keep running, all of us will die. Keep him busy! Lei Jue is coming!"

They were all decisive individuals. Right after those words were said, they stopped fleeing and charged toward Su Yu. One of them appeared below Su Yu with a flicker and stabbed at him with his sword.

The peng flapped his wings and chopped toward Su Yu. The others also unleashed their own attacks. One of them even used a character, but that character had disappeared right after appearing...

The white sable was stunned. Where was his character? Why was it gone? He was completely stunned. Even if he knew that this would be fatal during combat, he still couldn't help himself. What was he supposed to do without his character?

Fortunately for him, he no longer needed to rack his brain for an answer. A large hammer descended loudly. His body remained fine, but his sea of willpower was completely obliterated....
