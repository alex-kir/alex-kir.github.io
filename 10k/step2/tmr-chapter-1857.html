Chapter 1857: Destiny Child Emerges (3) (Teaser)

At that moment.

Near the end of the Celestial River of Death, Liu Hong was busy hunting a death sovereign. Suddenly, his vision blurred. A sentence appeared in his mind: "Liu Hong, address Su Yu as your granddaddy or the heavens shall punish you."

"Cough, cough, cough!"

Liu Hong coughed violently as his eyes widened. What was that? He had always been good at controlling his emotions, but this was too shocking. What even was this? Why would such a thought appear in his mind? No, that was not his thought. That was the influence of the dao. The dao was telling him to...call Su Yu granddaddy?

Holy shit!

How was that possible?

That was a dao left behind by Culture King. And Culture King had vanished for over a hundred thousand years. It was impossible for this to be something left behind by Culture King.

Alarmed, Liu Hong looked around nervously. Was Su Yu nearby? Was that Su Yu's influence? Further away, a death sovereign was staring at Liu Hong blankly. Then, the death sovereign dove into the river and vanished.

Liu Hong wanted to give chase, but he gave up since he was no longer in the mood. Just what was going on?

Suddenly, another sentence appeared in his mind: "Do it now or a tribulation will arrive."

Do his ass!

Liu Hong cursed inwardly. This must be Su Yu! Was Su Yu nearby? That fellow seemed to have grown even scarier than before. Was that fellow really capable of influencing his dao?

Liu Hong decided that since this wasn't someone he could contend against, he should continue fleeing.

Run!
