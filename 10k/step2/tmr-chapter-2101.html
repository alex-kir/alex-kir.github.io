Chapter 2101: Everything Changed (1) (Teaser)

Celestial Chasm Realm.

Su Yu was finally back. His three days of battle with Martial Emperor had further perfected his pioneered dao.

"Your Majesty!"

Everyone was relieved to see him back. They had been worried that Su Yu's fight with Martial Emperor would turn into a fight to the death. That would be troublesome. Su Yu nodded and swept his gaze over everyone. There were gains and losses from his dao pioneering.

Lanshan and Lan Tian were both heavily injured. Great Zhou King wasn't too badly injured, but he had lost much of his lifespan. But Su Yu also saw many new Daofuses among the people before him. As for those who were already Daofuses, they had grown, but not by much.

Topsoil Spirit was the one who had grown the most. He was already near the level of a tier-two Daofuse. That was a surprising speed as he had caught up to even people like September.

"Still no Venerable?"

Su Yu looked around and noted that apart from Fat Ball, none had the ability to match Venerables. Clearly, his dao pioneering had not brought these people much. After all, there were so many heavenly kings among them. Not one of them had grown to the level of a Venerable.

Su Yu was quite disappointed. He had been expecting some of them to grow into Venerables.
