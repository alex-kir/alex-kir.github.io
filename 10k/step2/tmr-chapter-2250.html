The myriad races were gone.

Their six Masters of Laws were gone.

They would rather face the unknown dangers behind the Hell Gate than face Su Yu. Su Yu had completely crushed their courage. However, the myriad races wouldn't be completely helpless behind the gate with six Masters of Laws. But the two of them knew that this might be the end of them.

Su Yu asked, "Any trump cards to pull out? Perhaps Zhou Ji will kill Martial Emperor and rush over to save you? Hundred Battle, your wife is here. Your son is busy elsewhere. Why don't you drag your daddy out or something? Or the Human Ancestor, perhaps?"

Hundred Battle King had a heavy expression.

Su Yu stretched lightly and smiled, "Since everyone else is gone, let us have some fun. Xing Yue, kill that ugly woman. I'll handle Hundred Battle personally. They say that sovereigns should never meet. Since a meeting has happened, one of us needs to die. Let me do the killing personally."
