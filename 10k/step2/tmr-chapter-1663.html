Chapter 1663: War Of Extermination (3) (Teaser)

Great Yue King had perished.

In the sky, his projection appeared. The projection was gazing to the east, the direction of his home.

Slowly, he walked toward the east with a smile. He was going home.

For a hundred thousand years, humanity had fought and fought. Many had died away from home.

Great Yue King had never been a warlike person.

Alas, circumstances had forced him to fight.

Over four hundred years ago, he only wanted to become a wealthy local tycoon. That year, Great Qin King visited him, beat him up, and forced him to join the war against the myriad races.

In that war, he was a cowardly person. Often, he would hide behind the stronger combatants.

Eventually, the war ended.

He became a founding king. The founder of Great Yue. The protector of millions upon millions of people. He became the god of Great Yue. At that thought, he had the urge to laugh in amusement. God?

No. He only wanted to be a local tycoon. Would anyone believe that?

He had done many things when the world was in chaos.

Killing humans. Killing divines. Killing devils.

He was no saint.

Yet he had answered Great Qin King's call yet again. And finally, he had fallen. Fallen away from home.

He laughed.

"Qin Guang, Zhou Tianqi, both of you are bastards. Why should I obey you? Hahaha!"
