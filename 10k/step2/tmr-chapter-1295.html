Chapter 1295: When In Trouble, Call Daddy (1) (Teaser)

Su Yu was back.

The area was packed with ancient cities.

The people of the nine realms were feeling greatly pressured by the presence of all these ancient cities. The thirty-five ancient cities had surrounded the nine realm entrances in the middle. 

The locals felt like crying, and there was nothing they could do but pray that things would remain peaceful. If the ancient cities must fight, then please let the fight not spread to their homes. This was too dangerous. Clearly, one needed a lot of courage to become a neighbor of Grandstar City.

Right after Su Yu stepped out of Empyrean Cloud City, draconic roars filled the sky. Several massive dragons appeared in the sky. Among them was a golden dragon emanating an incomparably powerful aura. Yet even the powerful golden dragon was regarding the ancient cities with fear.

When the golden dragon saw Su Yu, his voice rang out.

"Since ancient times, the Sea of Stars has always been the territory of the dragon race. We do not wish to create trouble or interfere in the assembly, but Grandstar City must leave the Sea of Stars."

If the alliance were to happen, a powerful faction would emerge in the Sea of Stars. This would threaten their interests in the region....
