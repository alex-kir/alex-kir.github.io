Chapter 1675: Carnage Unleashed (3) (Teaser)

Boom!

Great Yuan trembled violently while Great Yuan King's laughter spread everywhere.

"Su Yu, if humanity survives this, your position as the leader will be solidified. If humanity fails to survive this, you will be nothing but a piece of trash! Hahahaha!" 

Boom!

A second explosion followed as all three Invincibles died together. Even more crimson clouds covered the sky while the rain of blood grew heavier.

Great Yuan King, dead.

With his greatly injured body, he had killed three enemy Invincibles.

No, that was not all. The spear he had unleashed with all his strength stabbed through an Invincible behind Su Yu before the Invincible could react.

The said Invincible instantly lost a body. When the second body emerged, the spear exploded, killing the second body. And when the third body emerged, Su Yu's blade arrived to turn it into mincemeat.

The two remaining Invincibles paled as Su Yu roared, activated his Myriad Door Formation, mustered the strength of his crown, and threw a punch out while waves of death qi billowed out of his body.

Boom!

That one punch blew the two apart.

They could keep him tangled when there were three of them. But with only two of them, they were no match for him.

This was a result of Great Yuan King's sacrifice.

"Me? A piece of trash?"

Su Yu roared.

Who was Great Yuan King to call him a piece of trash?

Who was Great Yuan King to judge him?

Boom!

In his fury, Su Yu attacked madly. His furious attacks shattered all space around him. The sky itself changed color from his onslaught. After losing two bodies in a row, the two Invincibles self-detonated.

Boom!

The explosion sent Su Yu flying away. His body was reduced to a mess of broken bones. All his flesh had been reduced...
