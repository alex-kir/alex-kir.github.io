Chapter 1878: All-Life Dao (3) (Teaser)

"Who was that?"

"Martial Emperor?"

Wan Tiansheng was also greatly shocked.

Su Yu smiled and said, "Yes. A talented yet unlearned man. He does not understand the dao. He has no ambition or dream. He doesn't even know what he wants."  

Su Yu said with disdain, "He opened the Heaven Gate earlier than Culture King and the others. Yet his dao is only slightly stronger than a brainless heavenly desolate beast. An embarrassment to all humans. A barbaric brute." 

Wan Tiansheng nodded, "Getting himself sealed even with the Heaven Gate open. That is indeed quite disappointing. The Heaven Gate allows one to understand the dao with one look. It is disgraceful for someone like that not to have achieved anything great." 

Lan Tian joined in, "A pity indeed. Back when I entered the Luminous Domain Mansion, he felt powerful. But now...he is too weak compared to his peers. As someone with the Heaven Gate, he shouldn't have fallen so far behind Culture King and the others even if he couldn't surpass them." 

And then, the three nodded and said in unison, "Barbaric brute."

What a waste of his talent. Truly lamentable.

The three tossed the matter aside and continued moving forward. They had something more important to focus on. It was pointless to waste their energy speaking of a barbarian.

Nevertheless, Su Yu had still learned something new from this. So Martial Emperor's dao was here.

It was even further away than the pen dao and human dao. In short, just because a dao was further away did not necessarily mean that it would be stronger. Or perhaps the pen dao had moved...
