Chapter 1840: Three Marquises Fall (1) (Teaser)

Tiangu was relieved. He was overjoyed.

How fortunate.

This wasn't a big problem.

He roared, "Attack the Human Realm!"

He noticed that Great Zhou King had departed as well. This was the perfect opportunity for them to attack the Human Realm. The moment Su Yu's group was forced to return, their offensive would fail. The three marquises could even take the chance to stab them in the back.

Or they could persist in their offensive, but that would give the myriad races the chance to wipe out their Eternals and eliminate the foundation of humanity. Did Su Yu believe that there was only one marquis in the Fate Realm? Was he confident that he could end the battle promptly? What a beautiful dream.

The myriad races moved with a tacit understanding. With Tiangu's roar, Divine Pseudo Emperor, Phoenix Pseudo Emperor, Nether Pseudo Emperor, Ape Pseudo Emperor, and Celestial Dragon Marquis moved out and started heading toward the Human Realm.

The Fate Realm couldn't be taken so easily. Everyone knew that two additional ancient marquises had arrived there. Each was strong enough to fight two or even three Daofuses alone.

Perhaps Su Yu's group would even suffer a defeat...
