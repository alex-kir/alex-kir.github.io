Chapter 1866: New Domain (3) (Teaser)

Everything would start anew in the new year.

The Anping Calendar reached its end.

That year, humanity obtained a massive victory over the myriad races.

It dominated the Myriad Realms.

As the end of the year arrived, humanity started preparing for the numerous celebrations, welcoming the arrival of the new calendar.

Celebrations were held in all the human prefectures.

The various major realms were sealed, allowing humanity to sweep through the Myriad Realms. The various races were forced to either submit or face elimination, and countless resources were transported to the Human Realm.

With the constant flow of resources, a large number of human geniuses emerged. Xia Longwu and the others were taking it slow in the wars against the minor realms. Instead of attacking the stubborn races with their main armies, they sent the younger geniuses to these realms for tempering.

This was understandable as the military leaders like Xia Longwu and Qin Zhen were individuals who had killed their way up from the very bottom. They did not want their juniors to grow up in a greenhouse.

Thus, even the likes of Qin Fang and Xia Huyou were sent to the various battlefields for tempering. As of late, the most famous junior was not Qin Fang. Rather, it was Zhou Hao. After opening 360 acupoints and completing sixty body forgings, he continued tempering himself. As such, his combat strength had been growing steadily.

He might still be a Skysoar, but like how Su Yu was back then, he could kill even Sunmoons. And Zhou Hao was not alone. Some of the juniors from Su Yu's age group were also shining brightly.
