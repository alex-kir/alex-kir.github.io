Chapter 1824: Old Wan Is Still The Best (1) (Teaser)

Death Realm.

Su Yu did not stay long after taking the guardians to Grand Dream City. As for Marquis Minshan's death, he was still unaware of it. Even if he did find out, he wouldn't be too bothered.

At most, he would lament that humanity had lost another expert. It wasn't like he actually knew that person.

Since that person was dead, so be it. It wasn't that he was cold, but he did not have too good an opinion about those in the Upper Realm. The composition of humans up there was too complicated. There were traitors, fools, disobedient elders, and conceited elders among them.

He was of the opinion that they would drag him down instead of being a help. Reality had proved that he was right. Hundred Battle King was the best example of this. He didn't even care about the humans in the Upper Realm anymore. With their help, even someone like Hundred Battle King had lost. What hope could he hold for them?

He would rather walk a different path. He only needed them not to bother him. It was fine if they couldn't help him.
