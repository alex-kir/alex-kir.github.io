Chapter 1961: Dao Painting (2) (Teaser)

Everyone was staring at the painting.

And everyone was seeing something different.

Su Yu was staring at the painting as well.

He saw something nobody saw.

Darkness.

Boundless darkness.

And heartbeat

Time did not exist in the primal chaos.

Suddenly, the heartbeat ceased. A dark figure appeared.

The appearance of the figure was indistinct. The race of the figure was unclear. The only clear thing was his incomparably bright pair of eyes. Confusion could be seen in those eyes. The confused figure started traversing the darkness.

Time did not exist. Life and death did not exist.

One day, that figure roared. Reason unknown.

But the primal chaos was torn apart.

A world was created. Countless beasts came into existence amid the darkness.

There was still no time. But the dark figure seemed to have comprehended something. With a wave of his hand, a mesmerizing river appeared.

Bit by bit, it grew.

The dark figure observed the myriad races in silence. He observed all living beings in silence.

Bit by bit, he added more things to the river. The river continued...
