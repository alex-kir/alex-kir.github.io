Chapter 1647: Holy Lord Su Yu (2) (Teaser)

Su Yu understood Liu Wenyan's thoughts. Even after fifty years, he was still unwilling to give up on returning the multiple character faction to its past glory. In a way, this was his obsession.

However, the leadership position was truly not that big of a deal for Su Yu. As for his teacher's obsession, he could worry about it in the future.

***

Stepping into the hall once again, Su Yu looked around. Instantly, the others lowered their heads to avoid his gaze. He sneered.

He had his answer.

Six families were still against establishing a new holy land. He knew who the culprits were behind Zhang He's complaint. Su Yu couldn't be bothered to say much. He ignored the prefects and stepped to the front of the hall and sat down in the middle seat of honor.

While overlooking the people below, he cupped his fists at Great Han King in greeting, but he ignored Great Yuan King.

The two said nothing.

With a nonchalant tone, Su Yu said, "So? I hear I'm here to explain myself. Come on. Where is the accuser? Step forth." 

Zhang He paled.

After a long while, he walked out. With his head lowered, he said, "Mayor Su, my son was assassinated in Nanyuan—"

Su Yu said, "I killed him."

Silence descended.

Xia Longwu and the others looked at Su Yu...
