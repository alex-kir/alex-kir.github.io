Chapter 1952: Surprise, Kill, Encircle (2) (Teaser)

Sealed Mountain.

The various races had placed a lot of effort in keeping Hundred Battle King sealed.

Back then, only six heavenly kings were in charge of suppressing Hundred Battle King here. But after the rescue attempt by Control King's lineage, the myriad races had added two more experts here. The two additional experts were even stronger than the original six.

That was mainly because the six had to constantly use a part of their strength to keep Hundred Battle King sealed. Back then, a powerful heavenly king could still enter and attempt a rescue without much risk. But now, it would be tantamount to suicide.

This would be an act of courting death even if there were two or three of them. Four or five would do, but the difficulty of defeating eight heavenly kings was still extremely high. On top of the eight heavenly kings, there were also numerous experts in the surrounding areas who could reach the mountain in about two minutes.

Then again, it was quite unlikely that a group capable of defeating eight heavenly kings would bother with sneaking around. They might as well launch a frontal attack. This was what the myriad races believed.

As usual, Sealed Mountain was enveloped in silence.

The eight heavenly kings stationed here spent the majority of their time in silent cultivation. They rarely moved out of their respective cultivation spots. Six of them were spread around the mountain in fixed positions. These were their assigned positions to maintain the seal on Hundred Battle King.
