Chapter 1588: Qin-Zhou Secret Meeting, Another Storm Rises (1) (Teaser)

When Grandstar returned from his short hunting trip, Su Yu bid the old turtle farewell before returning to Grandstar City. That trip hadn't been for nothing. It had helped establish the beginnings of his future path. Inside the city, Wan Tiansheng continued his lesson about the laws.

Only with a clear mind could one advance with clarity. When Su Yu listened to Wan Tiansheng again, he found himself comprehending more things than before. He also came to understand the reason for Wan Tiansheng's mastery over the laws. That was mainly due to the large number of characters Wan Tiansheng had formed. And characters were essentially laws.

***

Su Yu did not wait around doing nothing.

While listening to the lesson, he was also digesting his gains from the previous battle.

He had gathered some heavenly rewards from killing Wisdom King and the other Invincibles. He had been too busy to absorb the rewards at the time. Thus, he had stored all the rewards in his Mark of Civilization.

Equipped with his recently gained knowledge that the rewards were essentially the power of heavenly laws, he was starting to view them differently....
