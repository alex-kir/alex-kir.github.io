Chapter 1945: Gather Up, Let's Kill Some Heavenly Kings (1) (Teaser)

Su Yu's mastery of the pen dao had increased significantly. As he sat cross-legged, dao power poured into his body. His aura surged. This was the benefit of comprehending the dao. So long as one had enough comprehension, one could increase one's mastery and absorb more power to grow stronger. 

Su Yu had seen the ancient beasts of the Chaos Mountain before. Their growth was much slower in comparison. Without order, they could only grind their way forward slowly.

"Am I at the level of a heavenly king now?"

After a long while, Su Yu opened his eyes. His eyes gleamed for a split second. Could he contend against heavenly kings now? It was hard to judge before actually fighting one.

But he could feel his grasp over his dao strengthening. The rate of his fusion hadn't increased by too much, but the nearer he was to the end, the more strength he could gain from the same rate of fusion.

He remained seated as he processed his recent gains. Done with that, he stood up and returned to the entrance to the Lower Realm.

The flow of time in the Lower and Upper Realms should be the same. However, he couldn't be certain how long had passed since he had been staying in the river. He could only hope that not much time had passed.

He had promised to return in a few hours. It would be rude to disappear for days after that, right?
