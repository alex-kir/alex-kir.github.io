Chapter 2239: Breakthrough, Master Of Law (1) (Teaser)

Covering the Human Mountain with his world. This was the main focus of Su Yu for the past few days.

Seven days.

His powerhouses had arrived in stages. Each new arrival would stir the ambient energy in the air, giving Su Yu the chance to move his world undetected. Slowly crushing the morale of the myriad races? Slowly increasing the pressure on them? Everything was only a cover. He only needed enough strength to make them tremble in fear. What was the point of slowly increasing the pressure on them?

Moving his world would create considerable disturbances to the environment. Thus, the appearance of Venerables for seven days straight would be perfect to cover for the movement of his world.

Seven days were more than enough for Su Yu to move his world near the Human Mountain. This was the prime example of doing something under the cover of a diversion. And now, it was time for him to reap the reward for his patience.

The Human Mountain was a part of chaos, but it was also a dao center of Human Emperor. Anyone else would not have been able to encroach upon it, but this was not an issue for Su Yu since the very foundation of his dao was the human lord's token.

Thus, Human Emperor had allowed him entry....
