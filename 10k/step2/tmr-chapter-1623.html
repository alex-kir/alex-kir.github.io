Chapter 1623: Young And Rash (3) (Teaser)

Su Yu’s hammer dropped down with 270-stacked vibrations.

The impact of the strike rippled over the whip.

Even space itself cracked from the impact.

Instantly, a golden stripe appeared on the whip. But Heavenly Forge King merely said, "You are trying to complete this with only brute force. Do you think—"

Su Yu ignored Heavenly Forge King and released his five elemental fire and fire of inheritance before bringing his hammer down again. He said, "Senior, don't talk before showing that you can keep up with me."

Heavenly Forge King snorted and released his fire as well.

Boom!

Unlike Su Yu, he was forging the whip with only his fire. But with Su Yu's inclusion, the vibrations from the whip were shaking his fire, forcing him to gnash his teeth and put more power into his fire. Instantly, more golden stripes appeared on the whip.

Then, he said, "This is too risky! Any carelessness will result in the destruction of the weapon!"

Su Yu said nonchalantly, "I don't lack money. This is the way to forge a stronger weapon. I want a strong weapon, not a cheap weapon. With the same set of materials, I can forge 120 golden stripes. The failure rate will be around 70 percent. As for you, you have a failure rate of 30 percent, but you can only forge 118 golden stripes."

Heavenly Forge King retorted furiously, "Stability naturally matters more in weaponsmithing!"

"Wrong. For weapons, only strength matters. You can always gather more materials when you fail. But it is too conservative to settle for 118 stripes when you can push for 120. This is a waste of materials."
