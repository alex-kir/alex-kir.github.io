Chapter 1235: Back In Time (1) (Teaser)

Great Han King had betrayed humanity.

Everyone was in disbelief. Even Great Zhou King was greatly pained by this betrayal.

He didn't want this to be true.

"Tianyuan, stop him!"

Great Zhou King, who had always been a calm person, was roaring furiously.

"I told you it was him long ago. You refused to believe me."

At that moment, space warped as the River of Time connected to the Human Realm shook before it was severed by someone with a character.

"Back in Time."

With a command, several characters appeared. Great Han King's expression changed drastically just as he was about to reach the Human Realm. Instead of moving forward, he started moving backward, as though time was moving in reverse.

"Zhou Tianyuan!"

Great Han King frowned. Behind him, an expert wearing a golden mask had appeared. The masked individual cupped his hands to the sky and said, "Fellow daoists from the Heavenly Hunt Pavilion, please accept my apology. Zhu Tianfang and I have been borrowing some of your space temporarily. You may have your space back."
