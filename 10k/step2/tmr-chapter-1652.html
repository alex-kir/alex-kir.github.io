Chapter 1652: Accidental Gain (1) (Teaser)

Emperor Yu's Court.

That was the name Su Yu had decided upon.

The name was too attention-grabbing. All the Invincibles believed that it was not a good idea to have this name, but Su Yu did not seem to care.

Was the Human Emperor really dead? Nobody knew. What was the actual status of all those ancient experts? Were they missing? Were they beyond the net? Were they all dead? Were they all in the Death Realm?

Su Yu wasn't too bothered. If the Human Emperor were to return, he would be happy to relax and let the Human Emperor take on the burden. But if the Human Emperor was dead, why would that name matter?

As for the fact that he could get his karma tangled with the Human Emperor's...well, he was already entangled in so much karma that he didn't care anymore.

Culture Tomb, Book of Time, Culture King, and Time Sage. He was already entangled with all those people. The karma of one more person wouldn't matter.

The Invincibles continued working on the formation. At this point, the formation was already near completion.

Suddenly, Su Yu vanished.
