Chapter 1393: The Charm Of A Top Student (3) (Teaser)

After a slight hesitation, the old turtle said, "When a new reign begins, it is quite unlikely for past mistakes to be punished. Also...Su Yu might not be the successor of the Time Sage. You worry too much, Heavenly Overseer Marquis. Don't forget that he is also the mayor of multiple holy cities. You don't have my blessings to kill him." 

"I am aware of that."

The pavilion master sighed, "I don't intend to do that. I'm merely troubled...so be it. It has been years since we last met. Let's not talk about such things. Cheers, Brother Grand Dream."

The old turtle raised his cup. Then, the two drained their tea.

The pavilion master did not stay for long. After finishing the tea, the golden page floated into the sky, split space apart, and vanished.

The old turtle shook his head with a smile. Just then, his gaze landed on the two cups with a tiny bit of unfinished tea. He casually tossed them outside the city. Instantly, countless great daos manifested in the eyes of the little oceanic beast outside. She was overjoyed.

Once again, she bowed and kowtowed to the city. Then, she drained the two cups....
