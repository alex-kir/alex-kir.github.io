Chapter 2001: Bone Picking (3) (Teaser)

Su Yu started his search at the severed end of the river. With Xing Yu's token in one hand and Lan Tian to his side, he started moving forward bit by bit.

Each tributary was essentially a fake dao. He only needed to follow the thread of each tributary and check if it was connected to someone in the Dao Origin Land. If it wasn't, that would mean that the cultivator had left. Otherwise, the cultivator would still be somewhere in the Dao Origin Land.

He could also see the fusion rate of these tributaries. Those with a low rate of fusion belonged to Eternals, signifying that he wouldn't need to pay much attention to them. He only needed to focus on those with a far higher rate or near mastery.

These people would either be Daofuses, heavenly kings, or Venerables. Su Yu did not fear fake dao Venerables. In fact, he could even win against a fake dao Venerable after suppressing the dao of the Venerable. Thus, he had nothing to fear.

After a while, he reached his first fake dao. He tore a rift in space and stepped out with Lan Tian. But after a quick search, he shook his head and said, "Gone. This is probably a Daofuse, but the Daofuse is not here anymore. The dao is being projected to somewhere near the Chaos Mountain."

This fake dao cultivator, in particular, had departed the Dao Origin Land.

Su Yu continued his search.
