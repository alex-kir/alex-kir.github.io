Inside a certain massive ravine, members of the Vanguard Regiment were engaged in an intense battle. Countless demonic beasts were attacking them. Several gigantic dragons hovered in the sky. A large number of experts had gathered there.

It was an endless carnage. Human and beasts were constantly dying, painting the ravine in the color of their blood.

Of the hovering dragons, one was a golden dragon. Overlooking the battlefield below with his large eyes, he coldly demanded, "Release all of the captured dragons or the dragon race won't let this rest."

Right after that demand was made, a spear shot through his stomach, causing his golden blood to rain down from the sky. An expert in black armor snorted.

"Big Worm, why are you uttering so much nonsense? Are you here to chat or fight?" 

On the spear, suns and moons spun. Then, five half-moons appeared. The golden dragon was sent into constant retreat, his desperate roars reverberating in the area.

A battle between Sunmoons erupted in the sky.

Down below, Liu Wenyan was running all over the battlefield under the protection of his uncle. His robe was wrapped around his waist instead of worn on his body. His figure flickered everywhere with the occasional roll on the ground to avoid the random incoming attacks.

His usual elegance was nowhere to be seen. Su Yu had always viewed his Teacher Liu as a refined and elegant scholar. If he saw this scene, that image would be completely shattered. At present, Liu Wenyan looked more like a vulture than a cultural researcher.

Suddenly, a massive tail shot up from the ground and swept toward him. With a roar, Liu Wenyan manifested his axe and cut the tail off. Next, he sent a character into the ground beneath him.
