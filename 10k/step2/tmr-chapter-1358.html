Chapter 1358: Saint Outwardly, Demon Inwardly (1) (Teaser)

Mayor's residence.

Su Yu looked at Zhao Li and smiled, "Teacher, let's start."

Zhao Li nodded. For this project, Su Yu would be the main weaponsmith while he would be the assistant. As this would be Su Yu's dao weapon, it would naturally be better if he could complete it with his own hands.

The other weaponsmiths and Hu Xiansheng couldn't help feeling excited about this.

Only they themselves knew how much Su Yu had sacrificed for this weapon during the past month. If they ended up producing a normal earth weapon, it would be a massive loss for Su Yu.

One could say that Su Yu had invested everything he had accumulated so far into this weapon.

A hammer appeared in Su Yu's hand. It was the Soul Expanding Hammer.

Zhao Li solemnly said, "First, forge the covers. Then, bind them. We will first build the frame before putting everything together."

"I understand."

After a deep inhalation, Su Yu started swinging his hammer. The covers and the binder would serve as the frame and foundation...
