Chapter 1474: You Know, I Know, Qin Zhen Doesn't Know (3) (Teaser)

"There is zero unity among them."

That was Su Yu's thought. Each race was relatively united internally, but unity between races couldn't be achieved. Was killing He Tu really so hard for them?

No.

If the Blood Fire Devil King and Heaven Department head were to go down there, He Tu and Dummy would most likely flee. They wouldn't be a match for the two. But the two would never agree to this.

Meanwhile, He Tu and Dummy would only fight near the entrance. They would flee when resistance became stiff instead of fighting to the death. Thus, the Invincibles were helpless against the two death sovereigns even when they were enjoying the absolute numerical superiority.

"At this rate, it is only a matter of time before the death tunnel is opened by He Tu."

Su Yu had zero confidence in those Invincibles. They were all targeting the nine-leaf skylotus. None of them wanted to use all their strength to fight He Tu. In such a situation, it was impossible for He Tu to fail.

"The nine-leaf skylotus will probably bloom in about ten days. Maybe I should keep a low profile in the meantime."

With that thought in mind, he shut the door and said to Old Hao, "Let's wait here. It's too dangerous out there. We should wait until the residence unlocks and return to the safe house. We will wait there until it's time to leave the mansion. I don't want any treasures anymore. I only want to leave alive." 

Old Hao nodded in agreement. The dangers here were far beyond what they could handle. They were mere Mountainseas. It would be wise for them to hide in the safe house. This was too dangerous.
