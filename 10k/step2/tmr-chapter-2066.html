Deep inside the River of Time.

"Xing Yu's token..."

Somewhere in the future, someone was suppressing the river with Xing Yu's token. What was that person doing? The figure could sense the rapid drain of the token's power.

"Did Xing Yue gift the token away?"

How odd.
