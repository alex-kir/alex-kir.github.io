Chapter 1799: Su Yu's Plan (3) (Teaser)

Meeting Su Yu's gaze, Great Zhou King nodded and said, "Yes. Lan Tian is the perfect person for this job. He has countless clones. Not even I know how many clones he has now. Perhaps he already has his clones in all these realms. Ignoring his...unique personality, he is actually quite useful."

Even Great Zhou King couldn't maintain his trademark calm expression when he spoke of Lan Tian's personality. Noticing that, Su Yu's expression turned odd. Was Great Zhou King one of Lan Tian's victims as well?

Noticing Su Yu's gaze, Great Zhou King coughed and said, "I am naturally not one of his victims. But as far as I'm aware, no fewer than five human Eternals have fallen victim to him."

Su Yu gasped in shock. Five.

At least five!

The desire to gossip burned within him. But upon further thought, he decided that he was too busy for that. He said, "You're right, Great Zhou King. Lan Tian is perfect for this job. He might already have suitable clones hiding in the various realms." 

Never had Lan Tian been so important before.
