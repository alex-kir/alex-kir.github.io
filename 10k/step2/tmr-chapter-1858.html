Chapter 1858: Pacifying The Phoenix Realm (1) (Teaser)

Spluuurt!

Right after leaving the Celestial River of Death, Su Yu coughed a mouthful of blood.

Marquis Lanshan and the others were alarmed to see his heavy injuries. They were glad that he was back, but they couldn't help but worry about his injuries.

Su Yu merely flashed them a wide smile. This trip had not been in vain. However, he also knew that he hadn't gone far enough. The river still hid numerous secrets. For example, where was the beginning of the dao? And where was the end?

But that was not that important anymore. His key takeaway was the discovery of a potential new method to grow himself: theft.

"The Time Sage has been a source of the initial framework, while the Culture King has been the source of my inspiration. This pair of siblings is truly remarkable."

Culture King was a lot more well-known than Time Sage, but for Su Yu, Time Sage was the true genius of the two. Her Book of Time alone had given him countless gains in his path of cultivation.

Her train of thought and her ideas in regard to cultivation, such as creating a new river, a fake river, were truly revolutionary. Su Yu couldn't even begin to imagine how someone could grow into such an amazing individual. And that was the conclusion he had reached from a single book.

He had never even met her. He had only seen her back, at a time when she was weeping...
