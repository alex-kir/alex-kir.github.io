After an indeterminate amount of time, Liu Hong regained his consciousness and hurriedly shouted, "Just what do you want from me?"

Su Yu smiled, "Don't talk. Don't pretend to be unconscious. I control everything in the city. I don't care about your explanation." 

Liu Hong stared at Su Yu gloomily.

Su Yu continued sipping his tea leisurely. He said, "Teacher Liu, I have a question. Where did you learn your character technique?"

"My teacher, Zhang Ruoling—"

Boom!

Once again, he was knocked unconscious.

After some time, Liu Hong reopened his eyes. At this point, his head was already aching badly. Su Yu smiled and asked, "Where did you learn it?"

"I'm telling the truth. It's Teacher Zhang Ruoling—"

Boom!

He was knocked unconscious again. Liu Hong had numerous excuses prepared, but he was not given the chance to say any of them.
