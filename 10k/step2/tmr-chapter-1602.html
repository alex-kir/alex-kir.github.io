Chapter 1602: Returning To Human Realm (3) (Teaser)

"This is Great Han King."

Su Yu bowed respectfully as well.

Great Han King nodded with a smile. He could be considered one of the first human Invincibles to learn of Su Yu's name. He had first learned of Su Yu's name from Zhu Tiandao back when Zhu Tiandao threw a tantrum in the Knowledge Seeking Realm.

"This is Great Shang King."

Su Yu looked at the middle-aged man dressed in a traditional outfit. He bowed slightly and said nothing. He did not have any quarrel with Great Shang, but an elder of Great Shang once went after him before dying at the Starfall Mountain.

After that incident, Shan Xiong had challenged him. At the time, Shang Tianjiao of the Shang Family had attempted to create trouble for him.

"This is Great Tang King."

As usual, Su Yu bowed slightly. He was quite unfamiliar with Great Tang, but he knew Cheng Mo.

"This is Great Song King."

This was yet another face Su Yu knew. He bowed again. This was a respect shown not to the strong, but to the heroes who had saved humanity during the early years of the Anping calendar...
