Chapter 2258: You Need To Trust Me (1) (Teaser)

Even Su Yu had expected Martial Emperor to fight until the very end. But no.

Su Yu looked at Martial Emperor with disappointment and sighed, "The world has lost its purest brute. You have turned into a cowardly cultivator." 

Martial Emperor grimaced at that.

What nonsense was this fucker saying? Were brutes supposed to not fear death or something? Everyone feared death! Even him! It had only been a few days since he regained his freedom. Was it so surprising that he didn't want to die yet?

This fucker was too abnormal. He was unlike a regular sovereign. Any sovereign would treat such a powerful warrior with respect. But Su Yu was different. He was really trying to kill. Martial Emperor could feel it. Su Yu was really trying to send him to the afterlife.

In that case, how could he not submit?

Martial Emperor glared furiously, but he said nothing. He had surrendered. Would this guy still want to kill him? He hadn't even done anything against this guy! He had merely talked about doing something, but he hadn't even killed a subordinate of this guy. Wasn't it a tad too excessive for this guy to resort to killing for something so minor?

After letting out a sigh, Su Yu smiled and asked, "So you want to surrender?"

Martial Emperor glared with his usual ferocious and proud expression.

"I am surrendering!"

He was even shouting as he said...
