Chapter 1697: A Dog's Dao (4) (Teaser)

The tribulation this time was far stronger than the one before. It transformed into a red humanoid. With a spear in hand, it stepped forth and declared, "For defying the assembly, a punishment is deserved. Die."

When the cloud dropped down on Su Yu, he pushed it toward the tribulation with his Mark of Civilization.

Boom!

Then, Su Yu released thirty characters into the sky.

The fusion of his two remaining acupoints accelerated.

It was time for him to become a Sunmoon.

And finally, he completed his advancement.

He strode forth, approaching the red humanoid one step at a time. When he was near enough, he opened his mouth and bit down at the humanoid. The red humanoid was very powerful, but it had been weakened by the clash with Su Yu's heavenly reward.

Su Yu was consuming everything. The reward and tribulation continued clashing against each other, releasing power of laws suitable for consumption.

Su Yu wasn't worried that the tribulation would be too strong. As the reward and tribulation clashed, Su Yu started consuming the power of laws leaked by the two to strengthen himself. He had grown far stronger after becoming a Sunmoon.
