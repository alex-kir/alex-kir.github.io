Chapter 1270: End Of Battle (3) (Teaser)

Before long, Liu Wenyan was gone.

Not long after, numerous individuals appeared in the sky. There were people of various races among them, including some humans. This seemed to be the finale of the recent conflict.

A human expert demanded furiously, "Su Yu, where is Wan Tiansheng!"

News of Wan Tiansheng's slaughter had finally spread to the Allheaven Battlefield.

A myriad race Invincible demanded coldly, "Hand Wan Tiansheng over and the matter will end here. Wan Tiansheng is the enemy of the myriad races. There is no redemption for his bloodlust." 

They did not say anything about Su Yu himself. As an ancient city mayor, it wouldn't be easy for them to do anything to him.

Su Yu was quite surprised. These people had been fighting each other not long ago. But now...they seemed to have reconciled with each other.

He was rendered speechless.

Sure enough, the truth might be trickier than it appeared to be. The myriad races were indeed hostile toward the humans. But with all the fighting done, peace would be maintained as long as it wasn't a blood feud.
