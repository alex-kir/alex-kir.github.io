Chapter 1885: Entering The Upper Realm (1) (Teaser)

After the meeting with West Royal Consort, Su Yu started going through the treasures he had gathered. A lot of them couldn't be taken to the Upper Realm, including the weapons from Immortal Battle Marquis.

It was very likely that he would be detected the moment he stepped into the Upper Realm with that weapon. He also couldn't take any assembly tokens with him. All the treasures related to the Upper Realm, regardless of origin, would be left behind.

As for the fact that this would leave him without any treasures, it didn't matter. It was not an issue. There were so many rich experts in the Upper Realm. Their treasures were as good as his.

Anything he could see would be his anyway. Why should he care?

***

This did not apply only to himself.
