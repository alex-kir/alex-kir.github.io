Chapter 1802: Switching Guardians (3) (Teaser)

"Why should I?" Chen Hao said, "You have too many experts around you. I will only be a burden. My old man gave me a beating previously, warning me to keep my distance from you and prevent anyone from knowing the relationship between us. Things aren't too bad now."

Su Yu asked, "Are you not disappointed that I'm not giving you any benefits after growing so strong?" 

"No." Chen Hao maintained his silly smile, with a trace of shrewdness flashing in his eyes. He said, "Those who are strong will end up as your fighters. They will all need to participate in the battles between the top experts. I know you. These battles are probably extremely dangerous. The fastest to die will be these people. Thus, not helping us grow strong faster is the same as allowing us to live longer. Don't worry. Our turn will only come after all of you are done fighting. You guys will die first. I will definitely live longer than you."

Su Yu chuckled, "What a nice dream."

After taking a sip of his tea, he gestured at Chen Hao to sit down.

He said, "The experts around me are already known to the myriad races. My teachers, my father, and my friends are known to them as well. You are the exception. Contact between us has been minimal since we joined the academies. The rest of my inner circle are already known everywhere, but not you. I have known you're smart since we were kids....
