"Who are you?" Marquis Qiyun roared in alarm, "Just who are you?"

Who was this?

A book?

Whose?

"Time Sage...is that it? No...impossible! Book of Time? How can the Book of Time appear here?" 

Marquis Qiyun started trembling in fear.

When he saw a book, he could only think of the Book of Time. No other book should have the ability to instantly trap a marquis. He might be a death spirit, but a marquis was still a marquis.

The book spirit chuckled and said, "No. The Book of Time is much stronger than me. How dare you disrespect the imperial court and attack the Spirit Suppression General? You deserve death for this. I am here to kill the rebels on behalf of my master." 

Boom!

The net started tightening itself.

Then, the tea plant attacked through the gaps of the net in excitement.

Boom!

Countless branches swept forth. Alarmed, Marquis Qiyun roared and attacked the net. Alas, it was pointless. Meanwhile, the book continued flipping. The more pages he flipped, the more characters appeared, and the tighter the net became.

The tea plant continued attacking in excitement. This was her first time beating someone up. She wasn't completely clueless...
