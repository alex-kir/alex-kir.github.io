Inside Human Emperor's dao.

Su Yu had arrived after tearing a rift in space. Just as he was about to head toward the waterfall to hide away, his expression changed.

Su Yu frowned slightly. He did not panic. This opponent might be powerful, but this was still not a Master of Law. Now that they were inside the river, he couldn't be killed so easily, even if he was weaker than this super expert.

"The current strongest being in existence? I don't even fear Martial Emperor. Why should I fear you?"

Su Yu snorted. He was no longer the same kid who would look at mere Eternals with admiration. After experiencing so many things and growing significantly, he was no longer fearful of such opponents.

"Blocking my path forward?"

Su Yu snorted again. Sure. Let's play. He turned around and moved upstream instead. As he moved, he calmed the way ahead with his little stone. Behind him, the powerful aura started approaching.
