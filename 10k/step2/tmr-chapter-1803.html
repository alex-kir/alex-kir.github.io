Chapter 1803: Grand Dream Turtle (1) (Teaser)

One after another, the guardians were swapped.

All the old guardians were filled with excitement.

Grand Dream City.

Slowly, the number of guardians standing before Su Yu increased. He had opted to free them now to grant them more freedom. That way, they could fight elsewhere without worrying about the Spirit Suppression Domain.

"For now, don't remove your petrification."

Su Yu reminded them. That was a good technique to seal their auras and resist death qi. With the petrification technique, they could enter the Death Realm without triggering much reaction from the Death Realm.

He didn't know the technique. Otherwise, he would have used it on all the living beings going down there with him. This was quite a useful technique. The guardians listened in silence. Inwardly, they were still incomparably excited.

After taking a glance at them, Su Yu sank into thought. Of the thirty-five guardians, he was familiar with Heavendoom and a few others. Most of the rest were still strangers to him. But he needed them. Of the people under his command, they were the nearest to the Daofuse Realm.

People like Xia Longwu had exhausted all their previous accumulation after reaching the seventh-stage Eternal Realm. Killing a few marquises would not be sufficient for them to achieve Daofuse.

As for these old guardians, their foundation had long grown strong enough to help them achieve Daofuse. They only lacked a push from the power of laws.
