Chapter 2256: Brute (2) (Teaser)

Martial Emperor was ashen-faced as he stared at Su Yu silently. He understood now. Su Yu wanted him dead.

With a wave of his hand, Su Yu transformed his white robes into golden robes. The human lord's token appeared before turning into a crown on his head. A book appeared in his palm. That was the Mark of Civilization, also the core of this world.

Su Yu looked at Martial Emperor and announced, "Emperor Yu's Court, Su Yu. Today, I shall send Senior Martial Emperor to the afterworld. This is not a conflict of ideology, nor is this a suppression of a traitor. I am merely doing this to remove a potential obstacle from my path. This is merely an action of a human lord. There is no right or wrong. Heritage and legacy do not matter here. This is only a strategic preventive measure."

Killing Martial Emperor.

That was Su Yu's decision.

Martial Emperor was too unstable a variable, and the biggest variable remaining in the Myriad Realms.

Su Yu had thought of killing Martial Emperor long ago, but subsequently, he discarded the idea since he saw no need for it....
