Chapter 1349: Corpse Collector (1) (Teaser)

Inside the mayor's residence.

Su Yu stared at the little furball blankly.

The little furball was also staring at Su Yu, but he was trembling. Yummy must be sick. Why was Yummy behaving so weirdly?

He really wanted to go back to sleep, lick some characters, or do something else. But looking at the blankly staring Su Yu, he was worried that this was one of those times when Su Yu was going mad again. Thus, he could only remain still and keep staring into Su Yu's eyes.

"Past...fusion..."

Su Yu muttered to himself. Fusion with the myriad races. Experience their past. This was not a simple alteration of appearance or form. That was something he could already do.

But his new comprehension pointed to an entirely different concept.

This was no mere disguise. Rather, this was fusion in body and mind. For a short moment, he seemed to have fully turned into the little furball.

"Civilization."

Su Yu muttered to himself again. Was this the purpose of the civilization character, a result of the culture and radiance characters?

To truly understand a civilization, one needed to become a part of it.
