Chapter 1755: Leaving The Luminous Domain Mansion (1) (Teaser)

Eighth level.

Su Yu was undergoing a transformation. After unifying all his acupoints and apertures, he had transformed into a true Sunmoon. Both his body and sea of willpower were strengthening.

The world started looking different to his eyes.  When he looked up, the previous chaotic power of laws was presented as one dao after another. He also saw a massive river hanging in the sky.

River of Time.

This time, he could see it clearly. It was as though the real form of the world was finally presented before his eyes. Ignoring the reward and punishment descending on his body, he stared at the sky blankly. He was looking at the real world. It felt as though this was his first time knowing the laws. The daos.

"Laws? Daos? Net? All of them are branches."

Su Yu muttered to himself. The completion of the Heaven Gate had granted him a new eye. It allowed him to see the real world. He saw the River of Time, and he saw the countless streams branching out of it, spreading everywhere.

He also knew why one could only affirm one's dao after gaining the ability to enter the River of Time. Because that signified the ability to get in contact with the various daos. He also understood why the River of Time would differ in size depending on the people manifesting it. That was because they were all manifesting the different branches of the river.
