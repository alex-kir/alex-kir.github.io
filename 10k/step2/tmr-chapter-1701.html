The battle that had lasted an entire day was finally over.

Everyone returned home.

The various races hid inside their realms after sealing their realm entrances. As for the dragon race, it even sealed its realm barrier after losing its Pseudo Emperor. Additionally, the celestial chasm race had been wiped out, the Spirit Realm was shaken, while the golden peng race was trembling in fear.

Such was a war between the myriad races. No matter how powerful a race was, losing their strongest experts could spell their end. As for the weaker races, a single wrong choice could very well turn them into history.

From this one battle, one could see that the humans, divines, devils, and immortals had not dominated for so many years for no reason. The humans had an almost miraculous ability to gather allies.

The immortals had received the assistance of two Daofuses from the Upper Realm the moment they were in danger. The devils could produce a legendary devil who was supposed to be dead many years ago. The divines had been maintaining a low profile during the battle, but that was mainly because they were never in that big of a threat during the battle. Otherwise, they would have shown their trump cards as well.
