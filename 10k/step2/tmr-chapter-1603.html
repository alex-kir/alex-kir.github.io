Chapter 1603: Su Yu's Acting Skill (1) (Teaser)

Su Yu continued walking, flanked by Great Qin King and Great Zhou King.

With a smile, Su Yu slowed down half a step, allowing Great Qin King to take the lead.

When Great Qin King turned to look at him, he said, "Please show me around, Your Majesty. It has been a year since I last returned. I am no longer familiar with this place."

Great Qin King smiled. This time, his smile wasn't as stiff as usual.

"It has only been a year. Not much has changed."

Great Qin King resumed his walk, moving at a rapid speed even when it looked like he was merely taking a stroll. Great Zhou King followed, walking at the same speed in the same casual manner. He was right there, but his presence was almost nonexistent. Even Su Yu, who was walking beside him, felt like he wasn’t there.

As far as Su Yu was concerned, someone like this was the most terrifying. He did not fear someone like Great Qin King. For him, Great Zhou King was far more dangerous. No matter how strong Great Qin King was, he wouldn't do more than stab his enemy to death openly.

Meanwhile, Great Zhou King could converse with someone with an amicable smile just moments before sliding a sword through one's heart. Thus, Su Yu felt rather uncomfortable...
