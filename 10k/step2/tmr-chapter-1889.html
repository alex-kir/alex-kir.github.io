Chapter 1889: Stable Army Marquis (2) (Teaser)

Ignoring their shock, Su Yu said, "Stable Army Marquis is residing in the Brightmoon Flower Valley. That is also a danger zone. We're not familiar with that place. If he manages to escape us..."

Lan Tian grinned and asked, "Is escape even possible for him? According to Zhao Chuan, he is probably around Fallen Star Marquis in strength. I can instantly subdue him with Great Zhou King by my side. Where can he go?"  

In fact, Lan Tian could even defeat Stable Army Marquis alone. But accidents might happen. With Great Zhou King, not even accidents could happen.

Su Yu laughed and said, "Don't underestimate the experts up here. That's even more so for the humans who have survived for so many years. Ignoring everything else, they are very good at preserving their lives."

Lan Tian beamed and said, "You're around as well, right? No matter what, we won't have much issue capturing him." 

Su Yu nodded, "Yes, but only if the information we have is accurate. He has over twenty Eternals under him and about a thousand experts below the Eternal Realm. If there are no other Daofuses in his faction, it won't be too hard to subdue him." 

They had a decent number of Eternals. Over twenty of them. And prior to this, they had also deployed a decent number to the Lower Realm. In short, just Stable Army Marquis...
