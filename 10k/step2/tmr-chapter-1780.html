Chapter 1780: Treacherous Su Yu (2) (Teaser)

Inside the death tunnel.

West Royal Consort was cursing repeatedly in fury. Damn it! She had failed!

Damn it!

Not only had she failed to kill the old turtle, she had even exposed her existence.

"I'll join up with West Heavenly King for now. Let's see how things are progressing on his side..."

She continued flying through the tunnel gloomily.

She did not suspect anything even though there was no death sovereign in the tunnel. This was He Tu's territory. And with He Tu gone, it was normal for this place to be empty. She could already see the light at the end of the tunnel.

But in truth, the white dog, book spirit, tea plant, Heavendoom, Tianyue, Empyrean Cloud, and Grandstar were waiting outside the tunnel. Just moments ago, they had received a message from the old turtle: block a powerful expert.

An incredibly powerful expert.

He warned them to be careful when stopping this expert.

Incredibly strong?

How strong?

The seven waited curiously. The old turtle had also informed them of his near-death experience. Thus, they were quite wary. They had to take this seriously. Even the white dog was fully prepared for combat. He did not take the shoe out, but his flowery ball, ring, and chain had all been taken out. He looked incredibly mighty, surrounded by his equipment.

The book spirit had formed a massive net right at the exit of the tunnel with countless characters. He was on high alert as this was a very powerful enemy. Heavendoom and the others were fully prepared as well.

Seven Daofuses, with the white dog among them. Could they win?

They could already sense the approach of a powerful aura inside the tunnel. Inside, West Royal Consort was still moving rapidly without bothering to conceal her aura.

"Su Yu!" Once again, she looked back with hatred and roared, "I will return! I will kill you all! Curse all of you!"

Then, she flew toward the exit....
