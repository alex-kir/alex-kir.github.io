Chapter 1581: Catalog Page (3) (Teaser)

"A bone? What are you talking about?" Su Yu asked, "How did they look like?"

Tian He answered by drawing something out. When Su Yu looked at the drawing, he noted that it resembled a humerus. Did Tian He somehow enter the arm of Old Zhou?

Su Yu rubbed his chin and completed the story for Tian He, "As you travelled, you found another divine weapon. A smaller divine weapon. It was very long as well. As you went forward, you found five more weapons. They were akin to chains linked together. Am I right?" 

Tian He exclaimed in surprise, "You went there as well? Why didn't I see you?" 

Su Yu shrugged without answering. Sure enough, Tian He had entered Old Zhou's arm. Su Yu was quite surprised to hear that. How did that happen? Death spirits would be teleported to the first underground level. Meanwhile, a pseudo death spirit like Tian He would be teleported inside the arm. Was that the case?

Speaking of, what about the other pseudo death spirits? A decent number of pseudo death spirits had entered the mansion...
