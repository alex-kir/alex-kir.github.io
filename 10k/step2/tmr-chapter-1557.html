Chapter 1557: Great Deceit King (1) (Teaser)

Were there experts in the Heavenly Hunt Pavilion?

Many.

The heads of the spires and chambers were all incredibly strong.

Zhu Tianfang was a middle-degree Eternal and he was only a Yellow Department head back when he was in the pavilion. Zhou Tianyuan, who was stronger, was the Profound Department head back then. And the heads of the Profound and Yellow Departments were known to be the weakest back then.

The various heads were shocked at the order to kill Su Yu. They had been neutral for nine epochs. Finally, the Heavenly Hunt Pavilion was stopping their neutrality.

The ancient cities had also ended their neutrality. They had picked Su Yu. But that did not mean that they had picked the human race. However, the pavilion's choice to kill Su Yu would most certainly make them the enemy of humanity.

After a short silence, the first elder of the enforcers said, "He deserves death. In that case, we'll do so. He has killed many enforcer elders..." 

"Go."

The Heavenly Overseer Marquis did not bother to say much. He merely waved his hand at them. Apart from him, there were six more Invincibles present. Each was a respectable expert.

Among them, the heads of the South Chamber and West Spire were late-degree Eternals. The participation of such experts could change many things. They were probably strong enough to suppress even the combination...
