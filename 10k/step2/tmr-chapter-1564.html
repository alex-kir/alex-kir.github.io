Chapter 1564: Help Is Useless (2) (Teaser)

Wisdom King was truly strong.

He was facing six death sovereigns alone. Four were seventh-degree Eternals and two were sixth-degree Eternals. Even so, he was the one pushing his opponents back.

The two weaker death sovereigns looked rather miserable as even their death qi was starting to leak due to his attacks. He was too strong.

Su Yu had a feeling Wisdom King was probably comparable to Great Qin King in strength.

Back in the mansion, Great Qin King had also faced numerous Invincibles alone. Then again, that fight had happened in the Luminous Domain Mansion, not the Death Realm. And at the time, there were only two late-degree Eternals among Great Qin King's opponents.

"What a powerful guy."

Even if this guy might be weaker than Great Qin King, he would most certainly be slightly stronger than Blood Fire. Truly worthy of being an expert from the first epoch.

"Trying to run?"

Su Yu noted that Wisdom King was slowly heading toward the tunnel connected with the ancient city. That tunnel was quite conspicuous, visible even this far away. Thus, Su Yu decided to forget about the other Invincibles....
