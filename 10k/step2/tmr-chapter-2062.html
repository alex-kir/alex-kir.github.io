Chapter 2062: Migration, Hole Digging (1) (Teaser)

Su Yu was willing to fight his way back.

That was enough to make him a good human lord for many. Yes, that was what everyone felt. It was that simple. They didn't know Hundred Battle King. Who knew what type of person he was?

Morale was high.

Su Yu's excitement did not last long, however. He sent a voice transmission with resignation, "These bastards. They're doing this deliberately, right? I guarantee they would all forget their previous words if Hundred Battle was really willing to treat them well. So be it. Fortunately, I am a reliable, refined scholar." 

They all looked incomparably excited. But when Su Yu gave it more thought, he cursed inwardly. He knew very well what they were thinking. Since they were leaving, the masses would be in turmoil. Those leaving and those staying would both feel uneasy.

They were worried about the future. Those staying were worried about a purge, and those leaving were worried that they wouldn't return anymore. When they forced an answer out of him, most of what they said was actually a performance.

Su Yu glanced at Great Shang King, noting the perfect calm on Great Shang King's face. It was as though this was a completely different person from the agitated Great Shang King earlier.
