Chapter 1485: Death Tunnel Opens (2) (Teaser)

While the death spirits were conversing among themselves, some of the living Invincibles started contemplating withdrawal. There was no need for them to wait here until the tunnel was fully open.

Some were setting their sights on the Source Return Blade. Without the tunnel there, could the Source Return Blade be moved?

Thus, some Invincibles started sneaking off. However, those from the major races were in no rush.

They all waited indifferently, including the Heaven Department head. Yes, a divine weapon was extremely useful. Everyone wanted something like that. But making an attempt on that weapon now was tantamount to suicide.

He Tu had merely displaced it slightly to shift the tunnel away. But that did not signify that the Source Return Blade was up for grabs. Suddenly, a loud hum rang out and a massive blade appeared in the sky.

Above the Devote King's residence, the sky itself was split into two.

Then, a silhouette appeared out of the void.

Someone sneered and said, "Fool."

That was a Pseudo Invincible from the nether...
