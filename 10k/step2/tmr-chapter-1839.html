Fate Pseudo Emperor's eyes turned cold as he said, "Abyss Marquis, get out of here and remove the barrier. Or else..."

Endless Passage and the fate Invincibles frowned. One of them hurriedly said, "Patriarch, are we really letting go of our control over the realm entrance?"

"Yes." Fate Pseudo Emperor coldly said, "It's pointless to keep it since they will keep monitoring us like we're thieves. We might as well let go of it. When humanity attacks, Abyss Marquis can handle it himself." 

Finally, Abyss Marquis appeared again. With a smile, he said, "You misunderstand me, Brother Fateless."

Fate Pseudo Emperor shot him an indifferent gaze and said, "For nine epochs, the fate race has remained neutral. But in this epoch, you are still treating us like enemies. In the previous epoch, Hundred Battle King tried to recruit me multiple times. I refused each time. Even our Heavenly Fate Marquis descended personally to handle Hundred Battle King. Don't push it, Abyss. Do you think the myriad races will let the celestial chasms replace us after you force us to stand with the humans?"
