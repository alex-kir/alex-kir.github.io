Chapter 1939: Weird Human Emperor (1) (Teaser)

"Human Emperor!"

Su Yu couldn't help but wonder about the little stone. Was that something Human Emperor had deliberately left behind? Was it a pure accident that Xing Yue had buried it in Culture King's Resident?

Perhaps.

Thanks to the stone, the pressure he felt from the Dao Origin Land was reduced by at least a third. He was certain the stone could also suppress the different powers of laws in the curtain. Even just suppressing a third of the power of a heavenly king would turn such an expert into a tier-two or even tier-three Daofuse. 

"Truly incredible."

Su Yu sighed again.

A vast majority of the top experts of the myriad races resided in the Dao Origin Land. Were they aware that they were actually residing in Human Emperor's personal territory? Were they not afraid that he would have a successor one day? Such a successor could gain control over this place and wipe them all out.

With those thoughts in mind, Su Yu stepped into the Dao Origin Land. Unsurprisingly, he created no fluctuation. And the moment he stepped inside, he sensed the many masterless powers in the air.

"Truly a treasure land."

Su Yu couldn't help but gasp in admiration. He had seen such masterless powers before. In the Allheaven...
