Chapter 1981: Reaching An Agreement (1) (Teaser)

Buried Soul Mountain.

A group of people was emerging out of thin air. The moment he was out, Lan Tian took a deep breath and pulled a swarm of bugs into his mouth. He even released his forked tongue to lick his lips after he was done.

The people around him hurriedly distanced themselves from him. 

Heavendoom poked Grandstar and asked, "Is that your brother?"

"Little Doomie, don't talk behind my back!"

With a grin, Lan Tian gave Heavendoom's head a pat. Instantly, Heavendoom stiffened. This felt so uncomfortable. He wanted to give Lan Tian a beating, but he recalled that he wasn't a match. He also remembered that his boss wasn't here. Furthermore, he was the weakest in the group.

Thus, he could only look at Su Yu pitifully, hoping that Su Yu would rein Lan Tian in. He felt very uncomfortable with that madman standing beside him.

Meanwhile, Lan Tian was happily taking in the fresh air around them before saying, "Things have been calm for the past few days. The first meeting of the Myriad Race Assembly has ended, but they didn't reach an agreement."
