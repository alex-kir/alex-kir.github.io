Chapter 1797: Su Yu's Plan (1) (Teaser)

Human Realm.

Su Yu arrived in the blink of an eye. Great Zhou King only arrived a short while later. He was actually slower than Su Yu now.

As he looked at Su Yu while panting lightly, Su Yu turned around and said, "Two Daofuses descended?"

"Yes. Abyss Marquis from the celestial chasm race and Celestial Dragon Marquis from the dragon race. Both are ancient marquises."

Su Yu nodded and frowned slightly, "Why are you panting? Great Zhou King, don't act in front of me all the time. I don't like this." 

Or to be precise, he disliked someone better at acting than him. What was this? He was capable of teleporting dozens of Invincibles to the Celestial Chasm Realm. Would moving such a short distance alone be enough to tire him out?

Great Zhou King blanked out slightly before breathing like normal again. He coughed and said, "This is a habit."

Silence descended.

Indeed. He was acting out of habit. His brain was telling him that after moving such a long distance, it would be normal for him to gasp for breath. But he finally recalled that he was now a Daofuse, not an Eternal.

A peak Daofuse.

He had been pretending to be an Eternal for so long that he had forgotten that he was a peak Daofuse. Even Su Yu was left speechless.
