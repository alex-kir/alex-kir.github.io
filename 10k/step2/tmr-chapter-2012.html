Chapter 2012: Favor For Repayment (2) (Teaser)

By the time Su Yu was back among the iron devourers, it was already the end of the fourth day given for them to migrate.

Instead of storing their people inside a weapon, over ten thousand iron devourers were moving together while carrying numerous massive mountains with them. Yes. The iron devourers were migrating with their mountains.

One bamboo mountain after another could be seen moving atop the swarm of iron devourers. The bare Human Mountain was too boring for them. Thus, they had decided to migrate with some of their local specialties. However, they were worried that bamboo couldn't grow on the Human Mountain. Ultimately, they had decided to move their mountains as well.

The younger iron devourers were all still frolicking around on those bamboo mountains without a care, as though they weren't being forced to migrate. Even the older iron devourers appeared indifferent as they were all treating this as a holiday.

Atop the biggest mountain they were carrying, the large bamboo palace was visible. Yes, the iron devourers had carried even their palace with them.
