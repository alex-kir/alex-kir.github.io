Chapter 1244: Zhu Tiandao's Madness (1) (Teaser)

"You're courting death, Su Yu!"

The divine king roared, "First Yellow, you're a human as well?"

First Yellow answered gloomily, "No."

"Spatial tear, traveling through space, good at space manipulation..."

The divine king muttered doubtfully. There weren't a lot of spatial experts around, but there were some races who were proficient in spatial manipulation. For example, the spatial beast race.

This was a very rare ability among humanity. It was unlikely that First Yellow was a human. If he was a human, he would have acted much earlier. Instead, he had not done much during the battle. Was he really doing this for Yellow Nine?

"Put Su Yu down!"

The divine king demanded and released his aura, tearing more space apart. First Yellow's body warped and vanished. He had moved through space yet again. When he reappeared further away, he gnashed his teeth and said, "Divine King, I'm only sending him somewhere near. Please don't make things hard for me."

"Hmph!"

When did Sunmoons gain the qualification to bargain with Eternals? This First Yellow was getting overconfident just because he was proficient at spatial manipulation. He should not underestimate Eternals.

The divine king started tearing through space with sheer brute force, moving tens of kilometers with each step he took.
