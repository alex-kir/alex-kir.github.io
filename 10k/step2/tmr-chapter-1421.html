On the other side of the entrance to the third level, Su Yu was panting heavily with lingering fear in his heart. How did that guy enter the first level? This wasn't right.

Su Yu was sure he had swept through the first level. He had not encountered any powerful death spirit. He Tu would have gone after him long ago if He Tu had been there since the beginning. Sure, He Tu might not know his appearance. But at the very least, He Tu knew his voice. In any case, a death spirit would not need an excuse to kill a living being.

"It's over for the second level."

Su Yu might have killed some people there, but he had never planned to search the place in detail to hunt everyone down. That was a waste of time. He would only kill those he happened to encounter. That was different from He Tu, who was intentionally searching the place to kill everyone.

"He had probably reached the first level after my departure."

With that thought in mind, he vanished with a flicker.
