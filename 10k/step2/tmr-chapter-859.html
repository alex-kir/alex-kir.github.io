Chapter 859: Things Remained, People Changed (1) (Teaser)

Inside the Vanguard Regiment base was a large stone house. The house looked rather beaten. This was Su Yu's current residence. Compared to the Great Ming Consulate, this place was probably worse than a toilet.

After sending Su Yu over, Hong Du stationed a Skysoar to act as the guard outside the building. The guard would also act as a messenger Su Yu could contact whenever he needed something. Then, he left to get busy with his own tasks.

As for Su Yu, he sat cross-legged on the cold stone bed and cultivated silently while scanning his surroundings.

His Perception Jade was the best tool to examine his surroundings. With a range of one kilometer, it allowed him to see a lot of things.

"The Vanguard Regiment is not weak at all."

Su Yu lamented to himself. The base was filled with experts. In fact, he found two Sunmoons and six Mountainseas just in the area one kilometer around him. And this was only a tiny portion of what the Vanguard Regiment had to offer.

The Vanguard Regiment was also the vanguard of humanity. This was an army specializing in nothing but killing. But the aura of death...
