Chapter 1139: What Big Brother Stone Statue Can Do (1) (Teaser)

Su Yu wasn't aware of the conversation between the stone statue and Wan Tiansheng. At the moment, he was busy feeling smug.

He looked at Wan Tiansheng with a smug expression. Just look at this! He was indeed tight with his big brother! It was no big deal. He was just that important. Look at how quickly the stone statue had agreed to help.

Wan Tiansheng had nothing else to say. Since the stone statue had agreed, he had no reason to remain. He grabbed Su Yu, entered the River of Time, and started returning to reality.

Behind him, the stone statue's indistinct voice rang out again, "Not having a future does not equate to not having hope. Young Friend, as long as the present remains, the future might not be all that important."

"Thank you for the guidance, Senior."

Wan Tiansheng gave the stone statue a slight bow to express his gratitude. Su Yu was completely unaware of this exchange.

With a smile, Wan Tiansheng vanished into the distance with Su Yu.

"Sigh."

Only a sigh was left ringing about in the River of Time.
