Chapter 1893: Keep A Low Profile, Kill Less (3) (Teaser)

"Why aren't you giving chase?" Bloodbreak Marquis asked, "Are you waiting for me to come and give chase personally? Where is your brain?"

He was furious.

This piece of trash might also be an ancient marquis like him, but Bloodbreak Marquis did not think much of this person. Unfortunately, his real body was too far away from this place or he would have arrived in person.

Meanwhile, Marquis Bajue had been waiting here all along. Yet he had failed. What useless trash!

Bloodbreak Marquis was enraged. He believed that Marquis Bajue was not giving chase due to fear. But how could an ancient marquis die too easily? These days, any battle exceeding a certain scale would easily attract numerous other experts. But instead of giving chase, this bastard was staying here.

At this point, anger was starting to show on Bajue's face. There were outsiders here as well. Yet Bloodbreak was scolding him in front of them.

Despite his anger, he did not lash out. Instead, he said, "I will give chase immediately. But they might be far stronger than me."

"Just shut your mouth and get moving."

Bloodbreak Marquis was showing him zero consideration. Left with no choice, Bajue could only gnash his teeth before vanishing. What the fuck was he supposed to be in pursuit of? They were long gone by the time he arrived. He didn't even know which direction he should go.

So be it. He decided to fly in a random direction and put up a show. Inwardly, he kept wondering about these mysterious opponents. Who? Who were they?...
