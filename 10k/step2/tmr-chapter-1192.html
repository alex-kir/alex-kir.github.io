Chapter 1192: Start Of Slaughter, Plentiful Sunmoons (3) (Teaser)

Ye Hongyan did not give chase. She grabbed Liu Wenyan and flew in Uncle Liu's direction while shouting, "Leave! Hurry!

Blood dripped down her lips even as she spoke. Clearly, her injuries were heavy. The devil who was fighting Uncle Liu previously had fled as well. Uncle Liu, who was also soaked in blood, joined the two and spoke while coughing blood, "General Ye, leave without me. I'll watch the rear. The Liu Family...thank you for this favor!"

Right after saying that, he recalled yet again that the Liu Family was no more. Uncle Liu had the expression of someone ready to meet his death. His nephew must reach Nanyuan. There, his nephew would advance into the Sunmoon Realm, rise to the top yet again, and avenge their family.

Seeing the bad shape Uncle Liu was in, Liu Wenyan sighed inwardly. It would seem like he could no longer wait. He had to complete his advancement right here if they were to survive this journey.

In truth, he could advance anywhere. It wasn't like he was really relying on the Culture Tomb to help with his advancement. He had held that thing in his hand many times in the past. It had never done anything for him.

He did not know too much about what the Xia Family was planning. But the presence of the Culture Tomb indicated that Su Yu was involved as well. After all, the Culture Tomb was supposed to be in Su Yu's possession at the moment.

He did not know...
