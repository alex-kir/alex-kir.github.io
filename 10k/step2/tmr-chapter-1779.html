But so what? The Sunmoon still wouldn't be able to handle his punch.

However, Great Qin King and Great Xia King were already top Daofuses after fusing with the heavenly desolate beast's dao. In a battle between Daofuses, when neither side could gain an advantage through the clash of daos, they could only rely on their physical strength. And the two were very good at that.
