Su Yu headed toward the nearest peak that was over ten thousand meters in height with his Heaven Gate open. He could see that there was an expert residing on that peak. All the experts here were somewhat connected with the chaos power, with their projections revealed in the sky. Others couldn't see it, but Su Yu could.

The beast residing here was a beast with two long fangs. Su Yu did not know the name of this beast. The two sharp fangs were its most distinct characteristic. They looked like the tusks of elephants, but they were straighter.
