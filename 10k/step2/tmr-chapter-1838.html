Chapter 1838: Attacking The Fate Realm, Tiangu Smiles (2) (Teaser)

Fate Pseudo Emperor ignored them all.

Before long, a sleepy eye appeared on his forehead. Slowly, it opened. That eye was rather similar to the Heaven Gate. But at the same time, it was also different.

Originally, Fate Pseudo Emperor's hair was white yet glossy. But as the eye opened, his hair started withering. His skin was previously as smooth as a child's skin. And now, it started withering and sagging as well.

He was doing all he could to open that eye. With his third eye, the world in his vision had undergone a complete change.

He saw many things. First, he looked toward the Immortal Realm. A silhouette appeared above the Immortal Realm. That was Tiangu. He had sensed something from afar. Thus, he had emerged before looking in Fate Realm's direction.

Fate Pseudo Emperor ignored Tiangu and continued gazing upon the Immortal Realm.

He saw dark clouds converging above the Immortal Realm, and a bloody qi swirled around the entire realm. Numerous figures could be seen falling from the sky. It seemed as though Tiangu was among them, but at the same time, it didn't seem so.
