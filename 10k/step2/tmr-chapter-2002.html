Six Venerables had arrived at the Dao Origin Land.

They flew to the end of the Dao Origin Land right away before splitting into three groups for their sweep.

Each of them was radiating an incomparably powerful aura. As they moved, Venerable Scrape's cold voice echoed in the air, "All living beings in the Dao Origin Land are to gather at the Myriad Race Mountain to the west."

"From today onward, the Dao Origin Land is sealed. Entry is forbidden for everyone."

His voice echoed repeatedly, spreading far and wide, creating numerous dao fluctuations in the air. The six were not here to kill. They were willing to cooperate with anyone except humanity. 

Nevertheless, they wouldn't hesitate to kill if anyone dared to challenge their order.
