Chapter 1752: Grand Cycle Unity (1) (Teaser)

Su Yu walked toward the second room.

It was the inkstone-shaped room. As expected, the first room contained some methods to handle Martial Emperor. What would he find in the second room?

These were the four trump cards left behind by Culture King, the trump cards only his inheritors could obtain. It should be something that would be useful now, not in the future, right?

"The inkstone is a dao from a different race. Thus, there is a possibility that it might fall under the control of a non-human. It is unlikely that the trump card left there will be too important."

Su Yu started speculating.

Before long, he was standing before the second room. It had a door and a unique padlock as well. Su Yu was quite happy to see this. Just the padlock alone was enough to make this trip worthwhile for him.

He took the inkstone out and sank into thought. He was still quite worried. He had not comprehended the dao in the inkstone. Would he fail to open this door? But it turned out that his worry was baseless. With a crack, the padlock snapped open. The inkstone was a legacy Culture King had left in his old residence.

Perhaps Culture King knew that with the white dog around, it was unlikely that the inkstone would fall into the hands of an enemy. That would only happen if the white dog was killed, but an enemy powerful enough to kill the white dog could probably open this door even without the inkstone.

Thus, the opening of this door was quite straightforward....
