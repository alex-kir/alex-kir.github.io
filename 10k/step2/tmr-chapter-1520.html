Chapter 1520: Nine Transformations (1) (Teaser)

Bloodmount General's Residence.

With a punch from Su Yu, Blood Fire Devil King's sea of willpower collapsed. During his final moments, Blood Fire Devil King had fused his sea of willpower and flesh before throwing a punch at Su Yu.

He was dead after that. A ninth-degree Eternal had thus died.

Su Yu didn't know if this was an acceptable death for Blood Fire Devil King. After all, Old Zhou was the main culprit for Blood Fire's death. Blood Fire Devil King had left nothing behind for Su Yu except a storage ring that he had left beside the cauldron before their battle.

Su Yu watched silently as the final traces of Blood Fire Devil King vanished before him. After some hesitation, he took the Mark of Civilization out. This time, he wasn't trying to strengthen his Mark of Civilization. In any case, Blood Fire Devil King had not left anything he could consume behind.

Instead, he only used the Mark of Civilization to gather some blood, remnant willpower, and remnant aura in the air. He had decided to collect whatever remains of Blood Fire Devil King he could find and make a record in his book.

Blood Fire Devil King, second strongest devil, leader of blood fire devil race, dead in Su Yu's hands.

Su Yu started writing in his Mark of Civilization. Blood Fire Devil King could be considered a ruthless hero of sorts. Unfortunately, he was lacking in strength and luck. He had courage in spades. After all, he was one of the few ninth-degree Eternals with the courage to enter the mansion. He was willing to risk everything to achieve Daofuse.
