Chapter 2023: Trade, Investigate (1) (Teaser)

Great Zhou King was showing an ample amount of good faith to the myriad races.

After considering the offer, the Venerables agreed that this was a good deal for them. They would gain significant benefits without paying that high a price. They only needed to release some human experts to force the Torchbearers to start fighting the chaos lineage openly. This was a good thing for them.

With the general direction of the agreement determined, it did not take them long to iron out the finer details of the agreement.  It was agreed that Southbrook Marquis and the others would be released shortly.

Giant Axe Marquis would remain with the myriad races before the Torchbearers completed the task of killing two pseudo kings and two peak Daofuses. The Torchbearers could also kill a Venerable instead to secure his release. Toward the end, they started talking about the details of the release.

At that moment, Su Yu interjected, "Just send them out side the Line Gorge and leave. We will have our people collect them."

Line Gorge was the former hiding place of Brave Martial General. The Venerables started wondering if the Torchbearers were basing themselves there. However, that seemed quite unlikely.

It was more likely that they were merely making use of Line Gorge's terrain...
