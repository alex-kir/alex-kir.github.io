Su Yu felt like his sea of willpower was getting very busy as of late. The female furball was quite surprised to see the tea plant as she could sense that the tea plant was extremely powerful.

Su Yu had actually returned with a helper!

"Do you know my husband?"

"What is a husband?"

"Soy Bun."

"I don't know. I've never met him. But Fat Ball knows him."

"Fat Ball?"

"A white dog. Do you know him?"

"I think...so. My husband mentioned him a long time ago. He saw the white dog before, but he was still very young back then so he doesn't remember much..."

Yes. Soy Bun had met Fat Ball before, but that was a long time ago.

In truth, Soy Bun had also met Culture King's younger sister before. But it had also been so long that his memories were blurry. Or to be precise, his sentience wasn't fully developed yet back then. Thus, he could no longer remember much of the events from back then. Otherwise, he wouldn't have claimed that the Culture King and Time Sage were unrelated.

In terms of seniority, Fat Ball was older than Soy Bun, while Soy Bun was older than the book spirit and tea plant. The two were similar in age with the female furball as all three had developed their sentience at the late years of the ancient past.

Su Yu was naturally uninterested in the contents of their conversation. He was busy rushing toward the Allheaven Prefecture. As he flew, sounds of an ancient chant echoed within his head.
