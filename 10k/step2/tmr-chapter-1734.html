Chapter 1734: Schemes Are Useless, Battling East Heavenly King (1) (Teaser)

Su Yu continued fleeing toward the higher levels with Heavendoom and the others.

Thanks to the power of laws she just absorbed, Marquis Lanshan had mostly recovered from her injuries. However, her damage from losing her dao body remained as that wasn't something one could recover from so easily.

Even after recovering from her injuries, she was only as strong as a ninth-degree Eternal. That was basically the same level as Heavendoom and the others.

Marquis Lanshan coughed, looked at Su Yu with a complicated expression, and asked, "Human Lord, are we heading to the seventh level?"

"Yeah."

"There's a tunnel there...death tunnel...leading to the Death Realm. We all know that...it's much harder to killl East Heavenly King there. He can flee..."

There was a death tunnel on the seventh level. Almost everyone of a certain status in the Death Realm knew that. After all, that tunnel had been around since Devote King's time.

Su Yu did not say much. He knew that East Heavenly King wouldn't be easy to kill. But he wouldn't mind killing only the marquises under the heavenly king. He said, "He has three more marquises under him. If we can't kill him, we'll settle for killing his marquises. He can flee, but I'll go after him again." 

At that point, the roles would be reversed....
