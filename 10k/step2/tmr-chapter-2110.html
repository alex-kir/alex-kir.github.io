Chapter 2110: Everything I Did, I Did For You, Lord (1) (Teaser)

As the various dao powers poured into Su Yu's world, the River of Time shook.

Dao conflict.

When enough power entered his world, Su Yu had an instinctive feeling that this had turned into a dao conflict.

How troublesome.

A conflict of daos.

He had a sudden premonition that he was in trouble. However, the problem shouldn't be too big. This was happening because he was stealing from the River of Time.

"Dao conflict...Celestial River of Death..."

Suddenly, Su Yu wondered if the disappearance of the Master of Death was related to the Master of Time. After all, the Master of Death had stolen death from the Master of Time.

"A conflict of time? What is this so-called dao conflict? A conflict between Creators? A conflict between laws? Or a conflict between eras?"

Looking at his expanding world, a certain premonition hit him. Perhaps...a day would come when he would encounter the Master of Time. And perhaps trouble would follow that meeting. After all, they were all stealing from the Master of Time. 

They had created a proper world. The Myriad Realms were all well before all these thieves came along. Human Emperor and Master of Death had both stolen from Master of Time in search of a shortcut for their Creation....
