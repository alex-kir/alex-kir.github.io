Chapter 1713: Past Events (1) (Teaser)

Allheaven Battlefield.

Su Yu was back out here.

The Allheaven Battlefield was quite calm. The armies of humanity might have set off for war, but the armies of the myriad races had all withdrawn. Thus, they were without opponents. Even the unaffiliated cultivators who could normally be seen everywhere were gone.

That was understandable. A bunch of people had died the day before. Many people were still hiding away in fear. Of course, that did not apply to everyone. Some were still brave enough to wander the Allheaven Battlefield in search of opportunities. After all, the deaths of so many Invincibles would provide the brave with quite a lot of opportunities as well.

With so many deaths, there must be some blood essence left somewhere, right? There must be some carrier treasures left somewhere, right? Heaven weapons, or even earth weapons, and the remains of these dead Invincibles would also offer massive opportunities for these people.

These treasures could no longer catch Su Yu's attention, but they were still invaluable for Skysoars and Cloudbreaches.

Su Yu did not spare the random weaklings he saw a glance. He was moving rapidly. While moving, he was inspecting the memories in his Mark of Civilization.

Heavenly Seal King's memories, to be precise.

He had collected many corpses this time, including the corpses of Daofuses....
