Chapter 1491: Spear Of Qin (2) (Teaser)

Boom!

Qin Zhen was sent flying. His entire body shook, looking like his three bodies were on the verge of separating. When an Invincible was forced out of their fused state, their strength would drop significantly.

And upon losing one or two bodies, an Invincible could very well drop to the level of a Pseudo Invincible in combat strength. The power of time swirled around Qin Zhen, while three overlapping bodies started appearing on him. These were signs that his bodies were going to be forcefully split.

Even in such a condition, and even with all the blood on his body, he grinned and said, "Not bad, Little Bastard. After living for thousands or perhaps tens of thousands of years, you have grown to a level strong enough to barely suppress me. Tell me. Is your woman one of the immortal fairies I killed?"

His opponent was none other than Immortal King Yueshi. The immortal king did not bother to reply. Instead, he stole a glance at Zhu Tianfang, who was giving a different immortal king a beating. Like Qin Zhen, the power of time was swirling around that immortal king, indicating that the immortal king was also on the verge of being forcefully split into three bodies.
