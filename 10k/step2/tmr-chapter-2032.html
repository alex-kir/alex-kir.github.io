Chapter 2032: Raining Death (1) (Teaser)

Su Yu's group, which had emerged out of nowhere, was displaying a surprising level of brashness. They had attacked to kill right after emerging. The attacks of twenty-three experts had instantly blasted a Daofuse apart, snapping the dao of that Daofuse and turning the body of that Daofuse into fine powder.

"You're courting death!"

Yue Hao was furious. These people were too brazen! They were attacking for real! But before he could do anything, a shoe dropped down from the sky.

A deafening rumble rang out.

Yue Hao was stomped deep into the ground.

"Kill them!"

Deep underground, Yue Hao roared in fury as countless dark chains shot out and tied the shoe down. The white dog manifested his dao, showing the projection of a white dog that was madly biting down on the chains.

"Awooo!"

With a howl, the projected white dog snapped a chain with one bite. Deep underground, Yue Hao's heart pounded heavily. What manner of a dao was this? It had so easily broken one of the chains he had manifested through his dao.
