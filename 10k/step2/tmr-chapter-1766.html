Chapter 1766: Follow Me, Live A Good Life (3) (Teaser)

After removing the aura imprint left in the blade, Su Yu tossed it at Xia Longwu and said, "Refine it. The Weapon Fusion Method isn't hard to learn, especially for an Eternal like you. You have already fused all your acupoints into one. Just pour all your comprehension of the Sky Sundering Saber into the Source Return Blade. I use this method to transform my techniques into characters, but since you can't do that, you can use the Source Return Blade as the intermediary instead."   

Xia Longwu only understood part of what Su Yu was saying. But as a whole, he was still feeling quite clueless.

Nevertheless, he was a genius. It didn't take him long to gain his own understanding of this method. He unhesitatingly channeled his saber qi and comprehension of the Sky Sundering Saber into the Source Return Blade.

A divine weapon was very powerful. The blade was trying to reject the attempt. After all, it used to be Devote King's weapon. It did not want to fuse with a different dao. Noticing what was happening, Su Yu released his aura and suppressed the blade.

With a slight tremble, the blade submitted. It was essentially a masterless item. Thus, it wasn't able to resist much. Before long, it started shining resplendently. Xia Longwu had completed the refinement of the weapon.

Then, Su Yu took Xia Longwu back to their previous spot. At this point, even Su Yu was starting to feel some exhaustion. The River of Time was not something to be easily traversed.

When they arrived, the saber character floated above the gap again. As of this moment, Su Yu could start fusing with this dao if he so wished. But he did not do so. There was no need for that.

What if there was a conflict between this dao and the pen dao? It would be troublesome...
