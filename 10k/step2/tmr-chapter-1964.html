When Su Yu opened his Heaven Gate again, he started bleeding from his eyes. The air was filled with broken daos.

Su Yu did not tell the others about this. More than two Masters of Laws had died here. He was certain about that. That was clear from the strength of the broken daos in the air.

Suddenly, Su Yu asked, "Were there more than one Masters of Laws in the devil race?"

Great Zhou King answered, "Yes. There were forty-one Masters of Laws among humanity. It was natural for the devils to have more than one as well. In fact, even the iron devourers had more than one. Both January and February were Masters of Laws."  

Su Yu nodded.

"So there were more than ninety-nine Masters of Laws among the myriad races?"

Great Zhou King nodded, "Yes. Some of the myriad race marquises were Masters of Laws as well. However, none were appointed kings. That was reserved for humans. However, humanity had its allies as well. Thus, there were also more than forty-one Masters of Laws on humanity's side."
