Chapter 1856: Destiny Child Emerges (2) (Teaser)

Su Yu had gained numerous new comprehensions from his recent cultivation. He no longer utilized the blood essence of others for his book. Rather, he was starting to use his own blood essence for the projections formed by his book.

Even a single drop of blood essence would drain quite a lot from him. And losing too much blood essence would even damage one's foundation. Of course, one could always recover as long as one stayed alive. Back then, Hong Tan had been selling the blood essence of the beasts for decades, and those beasts had survived just fine.

As Su Yu worked, the dark page shone brighter and brighter. More and more golden stripes appeared on it. From the early heaven grade, it advanced into the middle heaven grade. As for the projection, it grew from the peak Sunmoon Realm, slowly reaching the level of an Invincible.

Su Yu had yet to reach the level where he could create an Invincible with a single drop of blood essence.

"Absorb!"

Finally, Su Yu tossed the page out, allowing it to devour the incoming death qi. Slowly, the weak, miniature dao on the page grew.
