Chapter 1107: I'm Billing You (2) (Teaser)

About fifteen minutes later.

In front of Su Yu's house.

The entire apartment block had been emptied.

Too many experts had been trying to enter this place. Those with strong backgrounds would do it forcefully while those without would do it sneakily. Because of that, several fights nearly broke out.

One time, two groups of sneaking experts encountered each other by accident and started fighting because both parties thought they were under attack. The entire building was nearly demolished by their fight. Fortunately, they held back since this was still Great Xia's territory. Ultimately, several civilians were injured but nobody was killed.

Even so, this place was no longer a good place to stay. Even Mountainseas and Sunmoons were starting to visit this place. Even a small fight between these experts could easily kill all the residents in the building.

Thus, the building was eventually emptied. The people in the neighborhood had also moved away with Great Xia's assistance. Thus, the empty neighborhood looked rather desolate.

In the past, one would be able to see some old people and children enjoying their time in the neighborhood...
