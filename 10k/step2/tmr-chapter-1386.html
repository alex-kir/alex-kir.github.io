Chapter 1386: Special Entrance (2) (Teaser)

Boom!

An explosion erupted beside Su Yu. The humanoid fish had been blasted apart with a single palm strike.

With a roar, Langtu transformed into a massive wolf with one normal head and one illusory head. "You will need to pay a price for attacking us!"

Su Yu did not hesitate to transform into a bird and joined his temporary companions in attacking the nearby giant. The giant merely stared at them coldly. After giving them a sneer, he ignored them and left.

It didn't matter to him to kill a random nobody in his way, but he also saw no reason to waste more time on them. He could see that the wolf leading the group was a somewhat capable combatant. He could even see that there were traces of three-headed demon wolf bloodline in that wolf. He simply did not have the time to bicker with such a person during a moment like this.

Langtu was relieved to see the giant leaving. However, he remained tense. In the one-kilometer radius around the entrances, numerous corpses were dropping from the sky. One kilometer. This distance was nothing for experts. Yet this had been an incredibly difficult distance to cross for many.

One had to constantly be wary of sudden attacks as people were everywhere. The seawater below them...
