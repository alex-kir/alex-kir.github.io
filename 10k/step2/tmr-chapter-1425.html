Chapter 1425: Heavenly Hunt Index (2) (Teaser)

The Allheaven Treasure House had been a long-time opponent of the Heavenly Hunt Pavilion.

It was a relatively young organization. Thus, the pavilion master had been wondering about the identity of its master. He was quite surprised to see that it was this chubby man. He said, "Bountiful Treasure General, it has been a while."

The chubby man smiled and said, "Indeed. Greetings, Marquis."

After a slight pause, he laughed it off and said, "No, forget that. I should be calling you the pavilion master instead. I heard you, Pavilion Master. I'll be frank. I did pick up a piece of treasure many years ago. Thanks to that treasure, I was able to found the Allheaven Treasure House and start the Dao Affirmation Ranking. Now that you're asking for it...you might as well ask for my life, Pavilion Master. My Allheaven Treasure House won't survive without it."

The scholar sank into silence. After a long while, he said, "Which piece do you have?"

"I only have one..." The chubby man grinned and said, "It has a...character on it."

The scholar instantly sat up straight and asked, "Which character?"

"Record."

The scholar's expression changed slightly as he asked, "Is the...visual character not with you?"

"Nope. I'm telling the truth!"...
