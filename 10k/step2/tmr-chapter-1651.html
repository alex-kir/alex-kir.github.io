Chapter 1651: Emperor Yu's Court (3) (Teaser)

The various parties were in discussion about the new developments.

Su Yu might be a nominal leader without much actual power over humanity, but this was still a matter to be taken seriously.

At the same time.

Celestial chasm race.

The Celestial Chasm Realm was a realm constantly shrouded in darkness. The Celestial Chasm Pseudo Emperor had just sent the South Chamber head out of his dark palace. The Heavenly Overseer Marquis was trying to buy the visual character from him. The marquis must be dreaming.

He stared at a fragment in his hand. Above the fragment, a visual character could be seen. When he focused on the fragment, he could see the images of numerous geniuses and experts from the various races. After a while, a certain figure emerged from the fragment. But soon, it faded.

The Celestial Chasm Pseudo Emperor frowned and ignored the figure. Before long, he summoned another figure, a figure resembling Xia Longwu. After a slight hesitation, he produced a drop of black blood at the tip of his finger and pointed at the figure.

Splurt!

Instantly, a saber emerged.

The drop of blood was reduced to nothingness.

"Is his willpower so powerful?" The Celestial Chasm Pseudo Emperor sighed regretfully...
