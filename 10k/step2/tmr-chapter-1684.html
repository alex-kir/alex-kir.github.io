Chapter 1684: Man, Dog, Plant, Book, Ball (3) (Teaser)

Su Yu said, "Senior, nobody can reach this place except me. It will be fine for you to be away for a bit, right? Senior Book Spirit and Senior Tea Plant have never been out before. They are too unfamiliar with the outside world. You should be more familiar with the outside world, right?" 

The white dog nodded, "Yeah, I went out before. Master used to take me out a lot. But..."  

He looked at the flowers around him and said, "But I need to water the flowers every three days or they won't be pretty anymore!"

Su Yu was alarmed. He asked, "Senior, are you saying that you have spent the past hundred thousand years without going into deep slumber?"

He had thought that the white dog had been in deep slumber until his arrival. Even the old turtle had spent a lot of time in deep slumber. The same applied to Heavendoom and the others as well. For the most part, they would all enter deep slumber each time the Allheaven Battlefield was shut from the various realms.

That would reduce their energy expenditure and lessen the impact of time on them. But since the white dog needed to water the flowers every three days...he must have lived normally for the past...
