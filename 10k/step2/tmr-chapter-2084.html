Chapter 2084: Reactions (2) (Teaser)

Su Yu started raging. This was infuriating. He had never seen this coming.

But soon, he grinned. Just as another bolt of lightning descended upon him, he punched it apart before extracting the five elements out of the scattered lightning bolt to nourish his five elemental daos.

This was troubling.

Or to be precise, this looked troubling to the ones observing the process. Wan Tiansheng did not want to distract Su Yu, but he couldn't resist saying, "Don't waste your time challenging the chaos dao. Just focus on your dao pioneering. It won't be able to kill you so easily."

What was he doing?

He was fighting chaos like an angry child.

Su Yu laughed and said, "I'm merely doing the unexpected. It's striking me with lightning, so I steal from his power to feed my daos. In any case, this will also help deepen my comprehension."

The five elements he extracted from chaos were the wildest form of the five elements. They were a lot more intense than his five elements. However, he was only extracting a small amount of power each time, allowing his five elements to easily suppress and devour the power he was extracting.

With this, the five elemental daos grew even longer. Meanwhile, Su Yu started punching the lightning bolts...
