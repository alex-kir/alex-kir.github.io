Chapter 1352: Public Invitation (1) (Teaser)

Main hall.

Zhao Li wasted no time and asked right after taking a seat, "Do you have a blueprint of what you want?"

Su Yu shook his head, "I'm not yet certain. I only know that I want something like a book."

"You're not worthy."

"..."

Su Yu blanked out. Why was his teacher being rude?

Zhao Li said, "Your dao is not a book. Remember this. You are trying to use a book as the cover for the villainous acts you're going to do in the future. In my opinion, you should forge a saber, sword, axe, fork, or similar weapons."  

"No. That does not fit my image."

Su Yu rejected the offer. With a flicker, white robes appeared on his body. Clasping his hands behind him, he looked up at the starry sky and said, "Teacher, is something as crude as a blade fitting for me? I need a book in hand. I am erudite and knowledgeable. In terms of cultivation methods alone, I know thousands of them." 

Zhao Li stared at Su Yu. The kid was still as pretentious as ever.

Since Su Yu was insisting, he couldn't be bothered to argue further. He said, "The difficulty of forging a book-type weapon will be much higher. Swords, spears, sabers, and halberds are the easiest to forge, unlike the more unique weapons."

Su Yu nodded. That was expected.
