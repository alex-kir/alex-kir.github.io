Chapter 1666: Collapse (3) (Teaser)

The Celestial Chasm Pseudo Emperor smiled.

"Do you want to die together?"

He looked at Great Qin King. Then, he looked at Great Zhou King and asked, "Do you? The death spirits are here. The moment they spread uncontrollably, all of us will be viewed as the culprits by the heavenly laws. We will all be punished. With death spirits flooding this realm, heavenly tribulations raining from the sky, and myriad race experts surrounding the realm outside, can you still leave? Do all of you want to die with us?"

With a crazed expression, the Celestial Chasm Pseudo Emperor asked again, "Do you want to die together? It's not too late to stop the death spirits from crossing. We can still block the path. But the moment a death sovereign arrives, even killing the death sovereign will only produce more death sovereigns. Are you really ready to die with us?"   

Dying together.

The Celestial Chasm Pseudo Emperor was incredibly decisive. Seeing that even the old fossil from the first epoch had died, he realized that victory would be very unlikely. Thus, he had to do something. He knew that he couldn't rely on the myriad races to save them. They had to save themselves.

Everything would be fine during the initial phase of the death spirit invasion, but when enough time passed, heavenly tribulations would arrive. These humans would also be punished alongside the celestial chasms.
