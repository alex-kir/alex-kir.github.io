Chapter 1892: Keep A Low Profile, Kill Less (2) (Teaser)

Suddenly, Su Yu looked into the distance and frowned as he said, "This isn't good. The fluctuations are getting too conspicuous. Trouble might be coming."

He glanced at the stunned Stable Army Marquis and said, "Lan Tian, take him and leave. Stable Army Marquis, just stop for now." 

With a wave of his hand, several figures appeared. When Stable Army Marquis saw those figures, he blanked out. What?

"Mo Shenji?"

He knew one of them. A devil of the previous epoch. While he was in shock, Su Yu released a palm strike. With a rumble, the devil was turned into mincemeat, followed by the three Eternals around him. Then, Su Yu consumed all four corpses with the devil page.

Boom!

Only then did some crimson clouds appear in the sky. This was no heavenly tribulation. Rather, it was the phenomena that had appeared due to the deaths of experts.

Su Yu had killed them instantly.

One Daofuse and three Eternals had been killed before being devoured. Nothing was left behind. With them devoured cleanly, there was no need to worry about anything they might have on them that could expose Su Yu's group.

After all, he had just arrived in the Upper Realm. He had to keep a low profile. These were the experts they had encountered...
