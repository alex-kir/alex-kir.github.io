Chapter 1883: Intention To Ascend (2) (Teaser)

Su Yu stood up and added, "One more thing. Nobody is allowed to enter the boundless void without permission. Even when you see the chance to deal with Heavenly Overseer Marquis, do not act."

When Su Yu said that, he focused on Wan Tiansheng. Wan Tiansheng could only nod.

He knew that Su Yu was worried that he would go after Heavenly Overseer Marquis in Su Yu's absence. In fact, that was precisely what Wan Tiansheng was planning to do. But Su Yu's worry was reasonable. That was the bearer of the previous court's fortune. That wasn't someone they could kill easily.

"That is all. The meeting is adjourned."

Just then, Su Yu's gaze landed on Great Xia King and Great Qin King. With a slight frown, he said, "It might feel awkward for the two of you to walk the dao of the heavenly desolate beast. But you have cultivated the physical body dao for many years. This is merely a different form of the same dao. Forget about your spear and saber for now. Prioritize reaching the level of a heavenly king first. A true expert will be good at more than one dao." 

The two bowed slightly. Su Yu was right. Their cultivation had slowed recently. That wasn't supposed to be the case as they were already strong prior to that, and they had the dao all for themselves.
