Chapter 1770: West Royal Consort (1) (Teaser)

"Humanity has risen?"

Great Xia King and Great Qin King looked excited, but Su Yu muttered to himself with doubt. Had humanity truly risen in power? Including Tian Yue, there were only four Daofuses among humanity. Could this be considered a rise in power?

During the ancient past, there were forty human kings and a Human Emperor. All of them were Masters of Laws. Even with such power, their empire had collapsed.

Risen in power?

Su Yu disagreed. Hundred Battle King was someone who was near a Master of Law in strength. Even so, he was defeated. The Myriad Realms were not so simple.

Su Yu speculated that there were more unimaginably powerful experts hiding beyond the net in the sky. There were also those ancient assembly members who had all vanished alongside Human Emperor. Were they all dead?

And there was also the matter involving Culture King and Time Sage. Who were they fighting?

In short, things were incredibly complicated. Human Emperor was far stronger than anyone could imagine. He alone had taken dozens of human kings and an even larger number of assembly members with him before disappearing.

Such a terrifying existence had vanished for a hundred thousand years. There were only two possibilities. Either he was dead or trapped in an endless battle like Culture King. Nevertheless, Su Yu could understand...
