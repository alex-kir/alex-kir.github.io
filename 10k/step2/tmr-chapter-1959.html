Venerable Scrape was left without an argument. This was his very first time facing such an insult since the end of the ancient past. Venerable. That was indeed an imposing title. It was not a title the likes of him could have claimed during the ancient past.

But the ancient past was no more. All Masters of Laws had disappeared. People like him stood at the very peak of existence. In that case, why couldn't they call themselves that?

Venerable Scrape did not bother to argue about that. He knew that the person before him was too prideful to care about any argument he had. It would be a complete waste of time. Thus, he started stepping forth again, one step at a time.

Behind him, the indistinct aura of a different expert had appeared as well. Evidently, they were aware that Su Yu had reached a dead end. There was no escape for him.

Su Yu ignored them and looked at the hole below him with a frown. This wouldn't be an easy path to take. The hole here was unlike the hole on the death spirit dao. This was a far larger hole. It felt large enough to sever the River of Time itself.
