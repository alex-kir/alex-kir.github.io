Chapter 2262: Kill A Few Chaos Beasts For Fun (2) (Teaser)

Sounds started coming out of the gate.

After an indeterminate amount of time, an ancient beast resembling a buffalo emerged with some struggle. First, the buffalo stretched their head out to look around. The sight of the devastation stunned the buffalo.

Why did it feel like this place had just been faced with the great annihilation? Was this the damage from a previous battle? Was everyone dead? How convenient. They knew that Yu Qing was dead. The matron dragon was dead as well. A major battle had erupted in the Myriad Realms. But did everyone die from that battle?

They looked around, but there was no living being in sight. Only some corpses could be seen.

Before long, the buffalo emerged from the gate entirely. Voices were still coming from behind the gate, seemingly asking a question. The buffalo replied through their willpower, "The Myriad Realms...seemed to have been destroyed. Or to be precise, the Upper Realm has been destroyed. I can only see corpses and destruction everywhere."

A voice rang out, "Look around more."

"Sure."

The buffalo started exploring, but they were cautious and remained near the gate. They had yet to notice the tea plant. As an expert counterfeiter, how could Su Yu make the mistake of making his counterfeit too easily available?
