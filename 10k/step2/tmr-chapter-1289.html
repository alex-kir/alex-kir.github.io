Chapter 1289: Ancient History (1) (Teaser)

Empyrean Cloud was back.

It had taken her quite a while, but Su Yu wasn't bothered. Since he didn’t know where the empty ancient city was, he wouldn’t suspect anything even if she were to return a day or two after. In fact, he was quite surprised to see her back in less than two hours.

"Lord, you're back already?"

"..."

Empyrean Cloud thought about it and asked herself the same question. Why was she back so soon? Just look at Su Yu! He was still perfectly fine! Fortunately, this would only be a temporary stop. She decided to take a longer detour on her second journey.

"Yes." She nonchalantly replied, "I worried you couldn't last too long. Let's go. We'll be meeting my boss."

Su Yu grew excited. It was time for him to meet the boss! The two vanished with a flicker.
