Chapter 1471: I Have Been Wronged (3) (Teaser)

Numerous explosions erupted in the air.

Just then, Su Yu focused on the saber in the courtyard with flickering eyes. He had noticed that the saber had actually trembled slightly. As it trembled, a tiny amount of death qi leaked out from the center of the pavilion.

He was not the only person who had noticed that. All the Pseudo Invincibles standing guard near the gate had noticed the same. An immortal Pseudo Invincible roared, "Watch out! Notify your respective Invincibles! He Tu is scheming something! The divine weapon here seems to be reacting!"

Before long, a Pseudo Invincible roared, "Lords, He Tu is shaking the suppressive divine weapon to open the death tunnel. Please watch out."

At that, He Tu's voice rang out, "Damn you! Why are your eyes so sharp? This is my home! How dare you take over my territory? How dare you stop me from going home? Just you wait! You will regret this!"

Then, the explosions stopped as silence returned.

Moments later, several Invincibles arrived. Instead of entering the residence, they gazed down at the pond from above. One of them frowned and said, "Pay close attention to this place. He Tu is trying to shake the divine weapon and displace the tunnel. Notify us the moment you see anything. Nobody above the ninth-stage Sunmoon Realm are allowed to kill each other in the residence. Disobey this at your own risk."
