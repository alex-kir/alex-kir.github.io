Chapter 1442: Worrisome (1) (Teaser)

There were so many people here.

That was Su Yu's thought as he moved amid the group of immortals.

So many immortals were still alive. These people sure were good at surviving. There were nearly 1,500 of them here. Including those on the seventh level, there were probably around 1,800 survivors left in the mansion.

After several days of slaughter, Su Yu had assumed that almost everyone was dead. But in truth, about half of them had survived. This was already the fifth day. How were there still so many survivors?

"Sure enough, the myriad races are akin to weeds. They will never go away. Even after losing so many people, there are still so many of them."

Su Yu noted that there were a decent number of middle-stage Sunmoons present. If it wasn't for the sake of entering the seventh level, he could have killed all these people with his yang acupoint open.

When he looked at the humans, he saw many familiar faces as well. Sure enough, Old Liu was around. Su Yu wondered if Old Liu had performed the bloodline verification with Yellow Nine.

If Yellow Nine wasn't a Liu...perhaps he should...
