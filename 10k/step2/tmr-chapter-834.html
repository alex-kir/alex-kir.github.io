Su Yu denied it unhesitatingly. Damn it. Every single Sunmoon was an incomparably cunning individual. Even at this moment, Zhang He was still trying to test him.

Left with no choice, Zhang He said, "Fine. I'll try my best, but I can't promise that I can shake him off."

"Just do your best."
