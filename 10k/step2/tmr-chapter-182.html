Chapter 182: Monthly Examination, Mental Tempering Stage (1) (Teaser)

Character Faculty.

Intermediate class.

Su Yu was late. This was the first time he had ever been late since becoming the class monitor. The monthly examination was going to be held today. He was actually going to be late on such an important day. When he arrived, everyone was stunned into silence.

"Class Monitor, what happened to you?"

Seeing Su Yu's pale face and his dim eyes, some of the students thought of something.

One of them roared, "This is Lin Yao's fault!"

"This is too much!"

"Class Monitor must have cultivated too much in order to not embarrass the intermediate class and ended up injuring his own willpower."

"Damn that Lin Yao!"

"Class Monitor, are you fine?"

One person after another walked up to Su Yu and looked at him with expressions of grief and indignance. Why must he push himself so hard?

Even Lin Yue had a sorrowful expression as she said, "Su Yu, even if you lose, it's nothing. Even without the mountainbreak bull blood essence, you still have the chance to get more in the future. Why torture yourself like this?"

"Yeah!" Liu Wu, who had a decent relationship with Su Yu, said, "Class Monitor, it's not good to push yourself like this."
