Chapter 1782: Visit To the Five Elemental Realm (1) (Teaser)

Allheaven Battlefield.

Sea of Stars.

Su Yu waited indifferently. Not long after, Great Zhou King returned. Shortly after, the book spirit and tea plant returned as well. The two flanked Su Yu, with the tea plant staring at Great Zhou King curiously in her girl form. This was quite a strong guy.

But they also needed to be wary of him.

Just moments ago, she had received a voice transmission from the book spirit to watch out for any sneak attack this guy might launch toward the human lord. Thus, the tea plant was studying Great Zhou King curiously, wondering if he was a bad guy.

Great Zhou King ignored the two and said to Su Yu, "Emperor Yu, I have failed."

Su Yu said, "So be it. But why did you lose the record character?" 

Great Zhou King sighed and said, "He set a trap in the fragment. I never noticed it before. That was a good chance for us to return it to him. Upon seeing that, the myriad races would start suspecting him. After all, you have never lost anything in your many battles prior to this. Even Heavenly Overseer Marquis himself would be full of suspicion with the record character back in his hand." 

Su Yu laughed and said, "True. But remember to inform me before doing something like this in the future." 

"Understood."

Great Zhou King did not say more. This was already a very good response from someone as domineering...
