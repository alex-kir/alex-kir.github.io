Chapter 1862: Four Realms, Pacified (2) (Teaser)

The Phoenix Pseudo Emperor was relieved. This was already the best they could ask for. She had never expected that Su Yu would not only spare them, but also not seal them. Instead, he would only imprison them in some other realm.

This was a good thing! This was much better than being imprisoned in the Human Realm. This signified that there was still a chance for them to reverse their fate. The normal phoenixes were overjoyed to hear that as well. This was a stark difference from the despair they were feeling a moment ago.

Nobody would be killed. This was too surprising. They had thought that this would be the extermination of their race.

Meanwhile, South Heavenly King and the others stirred. Not killing even one? And not sealing them away? Only imprisoning them in some other realm? This was too dangerous. Su Yu had always been a firm and decisive person. What happened to him? Why was he so different today? If it weren't for their trust in Su Yu, these death spirits wouldn't have stayed silent about this for so long.

Su Yu added, "By the way, you need to hand over all your assembly tokens. How many assembly tokens do you have?"

"Only two." The Phoenix Pseudo Emperor had turned extremely cooperative. She hurriedly said, "In my heaven weapon. You can search it."

"Okay."...
