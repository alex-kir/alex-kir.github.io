Chapter 2075: Everything Is Ready (2) (Teaser)

Hou Pseudo Emperor looked at Su Yu with his ferocious face. He wasn't even angry. That was simply how he usually looked like. He asked, "Emperor Yu, are you saying that we have been cultivating the wrong dao? Am I incompatible with my father's dao?" 

How was that possible?

Su Yu nodded and explained, "Your father was never a savage. You can only blame Martial King for leading your father astray."

Was this for real?

Hou Pseudo Emperor was unwilling to believe that. He would rather believe that his father was a ferocious beast. Back then, his father had devoured countless experts while following Martial King, fully showcasing his brutish nature.

And now, Su Yu was saying that his father was originally gentle? How confusing.

But Su Yu was someone with a Heaven Gate. And Hou Pseudo Emperor had indeed been stuck for too many years. He had only reached the third tier due to his sheer age.

Had he truly been wrong?

He looked at Skyeater and the others. If that was the case, the hou race would probably keep failing to produce actual peak experts. But dao switching was very risky as well. There was no promise that the new dao would be compatible with them.

But after giving it some thought, he agreed, "Since Emperor Yu believes that I'm incompatible with my father's dao, I'm willing to switch. But forget about September's dao." 

He did not want to cultivate...
