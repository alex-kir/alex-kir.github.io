Chapter 1334: Consuming Blood Essence (1) (Teaser)

It had been decided that a competition would be held for the slots.

The various races were gathering their experts to get as many slots as possible from the competition. The humans were doing the same. Su Yu even saw Huang Teng, Qin Fang, Wu Qi, and the others in the palace of the human race.

The competition was a sudden decision. Fortunately, most geniuses picked to enter the Luminous Domain Mansion were already here on the island. Even those who were not on the island were already somewhere in the Allheaven Battlefield. With a night to prepare, the Invincibles had enough time to bring the rest of them to the island.

Inside the ancient city.

Su Yu landed from the sky.

Mayor's residence.

Liu Hong had been listening to the discussion without showing his face. When Su Yu landed, he smiled and said, "Today, you demonstrated your strength to the masses and showcased the strength of our city. Well done, Mayor! However, do we have twenty combatants to participate in the competition? I can't think of a suitable combatant apart from you!" 

Su Yu couldn't be bothered to answer. Instead, he sent a voice transmission to someone.

A short while later, Liu Hong vanished with a flicker as several demonic beasts entered the room...
