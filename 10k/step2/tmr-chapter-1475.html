Inside the safe house.

Everyone was looking at the sky, looking at the projection of the blooming nine-leaf skylotus.

Beside Su Yu, Old Hao gasped and said, "How beautiful."

Beautiful?

It might not be physically beautiful, but its ability to help one achieve Daofuse would most definitely give one an illusion of beauty.

One divine after another walked out of their rooms. A Sunmoon said, "Can our race win this treasure?"
