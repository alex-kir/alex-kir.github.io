Chapter 1881: Scholars Are Growers (3) (Teaser)

World of the living.

The crimson path in the sky had faded away.

Everything returned to normal. Countless people were still waiting for a conclusion to whatever it was that was happening.

Where were they? Su Yu seemed to have disappeared. Great Zhou King and the others were waiting with a heavy mood. Where were they? The crimson path was gone, but the three were also nowhere to be seen. Were they dead?

That shouldn't be the case, right? No phenomenon had appeared so far, signifying that the three were still alive. But where were they?

How was Lan Tian?

Just then, a gigantic crimson cloud formed above the Sea of Stars. It wasn't a death phenomenon. Rather, it was a tribulation from the heavenly laws.

Next, an individual appeared in the sky. With a giggle, that individual said, "Would you look at this? A tribulation! Back off, heavenly laws. Why are you punishing me when I haven't done anything wrong?" 

After saying that, the individual faded away. But next, the entire sky became filled with Lan Tians.

Countless Lan Tians, blanketing the very heavens.

Some vanished in the various minor realms, some dove into the Sea of Stars, some vanished in the various ancient cities, and some vanished elsewhere. Nobody could keep up with them.

And then, a seductive Lan Tian lured the crimson cloud toward the Eastrift Valley.

Great Zhou King was filled with shock. That Lan Tian was moving way too fast.
