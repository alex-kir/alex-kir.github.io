Chapter 1343: Life Death Fruit (1) (Teaser)

Numerous gazes landed on Su Yu, the owner of 136 slots.

Could he even find so many people from the ancient cities to enter the mansion? Unlikely. Rather than wasting the extra slots, he would be better off selling them.

The major races, such as the divines, devils, and immortals, had enough slots after winning about six hundred slots each. However, the minor races were still in need of slots. For the major races with enough slots, buying more slots at high prices wouldn't be worth it. But the minor races were more than willing to pay to enter the mansion.

Of the 3,240 available slots, the divines, devils, and immortals had received almost 1,800 slots. The humans had received 420 slots while the dragons had received 300 slots. Su Yu had received 136 slots and the Heavenly Hunt Pavilion had received 40 slots.

That left about 500 slots for all the other races. The ancient races alone had snatched a majority of the remaining slots. Thus, very few slots were left for the minor races. If even a major organization like the Heavenly Hunt Pavilion had only received forty slots, one could imagine just how difficult it was for the others to get one of these slots.
