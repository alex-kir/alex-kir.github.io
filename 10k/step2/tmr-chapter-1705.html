Chapter 1705: Indulgence (2) (Teaser)

As Su Yu was sighing inwardly with regret, Great Zhou King was looking at Su Yu with a genial smile. And what could this guy say now? He had agreed to every request of this guy! Could this guy still leave?

Su Yu continued, "Great Xia King and Great Ming King shall lead the various armies to the Eastrift Valley. Tell the myriad races to start a war between those below the Invincible Realm. This shall serve as the way for us to train our soldiers."

Great Xia King said, "The myriad races won't agree..."

Su Yu said, "Why not? The Allheaven Battlefield is filled with resources. If they refuse to cooperate, we can simply invade their realms and plunder their resources. Each of the major races has various minor realms colonized. If they refuse to cooperate, we will plunder their colonies. 

"If their Invincibles dare to participate, kill them all. Do they have the courage to challenge our Daofuses again? We won't be starting a war between Invincibles. Through this war, I hope to see countless new Sunmoons."

He looked at the gathered soldiers and asked, "Soldiers, are you scared? Do you have the courage to face the myriad races?" 

"We are not scared! We fight!" 

The numerous soldiers roared in excitement....
