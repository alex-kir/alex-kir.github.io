Chapter 1747: Eighth Level (2) (Teaser)

In the middle of the eighth level was the assembly hall. Ninety-nine doors surrounded the assembly hall. Even from the reception hall, one could see the towering doors. Ninety-nine doors for ninety-nine assembly members.

Heavenbridge Marquis said, "The outermost region is comprised of the residences of the untitled generals. The untitled generals with residences here were all incredibly strong. They were the nearest to the titled generals.

"The inner region contained the residences of the titled generals. Deeper inside, you can find the residences of the marquises. And if you keep going deeper, you will reach the residences of the human kings. Only then can you reach the Myriad Race Assembly."

Su Yu asked, "Were the assembly members not the same as the Pseudo Emperors?"

Heavenbridge Marquis said doubtfully, "The same? No. Assembly members were assembly members, while Pseudo Emperors were Pseudo Emperors. Each assembly member must be a Pseudo Emperor as well, but not all Pseudo Emperors were assembly members. There were more than ninety-nine Pseudo Emperors among the myriad races, but there were only ninety-nine assembly members. These were the strongest among them."   

Su Yu thought about it and nodded. He now had a rough idea as to how things worked here.

Some of the smaller races might only have Daofuses or Eternals...
