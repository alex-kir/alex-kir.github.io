Chapter 1748: Eighth Level (3) (Teaser)

Su Yu stared at the two, speechless. Were they too free? Was that why they were engaged in such a pointless argument? Since when was Heavendoom so talkative?

Su Yu couldn't be bothered to entertain them. He was focused on the eighth level as a whole. With his Heaven Gate, the world before his eyes was no longer the same. In fact, the world he viewed was starting to grow similar to how the white dog viewed the world.

He was able to notice some unique aspects of the eighth level. Numerous indistinct daos covered the entire level like a net.

"Laws."

Su Yu muttered to himself. The entire eighth level was covered in these laws. No wonder Heavendoom complained about the strict and rigid rules on the eighth level.

He also saw an indistinct silhouette above the assembly hall, seemingly there to suppress all existence, including the assembly itself. That was not an actual person. Rather, that was a silhouette formed by countless daos, working in tandem to keep the assembly suppressed. Those were most likely the laws of humanity....
