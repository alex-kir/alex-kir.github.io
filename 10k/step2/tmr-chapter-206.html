Chapter 206: Divine Character Rejection (2) (Teaser)

Su Yu spent three days reading without stopping. He only read through each text once. He did not want to spend too much time on a single text and accidentally destroying it. He didn't know too many languages. He only found 12 texts written in languages he knew.

Even so, reading 12 texts in 3 days was still an impressive feat. After all, one would exhaust willpower reading willpower texts. After every read, Su Yu would need to spend around an hour or two to recover his willpower. He had not been able to fully comprehend everything he had read.

Some texts were rather confusing so he wasn't able to get any Divine Characters out of them. In total, he had formed six new Divine Characters. If he was able to keep all six of them, Bai Feng and Chen Yong would probably be shocked. Six characters in three days. That was incredible.

Alas, Su Yu kept none of them. All the characters were destroyed and fed to his three existing characters.

He did manage to discover the advantages of doing this. The biggest advantage was the fact that he no longer needed to use too much of his own willpower to nurture his characters. They could nourish themselves with these destroyed characters.
