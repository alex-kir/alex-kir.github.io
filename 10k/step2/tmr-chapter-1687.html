Chapter 1687: Dual Mountainsea (3) (Teaser)

Heavendoom gnashed his teeth and flew off to deal with the other Invincibles. The female furball was enough to handle the Dragon Pseudo Emperor. There was no guarantee that the female furball could win, but it also wouldn't be easy for the Dragon Pseudo Emperor to defeat her. Both were similar in strength, and both were tired from their previous battles.

For a moment, it seemed as though both sides were equally matched. The Daofuses of the dragons, spirits, devils, and nethers were here. Meanwhile, the old turtle, iron devourer, and female furball were the Daofuses fighting on humanity's side.

Even outside the Celestial Chasm Realm, the two sides were also equally matched with four Daofuses per side. Including the dead Celestial Chasm Pseudo Emperor, sixteen Daofuses had been dragged into this battle. And seven of them belonged to humanity's side. It was fair to say that they were quite equally matched.

Sure, humanity had a lower number of Invincibles, but the ancient guardians were powerful enough to close the gap between the two. This would force some of the Daofuses who had been staying idle because they were confident in the myriad races' victory to join the battle as well.

Su Yu was certain. Because the same thing had happened...
