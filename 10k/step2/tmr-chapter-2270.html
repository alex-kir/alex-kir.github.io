Chapter 2270: Surprise? (1) (Teaser)

New Domain Era. A new era.

An era separate from Human Emperor's era.

If the new emperor had only the former members of Human Emperor's court serving under him, he would only be a successor. He would be someone who had brought the court to an even greater height, but not the pioneer of a new era.

In truth, Su Yu had never been too bothered about this. But upon learning that Human Emperor was dying, he understood that some preparations must be made. Since Xia Huyou and the others were willing to go completely crazy, he would naturally be willing to go crazy with them.

***

While everyone else was busy cultivating.

Su Yu was in the River of Time. With a wave of his hand, he said generously, "Pick any dao you want."

It felt as though he was an emperor letting his loyal subordinates pick their respective fiefs.

And for some reason, everyone was taking this seriously, as though these daos were already his.

Xia Huyou grinned and said, "I want humanity's physical body dao."

That was a powerful dao. He didn't dare to say that he would grow into a being like Human Ancestor with this dao, but it wouldn't be an issue for him to grow into a tier-one Master of Law. Human Emperor was at that level even before his creation,...
