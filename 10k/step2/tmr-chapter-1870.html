Chapter 1870: Four Daos Emerge (1) (Teaser)

Eastrift Valley.

The armies of humanity were no longer fully stationed in the Eastrift Valley. Instead, they were all busy warring on all fronts. A small temporary palace was built on the former site of the Vanguard Regiment. It was Su Yu's temporary palace.

At the moment, Bountiful Treasure was waiting nervously there. It had been months! He had been waiting here for months! He had never seen humanity ignoring a surrendering Daofuse for months like this.

Perhaps the last time humanity did this was during the ancient past. But even in the ancient past, a surrendering Daofuse would be welcomed by either the Human Emperor or a Master of Law.

Su Yu was truly arrogant! He was here bearing valuable gifts!

Just as Bountiful Treasure waited anxiously, a figure appeared before him. It wasn't Su Yu. Rather, that was a staff-wielding expert. At the sight of the newcomer, Bountiful Treasure's expression changed.

Heavendoom.

This madman again!

Heavendoom chortled and said, "Bountiful Treasure, come on. Let's have some fun. A spar?"

Bountiful Treasure cursed inwardly.  It was clear these people never intended to let him go. He would either be killed or accepted as one of them. There was no third...
