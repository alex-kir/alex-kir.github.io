Chapter 551: The Easygoing Great Ming (2) (Teaser)

Su Yu continued his casual stroll through the academy. He went near Zhao Li's research center a few times and hesitated about entering before ultimately turning away. He would put aside the visit. Perhaps he could visit right before his departure.

As Teacher Zhao had a bad temper, Su Yu was actually quite scared of telling him about the departure. Teacher Zhao had placed a lot of hope in him and had even permitted him to claim that he was a part of the weaponsmithing faction if needed.

"Forging an earth-grade cultural weapon..."

Su Yu muttered to himself. He would return. And he would help his Teacher Zhao complete that weapon. He was not strong enough to do so now. He had been too busy recently to the point where he didn't even have the chance to strengthen his grasp of the art of weaponsmithing. If he insisted on helping now, he would only make things worse.
