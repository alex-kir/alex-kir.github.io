Seventh level.

Su Yu looked up at the face in the sky.

Martial Emperor's cold voice rang out, "Truly impressive. You actually managed to complete the Heaven Gate."

He was no longer called the little bug.

Su Yu smiled and said, "I'm not that special. Thank you for the cultivation method, Martial Emperor. With the Heaven Gate open, I finally understand that I was merely a frog at the bottom of the well. Allow me to ask this question. With the Heaven Gate, can you see the River of Time? Has that river existed since the beginning of existence, or was it something created by someone?"

Martial Emperor replied indifferently, "It has been around since creation."

Sure enough!

Su Yu continued, "Allow me to ask one more question. Was the Celestial River of Death created by a supreme expert in imitation of the River of Time? Since you called it an incomplete product, did the creation fail?"

"Naturally."

Martial Emperor maintained his indifference, but he seemed more willing to converse with Su Yu. People would naturally flock toward those who were similar. Both Su Yu and he were likely the only existences remaining capable of seeing the true nature of the world.
