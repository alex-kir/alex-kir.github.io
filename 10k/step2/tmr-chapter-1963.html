Chapter 1963: Ninth Level Gains (1) (Teaser)

Su Yu only needed to push the door open and walk out. The door wasn't hard to open. But the moment Su Yu pushed it open, he blanked out. Great Zhou King and the others were also looking over curiously. All of them blanked out as well.

Rubble.

Yes. The outside had been reduced to rubble.  

The glorious Human Emperor's Palace had been reduced to rubble. Collapsed buildings and broken daos could be seen everywhere. The broken daos weren't visible to the others, but they were visible to Su Yu. He could see the broken daos that were tangled together.

And a corpse.

There was actually a corpse there. A corpse was nailed onto the wall by a spear, with a furious expression on its face. Even dead, the corpse was still radiating an incomparably powerful aura. Reality itself warped around the corpse.

Su Yu's face changed. He hurriedly looked around. Signs of combat could be seen everywhere. Heavenbridge Marquis had claimed that the various experts had vanished peacefully from the ninth level. Well, that was clearly nonsense.

Did he not feel the battle back then? Or was he sleeping on his job? The entire ninth level had been reduced to rubble by the intense combat.

Su Yu looked around blankly. The room behind him was the only building still standing on the ninth level. Everything else had been destroyed. The glorious palace was gone....
