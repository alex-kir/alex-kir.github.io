Chapter 1559: Great Deceit King (3) (Teaser)

Experts were arriving one after another. Some would help Su Yu while some would help the myriad races.

As for Su Yu, he only had one thought in his mind: opening the death tunnel and fleeing.

Holy shit!

He had nearly died.

The Celestial Chasm Pseudo Emperor had acted.

The Dragon Pseudo Emperor might take part as well. He was most likely waiting for a better opportunity to act. And at that point, escaping might no longer be possible. Just as Su Yu was about to start his escape, the Celestial Chasm Pseudo Emperor looked forth in surprise.

Biting sounds rang out from the empty air he was staring at. Abruptly, a gigantic figure emerged out of thin air. The newcomer seemed to be chewing on something unknown.

While chewing, the newcomer smiled and said, "You can't kill Su Yu! Not long ago, the laws of our great ancestor reacted to something. Su Yu seems to have completed the seventy-two forgings of my race. Therefore, you are not allowed to kill him." 

"Iron devourer..."

The Celestial Chasm Pseudo Emperor was greatly shaken.

The iron devourers had picked a side this epoch. Why?

He asked, "Why? The iron devourers had remained neutral even during the ninth epoch with that man around. Why choose Su Yu? I don't understand."   

The gigantic iron devourer flashed a silly smile and said, "Because...that man didn't complete the seventy-two forgings. I only know one thing. Since Su Yu has completed the 72-Forged Iron Devourer, he is our ally. He can't deny that since he cultivates the cultivation method of my race."

Su Yu hurriedly nodded his head in agreement.

Holy shit!

Yes! He would accept this! He could even call that giant bear his daddy if that was enough to secure their help. What a surprise....
