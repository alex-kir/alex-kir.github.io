Royal Consort Qi frowned slightly and said, "Convert the dao within you into pure dao power before fusing with Emperor Yu's daos. Emperor Yu's dao will embrace both of you. The two exchanged gazes. Great Xia King smiled and said, "Old Qin, let's see who can suppress this resistance first."

Next, he allowed the two to purely clash.

"Your Majesty, increase the power of your saber dao and devour the other saber forcefully."

Boom!

The two sabers continued clashing.
