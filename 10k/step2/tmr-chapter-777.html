Inside the massage center.

"That's not it. A friend told me that Zheng Heng is dead!"

"Who?"

"Zhang Heng?"

"I don't remember that name. Do I know him? Did we party together before?"

The two massage ladies were overjoyed. This was an era of cultivation. Everyone knew what source qi liquid was. This was a form of currency that could be used everywhere. A single drop could be easily sold for a hundred thousand anping dollars. Thus, they were definitely fine with this.
