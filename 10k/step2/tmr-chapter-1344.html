Chapter 1344: Life Death Fruit (2) (Teaser)

"Cough, cough, cough!"

Suddenly, Dao King coughed, assaulting everyone's minds with his cough. Next, Moge's voice rang out, breaking everyone's concentration, "Mayor Su, are you fine?"

They were doing this deliberately.

After blanking out slightly, Su Yu looked up at them and smiled, "I'm fine. I formed a natural character. How odd. How did I form a character out of nowhere? This is truly odd."

"..."

Everyone suspected that he was trying to infuriate them to death.

Meanwhile, Great Zhou King was staring at Su Yu solemnly. After a long while, he opened his mouth, "Su Yu, you will be a saint..."

Su Yu laughed in amusement. When he noticed that everyone was still staring at him, he said, "Everyone, don't take this seriously. Great Zhou King is trying to get me killed. It's pointless to envy me. Characters aren't that important. I can form them easily. In fact, I have so many natural characters I don't care about them anymore." 

Inwardly, he had an odd feeling. The radiance was not a human character either. Like the culture character, it came in the true form of all characters, the so-called willpower language.

Together, the two characters became civilization.

In truth, the two characters could be both significant and insignificant. Separate, each character wouldn't be anything too special....
