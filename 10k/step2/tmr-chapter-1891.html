Chapter 1891: Keep A Low Profile, Kill Less (1) (Teaser)

"Marquis?"

"Father?"

The white-haired old man and the young woman asked doubtfully.

Stable Army Marquis remained silent. Instead, he stood up with a solemn look in his eyes. His instinct honed over countless brushes with death was telling him that something had happened. He would never underestimate any reaction from his crisis sense.

Unlike the myriad races, humanity couldn't afford to be even a tiny bit careless in the Upper Realm. Any carelessness could result in death.

"Be on alert!"

Stable Army Marquis ordered. As his voice spread over the flower, everyone, regardless of what they were doing, stood up in surprise. Then, over a thousand people entered a state of high alert.

They assumed their standard protocol. The oldest would stand at the outermost part of their formation, with the younger ones at the middle. The weaker children would all be taken into their weapons.  

Of course, the death of the carrier of these weapons would also kill everyone in these weapons. Thus, the identities of these carriers remained a secret. Nobody would ask about it.

In any case, the marquis wouldn't be the carrier.
