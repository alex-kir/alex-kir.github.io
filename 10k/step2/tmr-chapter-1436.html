Su Yu couldn't be bothered to run around to search for treasures. There weren't a lot of people left on the fourth level. He Tu would probably be done with the level before long. In fact, even the fifth level was already mostly empty by now.

Those who had been informed about the coming disaster had fled. Those who were still clueless were either those from the minor races or lone travelers. There weren't a lot of them.

He was simply too rich. What could he do if he was rich enough not to care about these treasures?
