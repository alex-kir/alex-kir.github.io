Chapter 1351: Corpse Collector (3) (Teaser)

At the moment, the ancient city and its surroundings were packed with people.

Initially, everyone was wary of coming here. Eventually, they found that Su Yu had stopped sealing the city for no reason. He also seemed indifferent to those visiting his city. He would only charge the visitors some source qi liquid as the fee to enter. With that, everyone finally felt safe to gather in and around the city.

Suddenly, the sky above warped and a stone statue appeared. Below, everyone went silent and withdrew their auras. This had been happening quite frequently as of late. These stone statues were ancient Invincibles who had rarely left their cities in the past. But because of Su Yu, they started leaving their cities at random times, forcing the myriad races to be constantly on alert.

This time, the stone statue had not appeared alone. An old man could be seen behind him.

Looking down at the city from above, Zhao Li was hit by a sense of melancholy. It had been years since he was last here....
