Chapter 1160: Culture Tomb (1) (Teaser)

Su Yu was still unaware of the result reached by the higher-ups of the pavilion. But it did not take him long to receive the latest update.

Learning about the high price he was told to charge, he realized something. His original suggestion, one drop of Sunmoon blood essence, was not enough. He had been looking down on the pavilion too much. What was the pavilion known for? Their greed. He himself had scolded them many times for their greed.

How could he sell such an important piece of information for such a cheap price? As for the idea of creating a group before charging anyone wishing to join the group, that wasn't even an original idea. That was actually a tradition of the Heavenly Hunt Pavilion.

In fact, this same practice had once infuriated him so much previously that he refused to participate, forcing the pavilion to pay Niu Baidao two hundred strands of heavenearth profound light.

Just by providing a venue for Su Yu to negotiate with the myriad races, the pavilion had charged each participant one drop of Sunmoon blood essence. And this time, the information they were selling was much more valuable than a simple venue of negotiation.

Su Yu easily agreed to carry out the order faithfully.
