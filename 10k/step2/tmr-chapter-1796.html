Meanwhile, Su Yu was still struggling forward in the pen dao. He was trying to force his way forward, gaining more control over this dao. He did not know how much time had passed. He only knew that this tributary seemed endless. He had gone forward for a long time, but the end was still nowhere to be seen.

Just how much had Culture King developed this tributary? Then again, he wasn't exactly advancing completely blind. He could feel it whenever he reached far enough to cross a threshold and advance a degree.

"Fourth degree? So I'm about thirty percent through this dao..."

With that in mind, Su Yu gritted his teeth and pushed forward. Even with his impressive strength, he could only traverse about thirty percent of the dao. This pen dao was truly excessively powerful. Su Yu had a feeling his current strength had surpassed that of a regular Daofuse.

Even if he wasn't at the top, he would most certainly at the middle. Yet he was still a fourth-degree Eternal? Eventually, the character technique in front of him started dimming. The power inside was nearly drained....
