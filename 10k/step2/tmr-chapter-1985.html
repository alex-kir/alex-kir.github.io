Chapter 1985: War Preparations (2) (Teaser)

Su Yu and the others said nothing. Just then, the air around them turned heavy. A massive mountain stood before them. Su Yu stared at the mountain in shock.

Human Mountain. He had thought that it would only be a regular mountain. He had never cared too much for it. But the size of the mountain was truly shocking.

It was too big.

At a glance, it was at least a hundred thousand meters in height. The entire mountain was emitting a powerful aura. Quite a lot of people were gathered at the foot of the mountain.

Noticing that March was looking down, Celestial Devil smiled and said, "The experts have arrived with quite a lot of regular people. The chaos race has been murdering indiscriminately recently. The minor races were terrified so they brought their family here for protection." 

Almost everyone gathered below them was below the Daofuse Realm. As for the Daofuses, most of them had ascended the mountain.

There was a large palace at the top of the mountain. Back then, humanity had built it to serve as the new imperial palace. The construction was not completed as the ancient past had collapsed before humanity could finish building it. After some light renovation, the three major races had turned it into a venue for their assembly.
