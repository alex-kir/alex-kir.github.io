Chapter 1601: Returning To Human Realm (2) (Teaser)

Liu Hong stared at Su Yu with a slight frown. After a long while, he said, "Su Yu, you..."

"Teacher, were those dreams or memory transmissions?"

After a long silence, Liu Hong said, "Did you experience something similar before?"

"I'm the one asking here."

Liu Hong said helplessly, "What is there to answer? I never asked you about your fortuitous encounters! This is rude!"

He explained in an exasperated manner, "You can consider it a form of memory transmission. At times, some memories would appear randomly. That was how I became a top genius of the Great Xia Cultural Research Academy with my humble background."

"When did this start happening?"

"When did it start happening?" Liu Hong thought about it and answered, "About ten years ago. At the time, I was still a student in a secondary school. That was how I managed to get into the academy."

"About ten?" Su Yu pressed on, "When exactly?"

"You..." Liu Hong answered helplessly, "Almost fifteen years ago."

Su Yu frowned. He had his first nightmare at six. It had been around fourteen years since then, or to be precise, nearly fifteen years. Were the two events related...
