Chapter 728: Beginning Of Change (3) (Teaser)

Su Yu felt very fortunate that he had decided to join Great Ming. Otherwise, trouble might come even sooner with all of them concentrated in Great Xia. After seeing Niu Baidao off, Su Yu thought for a bit before leaving for the Clearheart Hall.

He intended to try teaching Hu Qi the life extending art. He wondered if it would work. If it really worked well, he would be able to rope in a bunch of elderlies to his side.

Along the way, he muttered to himself that he could no longer focus on increasing only his fame. At present, he already had sufficient fame. He needed more strength. Strength that was truly his to control.

"I need at least three more days to complete the fourth forging. I don't have enough heavenly source qi. Time to stock up on more heavenly source qi. I will be needing a large amount of blood essence for this."

At his current pace, even with the boost provided by the Life Trigger, he would still need about a dozen days to complete a forging. That was too slow for him. He couldn't afford to waste two years...
