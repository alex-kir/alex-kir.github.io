Chapter 1811: Crazies Galore (3) (Teaser)

Was this a joke?

His title was granted by the ancient imperial court. Nobody in this era was qualified to remove it!

Unlike him, the marquises in the hall panicked even more. One of them said, "Heavenly King, that pen of his...resembles...Culture King's pen! All the ancient conferments were written with the same pen! Since he's saying that so confidently, is it possible that he can really affect the laws with that pen?"

North Heavenly King trembled.

He would stand no chance against them with such a suppression on him.

Finally, North Heavenly King started to worry as well. But he maintained a nonchalant expression as he denied, "No. He's not strong enough."
