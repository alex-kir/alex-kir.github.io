Chapter 2128: First Return (1) (Teaser)

Just like that, fourteen top experts had died.

At the same time, the auras of both South Heavenly King and the giant started growing. Both were already Venerables, and both were growing to a higher level. Elsewhere, Dragonblood Marquis was also growing silently.

With the fourteen deaths, all the death spirits apart from those who had lost control over themselves calmed down.

***

Somewhere in the death spirit dao.

The giant, whose aura was growing stronger by the moment, frowned as it looked at Su Yu.

Su Yu was looking at him as well.

After a while, Su Yu's cold voice rang out, "Are you the full willpower of your main body? Or are you only a collection of remnant will? Is the Master of Death dead or alive?" 

"Dead or alive?" The giant replied, "Are you really asking this question to someone who has mastered death? Is this not laughable?" 

Su Yu laughed. "Mastered death? I admit that the Master of Death is incomparably talented. But I doubt death is truly under his control."

He was not denying that the Master of Death was strong. But fully mastering death? He found that hard to believe....
