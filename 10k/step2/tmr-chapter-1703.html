Chapter 1703: Setting An Example (3) (Teaser)

After a short wait, Great Zhou King and the others arrived.

There were fewer people in the group, and almost everyone remaining was suffering from heavy injuries. Humanity might have achieved a major victory this time, but the damage was also quite heavy, considering its lacking foundation.

They had lost eight Invincibles in the battle. At the moment, almost all surviving human Invincibles were gathered here. Twenty-six founding kings and some younger Invincibles. In total, there were only forty-three of them.

Including Fiery Sea King, humanity had lost nine Invincibles recently. In that case, humanity should have fifty-two Invincibles before all this happened. But when Su Yu noted that Heavenly Seal King was missing as well, he revised that number to fifty-three.

Everyone had a rough idea of the total number of Invincibles humanity had. But according to Su Yu's calculation, there should be fifty-six in total. Where were the missing three? Who were they? Su Yu was curious, but he didn't ask.

When the Invincibles of the two battlefields regrouped, they looked at each other before sighing. Some looked helpless, and some looked sad.
