Chapter 2107: Milking The River Of Time (1) (Teaser)

Su Yu did not bother to meet Hundred Battle King face to face. That was not necessary.

The current situation alligned with what he wanted. Even though they had only conversed from afar, Su Yu could feel that Hundred Battle King was not as terrible as he had originally speculated.

However, it was clear that the two had different ideologies. Six thousand years ago, Hundred Battle King had opted to fake his defeat even when he had the entire humanity to protect. That was not something Su Yu could accept.

Yes, Su Yu had once thought of doing the same. But the difference was that he didn't use to have humanity's support. It was only natural for him to stay away from them when he was unwanted.

By the time he started involving himself in the affairs of humanity, he knew that he couldn't abandon them anymore.
