Chapter 2167: The Curtain Rises (1) (Teaser)

The myriad races were quite efficient this time.

Twenty fake daos were no big deal.

After monopolizing the Dao Origin Land for so many years, the various races had collected quite a lot of fake daos. Some of the daos were in the possession of those unwilling to switch, and some were too weak to make the switch worth it.

They weren't worried that Su Yu would produce twenty new Daofuses with these daos. It wouldn't be so easy. Even if he could, these would be weak Daofuses that would lose to even the stronger Eternals. The myriad races were not scared of some trash Daofuses. Thus, the deal was completed smoothly.

***

The myriad races had visited Su Yu at night. By midnight, someone arrived again.

The visitor was here to deliver the fake daos. The visitor was none other than Thunderstorm. He was picked because the myriad races were worried about something. What was it? They were worried that Su Yu would instigate the person they sent to defect to his side.

Thunderstorm could defect for all they care. Thunderstorm's allegiance was already an open secret. Everyone knew he was on Hundred Battle King's side, but nobody said it. It was pointless. Since he insisted on staying with them, they would use him as cannon fodder...
