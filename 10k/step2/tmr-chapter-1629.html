Chapter 1629: Furious Action (3) (Teaser)

Su Yu said nothing. He was still staring at Great Yuan King with a smile.

Great Yuan King felt like Su Yu was wrong in the head. Was the bastard done staring at him? Why was someone so mentally unstable dreaming of becoming the leader? Great Yuan King was starting to suspect that he would be the first to suffer the moment Su Yu became the leader.

Just as that thought crossed his mind, he received a voice transmission from Su Yu, "Do not doubt your judgment. The moment I become the leader of the holy land, you will receive a mission to assassinate Tiangu, Your Majesty."

"..."

Fuck!

Great Yuan King cursed inwardly. What a madman! The bastard was already thinking of ways to abuse his power even when his ascension seemed so unlikely. More importantly, how did that bastard know what he was thinking?

He grew wary. Meanwhile, Su Yu sent another voice transmission, "Don't worry. That's a difficult job so I'll give you enough time. I can spare your life until the end of your time limit."

Finally, Great Yuan King erupted in anger and roared, "Su Yu!"

With a surprised expression, Su Yu asked, speaking out loud as well, "Yes, Your Majesty?"

"What did you just say to me?"

Su Yu chuckled, "What did I say?"

"..."

Shameless!

Great Yuan King was furious. How was he supposed to prove anything when Su Yu had merely been speaking through voice transmissions?

Some of the people in the hall frowned upon seeing Great Yuan King's loss of control. His temper seemed to have grown even worse than before. It was only the start of the conference. Why was he flipping out so soon?

Some were quite worried...
