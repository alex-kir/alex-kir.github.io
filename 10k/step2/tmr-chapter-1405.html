Chapter 1405: Harvest (3) (Teaser)

Su Yu curled his mouth with disdain. He had killed so many people that he would rarely get heavenly rewards for killing nowadays. This reward was probably an accumulation from all his recent kills. If his name was still recorded on the Heavenly Hunt Index, his highlighted accomplishment would probably be something like assisting in the killing of an Eternal.

Since then, he had killed a lot more people. However, he had not received a single heavenly reward for those kills. Not even killing Sunmoons was enough to grant him a heavenly reward anymore.

He would only receive a reward after his accumulated accomplishments reached a certain level. In short, the heavenly laws were valuing him so highly that not even killing index geniuses was worthy of a reward anymore.

Clearly, he had finally reached the required threshold for a reward after adding Zhan Ta to the rest of his recent kills.

As far as the heavenly laws were concerned, killing an Earth Index genius would hold more weight than killing even a third-stage Sunmoon. A young genius held a lot more...
