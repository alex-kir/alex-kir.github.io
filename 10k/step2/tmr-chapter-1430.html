Chapter 1430: Death List (1) (Teaser)

Fourth level.

The moment Su Yu stepped onto the fourth level, he felt a unique sensation.

The five viscera were akin to an oven, and the bowels were burning.

Su Yu inhaled deeply, sensing the power of the five elements in the air. In the path of cultivation, it was always said that the five viscera corresponded to the five elements. A certain understanding dawned on Su Yu. He must have arrived at the five viscera and six bowels of Old Zhou. Or to be precise, the fourth to the sixth levels were most likely the torso of Old Zhou. The navel would also be somewhere on these levels.

"What about the arms?"

Su Yu suddenly recalled that he had not seen anything resembling an arm. If Old Zhou had really been planted on the ground upside down, his arms might be drooping to the ground. In that case, were the arms in the basement?

Did Old Zhou's arms reach the Death Realm as well? That was possible. As for the fourth level, the source qi in the air was unimaginably thick.

"Are the lungs and heart here?"

The heart was an important organ for any cultivator. Even someone like Su Yu might die when his heart was damaged enough to prevent his body from regenerating. Or to be precise, his physical body would be dead.
