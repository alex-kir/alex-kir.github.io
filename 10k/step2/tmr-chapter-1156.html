Chapter 1156: If There Is No Su Yu (3) (Teaser)

After taking another look at the character, Wan Tiansheng smiled, "Yes, it shares a small part of an ability I have. Don't you have a fire of inheritance? Try to force the two characters to fuse before activating them again."

Su Yu blanked out. Forcefully fusing them? He had actually tried something like that before, but he had eventually stopped as that could easily destroy the characters involved. He considered it slightly and fused the character with his fire character.

Abruptly, there was an explosion of light. At that moment, Su Yu felt like his mind had become incomparably clear. It was as though no problem was too difficult for him to solve anymore.

At any given moment, his mind was giving birth to numerous thoughts. Those thoughts would remain for an instant before disappearing, replaced by more thoughts. In his unique state, Su Yu's comprehension of willpower deepened. Every single one of his characters also became much purer than before.

The seal character he had formed not long ago suddenly increased in tier as Su Yu seemed to have grasped the truth behind the concept of sealing. But this state did not last for long. Soon, he woke up from his reverie.

But he was still confused.

He looked at Wan Tiansheng questioningly. So what...could he do with this character?
