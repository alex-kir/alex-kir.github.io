Chapter 2259: You Need To Trust Me (2) (Teaser)

Su Yu merely watched as Martial Emperor departed. He did not bother to stop Martial Emperor. After taking a glance at the physical body, he took his crown off and dragged the body into the crown with a wave of his hand. Then, the crown vanished with a flicker, becoming one with the world again.

Only then did Great Zhou King exhale in relief. Then, he roared, "Congratulations, Your Majesty, for pacifying the Myriad Realms! Glory to Emperor Yu's Court!"

His voice spread everywhere. 

A total victory.

With Martial Emperor's surrender and Zhou Ji's departure, Su Yu had won. The chaotic war that had raged for a hundred thousand years was finally over. It had been ended by Su Yu. More danger might be awaiting them in the future, but there was no denying that Su Yu had indeed pacified the Myriad Realms.

He had achieved what nine human lords had failed. Cheers rang out as hundreds of powerhouses roared.

"Congratulations, Your Majesty, on your conquest!"

"Congratulations!"

One person roared after another.

The senior powerhouses, in particular, were crying tears of joy.

Victory!

After a hundred thousand years, they had finally secured victory in Su Yu's epoch. A total victory. The myriad races had been pacified. Hundred Battle King had been killed. Zhou Ji had been suppressed. Martial Emperor had been recruited.

The death spirits had submitted. The Myriad Realms had submitted. One could even argue that Su Yu's achievement had reached a level of glory...
