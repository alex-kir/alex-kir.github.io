Chapter 1974: Reward (3) (Teaser)

After looking around for a bit, the white dog approached Soy Bun. The two stared at each other for a bit before Soy Bun asked, "Is my young lad not here?"

Was his young lad left behind again?

In response, Fat Ball merely shook his head. At that, a little furball flew out of his ear. The little furball was sulking. After giving Su Yu a glare, he jumped onto Soy Bun's head. Yummy had changed. How could Yummy reject him when he asked to enter Yummy's sea of willpower?

Su Yu had refused the request because he was storing too many powerful items in his sea of willpower. He was worried of an accident when he carelessly exposed the little furball to these items.

He swept his gaze through the gathered crowd before addressing them with a smile, "A few Daofuses are absent. The death spirits are not called for this meeting. But I have to say that just the sight of all of you is enough to improve my mood.

"During my recent journey to the Upper Realm, I saw numerous Daofuses and a considerable number of heavenly kings among the myriad races. I was feeling quite pressured. But I can now see that our Emperor Yu's Court isn't that weak either."

Lan Tian stepped forth and said with a smile, "You are too modest, Emperor Yu. During the previous battle in the Upper Realm, you killed ten heavenly kings and eight Daofuses, showcasing the might of Emperor Yu's Court."
