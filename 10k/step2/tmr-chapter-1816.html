Chapter 1816: Hit The Disobedient Ones (2) (Teaser)

This was precisely what Su Yu wanted. He would always be unpredictable.

Rather than keeping his guard up indefinitely, Su Yu would much rather seize the initiative. That was especially true since he could already sense them. He wasn't foolish enough to leave them around to stab him in the back.

Not long after, a gigantic turtle appeared from the sky.

"Emperor Yu..."

The old turtle called out in confusion. A war against the death spirits in the river? This wasn't part of the plan.

"Are Great Qin King and the others around?"

"They are ready."

Su Yu ordered, "Have them all enter. Even death spirits will need time to awaken from the river. We only need to kill faster than their awakening. We will clean this river." 

At that, a powerful life force erupted from afar.

Boom!

Great Qin King and the others had entered.

Then, Su Yu opened his Heaven Gate and shared his vision with the old turtle. Instantly, the old turtle saw numerous small lines around him. Su Yu said, "Each line is connected to a marquis. Kill them all. They are only hiding in the river because they believe they can't be detected there. How strong can they be? I refuse to believe they are strong enough to stop us. Kill them."

"Understood."

With the arrival of Great Qin King and the others, the entire river started roiling intensely. More and more death...
