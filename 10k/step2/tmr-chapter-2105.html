Chapter 2105: Kings Do Not Meet (2) (Teaser)

Xia Huyou's heart thumped. Not even Human Emperor's return would be beneficial for humanity? That might really be the case. Su Yu had hinted at something similar before. According to Su Yu, Human Emperor would have returned long ago if he had the advantage. What was the point of dragging things out? Clearly, he wasn't doing too well either.

Both Su Yu and Hundred Battle King agreed on this.

"Are things really so hopeless to us?"

"Of course not." Hundred Battle King said, "Why would I bother to return otherwise? But there is no need for me to say much. You will understand when the time is right." 

Xia Huyou nodded. He couldn't help feeling like Hundred Battle King was actually a decent leader to follow. But he was Su Yu's friend. And Su Yu was the human lord of this epoch. Thus, he wouldn't change his allegiance.

Before long, they arrived at Emperor Yu's Court.

Looking at the vast palace, Hundred Battle King smiled and said nothing. But Riversea Marquis said with a frown, "Excessive extravagance and waste. Back when His Majesty became the human lord, he did not construct something so wasteful."

That was true. This building was a bit too wasteful.

It was built by Zhu Tiandao and Marquis...
