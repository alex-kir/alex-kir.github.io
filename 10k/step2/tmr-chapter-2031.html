Chapter 2031: Unyielding Torchbearers (3) (Teaser)

Sixteen sets of formations.

Su Yu started distributing the mobile formations among his people.

As for the ones who had not received any formations, including Heavendoom, Su Yu opened his Mark of Civilization and said, "The rest of you aren't strong enough. Just hide in here for a bit. We need to stay hidden until the very end. We're going to do something big this time."

They seemed unwilling. Both Heavendoom and Grandstar were among the ones asked to hide inside the book. Initially, Heavendoom was feeling quite bad. But when he saw that Grandstar was being treated the same way, he smiled as his mood went through a miraculous recovery. He was too weak to get one of these formations, but this guy wasn't any better off than him!

Apart from Su Yu and Lan Tian, Heavendoom, Grandstar, September, Great Qin King, and Great Xia King were the ones without a formation. It did not matter to Su Yu and Lan Tian as they had their own ways to avoid the detection of the ancient beasts.

Everyone else, including Snow Orchid General and the others, was given a mobile formation each. Su Yu said, "Let me repeat myself, everyone. Obey all orders regardless of the circumstance. Even when you're in the middle of an intense battle, do not hesitate to carry out your order."

"Yes!"

Most of them answered unhesitatingly. Snow Orchid General and her group appeared rather uncomfortable with this, but they still answered, "We will naturally follow your orders, Human Lord Su."

Su Yu nodded, "That'd better be the case."
