Chapter 1867: Hierarchy Of Contempt (1) (Teaser)

"How terrifying."

Su Yu, who had just left the Human Realm, exhaled in relief. That was truly terrifying. No wonder they had insisted on his return. How cruel of those people to attempt to sever his path of cultivation like that.

After a while, Su Yu looked back at the Human Realm and chuckled. Leaving his descendant behind to fight another epoch? Forget it. His life was bitter enough. There was no point in letting his descendants enjoy the same bitterness.

He would end everything in this epoch. If he failed, there was no need for there to be another epoch.

"Time to raise some daos."

With a flicker, Su Yu vanished.
