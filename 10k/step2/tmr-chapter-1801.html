Chapter 1801: Switching Guardians (2) (Teaser)

Su Yu was even ready to forge ten thousand carrier treasures into his book, upgrading all his pages into heaven weapons. With this, he was sure his Mark of Civilization would surpass the Book of Time. Ultimately, he would be able to fuse ten thousand daos and recreate a new Myriad Realms. He was a lot more ambitious than even the Time Sage.

In his opinion, since the Time Sage had abandoned the Book of Time, it was likely that she no longer required it. Perhaps he should take it apart and forge it into his book as well. At present, he had grown strong enough to gain a slight control over the Book of Time.

Back then, Time Sage did not have the courage to exterminate the myriad races since there were various Masters of Laws around. She could only hunt them down in secret. Su Yu was different. He had the courage to do anything.

The daos in the Book of Time felt too flimsy. They didn't feel real. That was not acceptable for him. Su Yu was crazy enough to exterminate every race hostile to him and fill his Mark of Civilization with them.

With a look of resignation, Heavenly Forge King said, "Fine, I accept this mission."

He could already picture his terrible life in the near future.
