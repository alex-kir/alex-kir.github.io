Chapter 1769: Double Daofuse (3) (Teaser)

Su Yu did not say too much. For him, gossip was meant to be remembered. And he knew quite a lot. For example, the love story of Lan Tian and Silk Destroying King. For example, the ugly Battle King. For example, Battle King's descendant, the number one pretty boy, Great Xia King. For example, Martial King and his countless wives. For example, Control King was a traitor. For example, Culture King had a psychopathic younger sister.

Had he leaked any of them?

Nope.

Oh right, he also knew about Martial Emperor's buttocks being used as a garden. That was quite a big gossip as well.

Fortunately, he was not the type to spread gossip.

Right, right, there was also the gossip about the Qin Family. However, Qin Zhen had leaked it himself. A story between him and immortal fairies.

As Su Yu recalled all that, he had the urge to write a book in the future. A book collecting the gossip of the various eras. History must be remembered, right? Thus, he decided to preserve these gossips in a way that only the top experts in existence could see them. These shall be the secret gossips of the world.

While thinking about all that, he drew his saber and got to work. He stabbed it at the gigantic beast in the air. He was pushed one step back after leaving only a tiny hole in the corpse. In mere moments, the hole had repaired itself. 

He gasped and said, "Wasn't the skin of this beast removed?"

Even dead and without its skin, the body was still unbelievably powerful. What if this beast were still alive? Su Yu would probably fail to leave even a mark on it with his saber. The gap between them was incredibly big.

The current Su Yu was still weaker than Daofuses, but he was definitely far stronger than regular ninth-degree Eternals.
