Chapter 2104: Kings Do Not Meet (1) (Teaser)

Hundred Battle King strolled across the Human Realm with an entourage behind him. However, nothing felt the same anymore. He could feel that he was unwanted in the current Human Realm.

He could sense the emotions of the thirty-six prefects behind him. Indifference, fear, indignation, hatred, and fury. They were too weak to hide their emotions from him. Perhaps they were annoyed by his return now.

He was supposed to be sealed six thousand years ago.

He was supposed to be dead.

Now that a new human lord had emerged, why did he return? 

In fact, the people around Hundred Battle King had sensed those emotions as well. They could even hear what the common people were talking about.

Some were asking about the purpose of this old human lord's return after so many years.

Some were saying that he was supposed to be dead.

Southbrook Marquis, the strongest subordinate of Hundred Battle King, heard those words as well.

With a complicated expression, he said, "Your Majesty, these are ignorant fools. Ignore them. They know nothing. If victory was possible back then, why would we have taken such a drastic action? We only did that because we didn't want to ruin the entire human race."
