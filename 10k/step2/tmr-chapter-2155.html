Chapter 2155: Changes In The Three Races (1) (Teaser)

"Your Majesty, were you not worried that they would attack?"

Giant Axe Marquis was sweating nervously. The myriad races had quite a lot of Venerables. There were only four of them. There was no promise that they would be a match for all those Venerables.

"Hmm?" Su Yu said, "Why would they attack? They're not dumb. We're here to help. We're even kind enough to send their people up here. Apart from the dragons, who would risk their own lives to attack someone here to help?" 

Here to help? Giant Axe Marquis cursed inwardly. This guy was scheming against them. How was he here to help?

Su Yu smiled. "Don't worry. The myriad races would have attacked the tiger and dragon long ago if they were decisive enough to attack. These fellows have both strength, talent, and combat will. But they lack the ability to act as one. There are too many schemes brewing between the different factions among the myriad races."

For example, Venerable Dragon would be extra careful as he couldn't risk dying. He was the final hope of his race. Venerable Phoenix was in the same position. Venerable Dao was worried that his death would lead to the suppression of the immortal race. They had too many things to worry...
