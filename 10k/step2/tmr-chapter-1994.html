Chapter 1994: Spatial Beast (2) (Teaser)

"I can only hope that we can find more of our old friends at the Dao Origin Land."

Venerable Desolate said, "I believe there are some still hiding there. After reaching the limit of their lifespan, they can only seal themselves since a breakthrough is not possible. However, an intense war is upon us. They won't need to worry about their excessive accumulation of power anymore since they will have plenty of chance to exhaust their power. It is time for them to emerge. The time for hiding is over." 

He was certain there were more slumbering experts in the Dao Origin Land.

Venerable Scrape smiled and said, "When we're done moving this mountain, we can go to the Dao Origin Land and begin our sweep. That is an excellent land of cultivation, but its importance will drop drastically once it's confirmed that the Heaven Gate can be used to suppress the daos gained from the Dao Origin Land. Thus, it won't be to big of a loss for us to ruin the Dao Origin Land." 

"Perhaps we can even dig out some of the old fellows of the human race this time."

They laughed and continued moving toward the Chaos Mountain. As they moved, more and more people noticed the gigantic mountain floating in the sky. Six Venerables were moving the mountain....
