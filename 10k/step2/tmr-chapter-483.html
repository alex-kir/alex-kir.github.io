Eighteenth Secondary School.

At the entrance.

When Su Yu and company arrived, Bai Feng and the others were walking out of the rear mountain.

That was quite a large group.

Wu Yuehua, Hu Ping, Zhao Mingyue, Xia Yunji...

All these people had arrived.

"..."
