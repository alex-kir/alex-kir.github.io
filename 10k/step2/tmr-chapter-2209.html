Chapter 2209: Clash Of Sovereigns, Full Victory (1) (Teaser)

"Hahaha!"

The world trembled, and a stretch of the River of Time ran dry as Martial Emperor's aura enveloped the entire world. His long hair fluttered in the wind as a set of golden armor appeared on his body. He would look a lot cooler if...his hair were less sparse. Unfortunately, he was balding.

Martial Emperor had likely not noticed that. His attention was occupied elsewhere. His legs were no longer opened in a split, but he couldn't fully straighten them either. They curved slightly when he stood. This was the side effect of staying in a split for over a hundred thousand years.

Balding hair and curved legs. Also, his neck was slightly curved downward as well. After all, his neck had been snapped to the ground for many years.

Powerful aura, gorgeous armor, and arrogant laughter...

Yeap. He was impressive. If one ignored his appearance, one would lament his overbearing presence. But when one looked at his appearance, one would lament that his appearance did not fit his reputation.

Then again, this was an era where strength mattered most. It didn't matter if he wasn't handsome. Someone who was handsome, talented, smart, brave, proud, and decisive like Su Yu was too rare.

These were Su Yu's thoughts. He floated above the river with his hands clasped...
