Chapter 1531: Dummy (3) (Teaser)

Su Yu started fleeing clumsily.

Inwardly, he was lamenting that death spirits were indeed easy to fool. These fellows had taken the bait without any thought. Then again, this was understandable. This was the Death Realm. The dead were not known for their cunning. That might be why.

Fine, fine. Su Yu decided to be kind and teach them a lesson. They might be dead, but one could still learn after dying, right? He opened his yang acupoint and slowly released the seal around his heart. His death qi started fading away even as he started borrowing a bit of Xing Yue's strength.

He only needed to do one thing: punch his opponent. 

If one punch was not enough, he would punch a few more times.

Just as the younger tiger's claws reached the back of Su Yu's head, he coldly said, "Die, Kid!"

Suddenly, Su Yu turned around and threw a punch. The tiger did not even bother to avoid the attack. There was no way he would miss such a target. Just before his punch struck, he wondered if there were heavenly rewards for killing death sovereigns.

Probably not.

He had killed quite a lot of death spirits before, but he had never been rewarded for it. He could harvest their blood essence, but their blood essence wasn't too useful...
