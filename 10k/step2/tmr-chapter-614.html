Chapter 614: First Visit To The Great Ming Cultural Research Academy (2) (Teaser)

After a while, Su Yu wiped the nonexistent sweat on his head. This Great Ming Cultural Research Academy was too interesting, to the point he wondered if he had arrived at some sort of a theme park.

Before long, a massive door appeared before him.

"Multiple Character Fusion Faculty."

The name of the faculty was written in a flamboyant cursive font. Before he could step through the door, he heard someone inside shouting, "Let's go! Big Ball has just given birth! Let's take a look!"

Then, a few students rushed outside the faculty. The student leading the group ran the fastest and nearly collided with Su Yu.

He stopped and asked, "Student, Big Ball has given birth. Have you seen it?"

"..."

What an energetic lad. This was unlike the multiple character faction in Great Xia where all its members were struggling bitterly.

Su Yu didn't know what to say. He wondered if he could really gain the support he needed here before returning to Great Xia to get his revenge.

"No..."

"Oh! We're going there right now! Are you coming?"

"No thanks."

The students then ignored him and ran off. After a while, one of them asked curiously, "Is that...Su Yu?"

"I guess so?"
