Chapter 2134: Full Resurrection (1) (Teaser)

Somewhere in the death spirit dao.

With the giant gone, Su Yu started resurrecting the others. Lightning and thunder roared. He extracted a large amount of life and death powers, causing the entire world to shake violently.

South Heavenly King was the second to be resurrected. Her resurrection generated powerful lightning tribulations as lightning bolts rained down on Su Yu's world, to the point it felt as though his world was about to shatter.

Ultimately, South Heavenly King survived. She was scorched by the lightning bolts, but she had returned. Like Marquis Lanshan, she looked different after returning to life. Her expression was as cold as ever, or to be precise, she looked even colder than before. Her attire was quite different as well. Unlike the others, she was not clad in anything eyecatching or luxurious. She only wore a simple, black dress.

She did not seem to be a human. Su Yu did not know her race. She had never mentioned it either. But after her resurrection, he could sense clearly that she was indeed not a human. However, she also wasn't a divine, devil, or immortal....
