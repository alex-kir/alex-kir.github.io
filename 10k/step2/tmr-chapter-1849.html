Chapter 1849: The Friendly Su Yu (1) (Teaser)

Inside the main hall.

Su Yu continued smiling for a long while. When he was finally done thinking, he stopped smiling and said, "Sit down. Do not be so reserved. You are all guests and seniors of mine."

He felt so friendly. But the friendlier he seemed, the more fearful the four were. They looked at Great Zhou King and Wan Tiansheng. The two said nothing and sat down to the right and left of Su Yu.

Then, a cup of tea appeared before each of them. They started sipping tea. They were both doing the same thing. As fellow old foxes, they knew that the safest thing to do was stay silent, sip tea, and listen. In fact, Wan Tiansheng had the habit of attending meetings with a large pot of tea back then. Great Zhou King had a similar habit as well.

After all, both were cultured scholars. Unlike the barbaric warriors, they did not enjoy quarreling. Noticing that, the four sat down quietly, not daring to say anything.

Su Yu said, "Give me a simple description of the situation up there. Also tell me what you need. Do they have any message for us? Tell me about the situation of the various races up there as well." 

He added, "What are your names?"

"Zhao Chuan."

"Ping Yao."

"Zhou Nan."

"Zhang Hong."
