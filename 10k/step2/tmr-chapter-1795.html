Chapter 1795: Dao Fusion Success (2) (Teaser)

After a short rest, Su Yu took a step toward the gap. Instantly, a massive suction force erupted from the gap, seemingly trying to drag him into a boundless abyss.

"Open!"

With a roar, his life force surged.

He was already very strong prior to this. His completed character technique only served to further boost his excessive strength. This was merely a dao fusion. Something like this wouldn't be able to halt his advance. He transformed into a giant before completing his step forward.

Rumble!

At the same time, the character technique smoothened the path before him while the human lord's token suppressed the violent waves around him. The Mark of Civilization also made an appearance, pushing away the incoming power of law.

"Today, nothing can stop me from fusing with this dao!"

Su Yu snorted coldly. Stopping him? Dream on! He would persist with using his physical body to bridge a path forward. Culture King might be strong, but he was no weakling either. He was only trying to fuse with this dao, not snap it. How dare the dao stop him?

Boom!

Step after step, he forced his way deeper into the pen dao. After an indeterminate amount of time, a massive rumble rang out. With that, Su Yu was finally across the gap, officially entering the stretch...
