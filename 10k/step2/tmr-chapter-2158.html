Bamboo Mountain.

At present, the Bamboo Mountain was empty. Some peaks had been dug off, and their dao center had been sealed off. Things were looking quite bleak. Even though the iron devourers had not been gone for long, the entire place looked like it had been abandoned for quite a while.

March removed the seal and allowed everyone entry. They didn't have too many people in their group. Snow Orchid General remained silent and Fat Ball was busy looking around. Heavenbridge Marquis was the busiest of all as he started cleaning the place. He had no choice as he was the weakest here. What else could he do but some odd jobs here and there?

He worked rapidly, cleaning an empty space before building a bamboo residence for Su Yu's group. For some reason, he seemed to be enjoying it. He was in a great mood since they were finally in the Upper Realm again.

He couldn't wait to kill Yue Hao and that Ancestor from Control King's lineage. He would benefit greatly from that. If they were to remain in the Lower Realm, he would need to set his gaze on Martial Emperor to grow himself.
