Chapter 1161: Culture Tomb (2) (Teaser)

Mo Duona did not say anything else. Instead, he contacted the pavilion and agreed to pay. A short while later, a figure appeared in front of him. The newcomer was not a white mask. Instead, it was a faceless elder.

Mo Duona's eyes flickered as he said, "The Heavenly Hunt Pavilion is truly generous this time. An elder is personally doing a job like this? Is the pavilion not afraid of losing their elders?"

The faceless elder spoke in an enigmatic voice, "We might worry about others, but we believe in you, Devil Lord Mo Duona. You will only kill me if you can do it yourself. You won't lower yourself to set up an ambush against me."

Mo Duona laughed, "Perhaps you're right. I hope you're still alive when I'm finally strong enough to kill you." 

"Thanks for your well wishes."

Before long, the payment was completed. After receiving the payment, the elder vanished with a flicker.

Staring at the empty spot in front of him, Mo Duona inhaled deeply and said, "Looks like the pavilion is placing a high level of importance on this. Even their elders have been deployed."
