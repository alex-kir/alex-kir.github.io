Chapter 1039: Su Yu Is Dead? (3) (Teaser)

Su Yu stopped thinking about anything else and focused on only one thing: his cultivation.

Every bit of strength mattered.

Three hours later, he completed his forty-first body forging after using eighteen strands of heavenearth profound light.

After five hours, he consumed twenty strands of heavenearth profound light to complete his forty-second body forging.

The consumption was rising at a terrifying rate.

Eight hours later, he completed the forty-third body forging with twenty-two strands of heavenearth profound light.

He had consumed sixty strands just to complete three forgings. And this number would only increase the stronger he grew.

Twelve hours later, he completed the forty-fourth body forging with twenty-four strands.

A total of twenty-eight hours had passed. He had only completed four forgings with eighty-four strands. Including what he consumed during his previous cultivation session, he had consumed almost half of what he had.

All in all, he had completed fourteen body forgings since the auction. He was quite satisfied with this growth. His physical strength had reached the level of 120,000 acupoints.

At this point, Su Yu had probably surpassed the previous Mo Duona, who was a seventh-stage Cloudbreach...
