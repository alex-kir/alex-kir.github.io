Chapter 2094: Come Kill Me (3) (Teaser)

Su Yu hurriedly concealed himself, but it wasn't working as reality itself was shattering. He summoned his clones, but his clones fell apart almost immediately. Even transforming his body into an elemental form didn't work as he was instantly pushed out of his transformed state by the impact.

After a while, he reappeared about ten thousand meters away from the dragon with a bloodied and mangled body.

"Hu..."

He panted helplessly. How impressive. This fellow was probably good enough to fight three Venerables alone. He couldn't do anything against this dragon. He had only survived due to the sheer number of tricks in his possession.

Suddenly, all of Su Yu's hair stood on end. He hurriedly moved away, but before he could move far, a tail appeared noiselessly behind him before stabbing through his chest. With a roar, Su Yu vanished before reappearing elsewhere. But a burly man was already waiting before him.

A punch was thrown toward him.

Boom!

Su Yu blocked the punch with both his arms, but his arms were blown apart before the fist landed on his chest, sending him flying far away.

With a flicker, the burly man reappeared behind Su Yu and kicked, sending Su Yu flying even as a fountain of blood burst out of his back.

"Your Majesty!"

Everyone exclaimed with alarm. This enemy was too powerful. That burly man was none other than the humanoid form...
