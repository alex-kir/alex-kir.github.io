Chapter 1615: Martial King's Descendant (1) (Teaser)

Su Yu the saint.

In only a few days, Su Yu was turned into a legend. Some were suspicious as all the rumors had emerged suddenly. They wondered if Su Yu was deliberately spreading these rumors.

But did that matter? No.

The stories mentioned in the rumors were mostly true.

Su Yu was truly strong. His backers were truly strong as well. The various prefectures couldn't find an effective way to dispute those rumors. It was also true that Su Yu had saved Great Qin King. It was true that he had killed enough people for the myriad races to tremble in his presence.

When all the stories were true, how the rumors emerged was no longer important. The masses had their own way of judging the world. They did not care about the scramble for power of the strong.

What did they truly care about?

The safety of Human Realm. Their own safety. Their family's safety.

Su Yu was strong. He had powerful backers. He was willing to help humanity, but he could always ignore humanity. What would the masses choose? Help Su Yu return to humanity or push him away?

Any normal person would know...
