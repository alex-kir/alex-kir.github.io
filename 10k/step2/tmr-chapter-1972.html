Chapter 1972: Reward (1) (Teaser)

"Emperor Yu!"

"Your Majesty!"

When Su Yu returned to the eighth level, everyone greeted him with excitement. Clearly, the harvest was good.

After looking around, Su Yu's gaze landed on the Radiant King's Residence and raised his brow, "Is Great Ming King not out yet?"

"No." Great Zhou King answered, "I checked, but the residence is protected by formations. I couldn't enter."

Su Yu soared up and flew toward Radiant King's Residence. As he approached, a large formation rose from the residence.

Su Yu raised his brow and swung his arm. At that, the human lord's token descended from the sky. When he focused, he sensed that the human lord's token was receiving a large amount of fortune from Radiant King's Residence.  

Su Yu smiled and said, "I believe there is a fortuitous encounter in there. This isn't a bad thing. Since Great Ming King is receiving something from his ancestor, he should be fine. Let's go. We'll leave first. Martial Emperor has agreed to open a path for us to leave and exit directly from the eighth level. There is no need for us to wait here."

He still had many things to do. He had to return to the Upper Realm as well. He couldn't predict when the war in the Upper Realm would start. He wanted to be there personally to stay updated at all...
