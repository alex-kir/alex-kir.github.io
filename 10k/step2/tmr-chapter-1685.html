Chapter 1685: Dual Mountainsea (1) (Teaser)

The white dog was surprisingly easygoing. He had agreed to almost all of Su Yu's requests after arriving. He was only stubborn about one thing: his mission to guard his master's home.

Su Yu did not know if this was due to the white dog's kindness, or perhaps the white dog had only been so helpful due to his inheritance from the Time Sage and Culture King. Was that why the white dog had refrained from biting him? Unfortunately, he was in a rush. He would have loved to spend more time with this loyal dog.

Not wanting to waste more time, Su Yu stood up and said, "Senior Fat Ball, I'll be taking my leave for now."

He looked at the book spirit and tea plant. With a slight hesitation, he asked, "Could you...hide for a bit?"

"Hide?" The tea plant looked at the book spirit, who nodded with a smile. Then, he transformed back into a book. As for the tea plant, she also vanished, turning into an imprint on the cover of the book.

For some reason, Culture King seemed to love books a lot.

That much was obvious from how a mere book he had written had grown so strong after developing sentience. Then again, the original form of the book, the Myriad Dao Sutra, was most likely something...
