Chapter 1804: Grand Dream Turtle (2) (Teaser)

One after another, the guardians left Grand Dream City.

Some left to prepare for what was to come, and some left to have a small celebration with their fellow guardians. Since this was a liberation, their mood was quite good. Most of them were in a rush to go out and have a good meal before doing anything else. In the end, only Su Yu and the old turtle were left behind.

The old turtle sipped his tea in silence for a bit before saying, "Five gone. This is within expectations. You are free to handle the ones remaining if traitors emerge among them. But as for the five..."

Su Yu laughed and asked, "Do you not trust me?"

"No." The old turtle said, "It's not the issue of trust. I'm just worried. I'm worried that you will enlist them by force when the fighting gets difficult." 

Su Yu thought about it and said, "I won't. Since a promise is given, I won't go back on it. If the situation is allowed to worsen to a state where I need to conscript five Eternals to war, we might as well accept our defeat decisively."
