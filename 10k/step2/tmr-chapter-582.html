Chapter 582: The Fall of Mountainseas (1) (Teaser)

Before a large mountain peak.

Su Yu stood there and looked at the mountain peak before crouching down and looking around. His eyes flickered as he scanned the area. Was this the place? Was this the place he saw when reading the memories of the deceased sect master? There were a lot of similarities.

"It's nearby. Seniors, please check and see if any enemies are hiding around us." Su Yu added, "Spread out and watch out for any sneak attacks. In the event of combat, ensure that the fight does not spread our way."

In the sky, one Mountainsea after another appeared. They all stayed about a kilometer away from Su Yu's group. Su Yu was right. If a fight broke out between Mountainseas, the resulting shockwaves could easily reach the students if they weren't far enough.

Among the dao protectors, there were six Mountainseas and two Cloudbreach cultivators. Initially, there were three Cloudbreach cultivators, but one had perished. Both of them were ninth-stage Cloudbreach cultivators. They were respectively from the black panther and the flying wolf races.

At this time, the cloud tiger elder said, "Black Panther, Flying Wolf, the two of you can stay with the students. It's enough for us to stand guard in the surroundings."

Since these were Cloudbreach cultivators, the other Mountainseas weren't so confident in their ability to guard their surroundings....
