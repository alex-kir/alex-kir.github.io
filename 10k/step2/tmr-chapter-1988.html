It was capable of seeing through illusions, observing the fortune of others, and spying on the past and future.

"How brazen!" Yu Xi sneered and said, "The nethers are quite brazen for leaving only four Daofuses behind. Only one is a peak Daofuse. There isn't even a pseudo king here."

Her head snapped in a certain direction, looking at a faraway dao center belonging to a minor race known as the dark phantom race. This was a relatively weak race with only two Daofuses.
