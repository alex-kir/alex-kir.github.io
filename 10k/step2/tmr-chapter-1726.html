Chapter 1726: Back To The Death Realm (2) (Teaser)

When Su Yu looked at him, Heavendoom said, "What? The death sovereigns at our places have changed many times. Just the territory under my city has switched ownership five or six times. Some were killed by the other death sovereigns, and some were killed by me for charging out of the tunnel. Even that is not that high a number since it has been a hundred thousand years."

Grandstar smiled and explained, "Xing Yue knows restraint. She rarely leaves unless it's under the order of the heavenly laws."

The truth was very simple. Grandstar was quite satisfied with his obedient death sovereign. Thus, he had never seen the need to kill her.

In fact, he would even help Xing Yue against the roaming aggressive death sovereigns to protect her rule.

Grandstar continued, "Furthermore, I have known Xing Yue for so many years. We are old friends. Am I right, Xing Yue?"

Xing Yue responded with a cold snort. This felt like a provocation! But she had to endure it since she couldn't beat any of them. What could she do if she was weaker? 

Suddenly, she thought of something and ordered coldly, "Su Yu, take them and piss off! I don't want to see them here."

Yes. She had remembered that Su Yu was her subordinate.

Su Yu coughed awkwardly and nodded. "Yes, we're leaving immediately. By the way, where are Senior Xia Chen and the others?"

Even Liu Hong had gone missing....
