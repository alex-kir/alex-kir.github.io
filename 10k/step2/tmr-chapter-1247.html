Chapter 1247: Saintless Skies (1) (Teaser)

Thanks to Grandstar, Zhu Tiandao became famous. More famous than before, to be specific. Now, his name was known across the Myriad Realms. The one who had slain four Invincibles in one day. Not an Invincible, yet slaughtering Invincibles like they were bugs.

As for whether people would actually believe this...things had changed. If even Wan Tiansheng, a so-called peak Mountainsea, could slay two Invincibles in one day, why couldn't Zhu Tiandao do better than that?

Everyone was more surprised at the speed with which the three Invincibles had died. They had died in succession, making it clear that they had been killed together. It was possible that Zhu Tiandao had received the assistance of Wan Tiansheng and Lan Tian. Some guessed that Lan Tian was actually strong enough to kill Invincibles as well. Some also speculated that Wan Tiansheng was actually not that badly injured, so he must have helped significantly during the battle.

With three of them joining hands, it would be much less surprising for three Invincibles to die in succession against them. In fact, that was the conclusion of the three Invincibles who were still fighting Great Han King in the Human Realm. They had seen the three leaving Nanyuan with their own eyes. 

Of course, First Yellow was also present when the three Invincibles died. When his identity was exposed, he would probably get to share some of the glory as well. After all, that glory...
