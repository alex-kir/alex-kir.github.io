Chapter 1936: This Is My Territory (1) (Teaser)

Su Yu was tasked with handling the six human Daofuses, but he had actually dealt with seven of them, including Wen Qi. The ease with which he had captured South Suppression Marquis had indeed shocked and awed Cloud Water Marquis and the others.

That was too fast.

No matter what, South Suppression Marquis was a peak Daofuse. Yet he had been dealt with so easily.  As someone who had survived the myriad races in the Heavenly Hole Range for so many years, South Suppression Marquis was by no means someone so easily captured.

Suddenly, Brave Martial General recalled that fleeting sense of terror she had felt when she met Su Yu. She had almost forgotten about that. Cloud Fire Marquis was frowning as well.

As all of them focused on Su Yu, Cloud Water Marquis asked, "Human Lord, have you opened the Heaven Gate?"

Su Yu nodded.

Sure enough!

All of them were shocked to hear that, with Shadow Marquis being the sole exception, as he was already aware of that. This was the first person to open the Heaven Gate since the ancient past.

Cloud Water Marquis asked again, "Human Lord, do you have the bloodline of an ancient powerhouse?"

In the past, Su Yu loved claiming relationships with powerful individuals.

But now, he reacted differently....
