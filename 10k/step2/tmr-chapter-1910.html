Chapter 1910: Let's Cut Someone Down (2) (Teaser)

Stable Army Marquis paled as he heard all that. He knew that Su Yu's group was powerful. They even had someone at the level of a heavenly king: Lan Tian. But that was the sole expert of such level in their group. There was still a gap between Great Zhou King and a heavenly king. The same could be said for Su Yu.

Including Shadow Marquis, they wouldn't be able to gather more than eight Daofuses. Even including Cloud Fire Marquis, they would have nine Daofuses. The gap was too big. The myriad races would assemble more than double the number of Daofuses humanity could amass.

And this wasn't even the limit of the myriad races. Reinforcements might arrive at any time to back those Daofuses up. They would never win against such a force.

With a look of despair, Stable Army Marquis said, "We can't do it. It's not that we don't want to, Shadow, but we can't. It's hopeless. You know how things are. If we fight rashly, we will only end up throwing our lives away without accomplishing anything." 

Instead of replying, Shadow Marquis looked at Su Yu.

Su Yu smiled and asked, "Why are you looking at me? Three pseudo kings and twenty Daofuses. I'm not a Master of Law. What can I do about a force this powerful?" 

It was indeed a hopeless situation. This wasn't even a lie. If he were already strong enough to defeat three pseudo kings and twenty Daofuses, he would probably only need several battles...
