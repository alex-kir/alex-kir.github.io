Chapter 1460: Horrified Mo Duona (1) (Teaser)

Sky Sundering Saber!

With the various tricks available to him, Su Yu started accumulating wounds on Zhankui's body.

Was Zhankui strong?

Yes. Stronger than Su Yu.

But Su Yu did not aim to kill Zhankui instantly. Rather, he circled Zhankui while accumulating wounds on Zhankui. Slowly but surely, his blood character gathered Zhankui's blood. One ought to remember that blood essence was something extracted from blood.

Zhankui was a Pseudo Invincible. Due to the backlash of losing his past body, his combat strength had dropped to the level of a peak ninth-stage Sunmoon. However, that did not change the fact that he was still a Pseudo Invincible. Thus, his blood essence would be the blood essence of a Pseudo Invincible.

Su Yu was slower, but he had too many tricks. Without using the river of time, Zhankui would be faster, but not fast enough to completely evade his attacks.

Su Yu had also been using the slow character to slow Zhankui down, the suppress character to suppress the space around Zhankui, and the vibrate character to generate tremors all around Zhankui.

All his characters were doing their job. This wasn't enough to kill Zhankui....
