Chapter 1845: Carnage (3) (Teaser)

Once again, Tiangu and the others looked over. They saw that the Divine Realm had been quickly sealed. An incomparably powerful being appeared above the realm. A woman. An ancient woman. Not a Daofuse of a previous epoch. Rather, she was an ancient being.

The old woman looked at the old turtle and said with a sigh, "The junior of my house was rude. Please accept my apologies, Brother Grand Dream. I believe...I have one last fight in me. Please do withdraw, Brother Grand Dream." 

When the old turtle saw the old woman, his jaw dropped as he said, "You're...not dead! How...unbelievable."

"I am merely struggling at death's door. I can't even leave my realm anymore. It is embarrassing to let you see me like this."

It seemed as though the old woman was struggling to even speak, but the old turtle was staring at her with a frown. After a while, he said, "We leave."

Beside him, Soy Bun complained, "Just attack! Why are we scared of her?"

Grand Dream was rendered speechless. Did Soy Bun not know her?

"That's Divine Pseudo Emperor's dao companion."

"Jiwu's wife?"

"The ancient Divine Pseudo Emperor."

The moment Soy Bun heard that, he dragged Flatbun away unhesitatingly.

Clearly, that name was enough to scare him away.

Dao companion of the ancient Divine Pseudo Emperor.

She was still alive?

The old turtle stared for a long while before saying with a sigh, "I see. You have been staying alive through the divine emperor dao. No wonder you're unwilling to fight. So be it. But know that Jiwu will die sooner or later." 

The old turtle decided to withdraw. It was possible that the old woman couldn't leave, but what if she could? Perhaps she could come out for one last fight before dying. She couldn't stay alive for long outside after losing the support of the dao.
