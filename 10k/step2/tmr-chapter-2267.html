Chapter 2267: Unwilling To Fall Off (1) (Teaser)

Everyone left. They all had a heavy mission to complete.

They had to become Masters of Laws before the day of departure. Those named would need to abandon their daos if they couldn't do it. This served as both pressure and motivation for them.

These people had benefited massively since following Su Yu. Apart from the beginning when Su Yu was still weak, barely anyone had died fighting for him. The last time he lost anyone was during the battle against the Celestial Chasm Realm.

As Su Yu grew in strength, people stopped dying. Even those who had self-detonated had all been brought back by him. But now, he seemed to have run out of his generosity.

It wasn't that he would send anyone to death, but those incapable of carrying their own weight would need to make way for others. After all, he still had many people under him forced to cultivate the daos they were unfamiliar with.

Sure, dao sharing was an alternative. But Su Yu's world was still too weak to produce significant powerhouses in such a situation.
