Outside Line Gorge.

Heavenly Fate Marquis' remained expressionless, but inwardly, his heart skipped a beat. It was found!

"Is this the Emperor Yu mentioned by Endless Passage?"

Naturally, that would also prove that they weren't worthy of a change in his stance. Since his little tracker had been discovered, it served as proof that these people were actually quite capable and cautious.

Beside him, Venerable Moon asked, "What are you thinking about, Fellow Daoist Heavenly Fate?"

"Nothing much. I just can't resist wondering if we will lose even more old friends in the coming war."

Venerable Moon merely smiled without saying anything to that.
