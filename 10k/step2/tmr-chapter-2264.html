In fact, the chaos dragon was already strong enough to be considered a tier-five Master of Law. He only lacked an opportunity to take a step forward.

He needed something like the removal of the restriction for Venerables. Just as Su Yu was done digesting his recent gains, the aura of the chaos dragon changed drastically.

Boom!

A large amount of chaos power converged around the chaos dragon.

Su Yu looked over.

The chaos dragon was a part of the so-called ancient chaos race. Breakthrough was seemingly impossible for them outside the gate. In that case, what did he require for a breakthrough?

Curious, Su Yu observed with his Heaven Gate.  He wanted to take a good look at the breakthrough process. Just why was the chaos will so important?
