Chapter 2183: Chaotic Battle, Chaotic Slaughter (2) (Teaser)

As the six Venerables attacked Purple Cloud Marquis, Su Yu laughed heartily. "I dislike those who enjoy hiding for a long time before emerging to reverse the situation. Do you understand? Who are you learning from? Are you dreaming of reversing the situation? Kill her!"

Countless attacks rained down on Purple Cloud Marquis. Under such a bombardment, she couldn't even self-detonate. Then again, that had never crossed her mind. Suddenly...Boom! Her body was blasted apart.

Instantly, chains, ropes, pen, and all sorts of sealing daos manifested from Su Yu's hands to suppress her sea of willpower.

Under the desperate gaze of the devils, Su Yu pointed his pen forward.

Boom!

A dao trembled as a crack rang out. A hole appeared in the dao, and power started leaking. Pulling his pen back, Su Yu threw a punch, blasting the sea of willpower apart.

A storm raged in the sky.

The death could be felt even in the Lower Realm. The myriad daos shook, as though the sky was collapsing. This was a being who could truly advance into a Master of Law right after the restriction was removed. She had only emerged from her seclusion for...thirty seconds. And then, she was blasted apart and killed by Su Yu's group.
