Chapter 1283: Can't Afford To Offend (1) (Teaser)

Xia Huyou and his entourage left just as suddenly as they had arrived. Meanwhile, more and more people were gathered outside the city. Not long after Xia Huyou's departure, an ancient city arrived.

It wasn't an ancient city Su Yu was familiar with. In fact, it wasn't even one of the ancient cities that were here previously. It was known as Openmountain City. It landed right in front of Grandstar City. On its walls, numerous fierce armored guards could be seen.

Above the mayor's residence, a silhouette manifested as a voice rang out, "Openmountain Holy City is here. Mayor Su, care for a meeting?" 

***

Grandstar City.

With a frown, Su Yu appeared above his mayor's residence and said, "Mayor of Openmountain City, please come in for the discussion—"

"Hahaha, I'm afraid I won't be entering Grandstar City."

The mayor said, "Everyone knows about Mayor Su's habit of locking the city. Why don't you come to my city instead? Openmountain City has never been locked before. Everyone knows this." 

Su Yu furrowed his brows. Clearly, this mayor was here to create trouble. Was this a way to put him in place before the assembly began? This was understandable. There was no reason for the other mayors to obey him....
