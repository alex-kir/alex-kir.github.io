Chapter 2145: Catch Heavenly Overseer, Life Dao Advancement (3) (Teaser)

After a short silence, Tiangu chuckled. "So be it. We shall see."

He was the actual ruler of the immortal race. However, he had stayed in the Lower Realm for too long. Some of the people in the Upper Realm had grown far stronger than before. They might not be willing to obey him anymore. Even after breaking through into a heavenly king, that might still not be enough for him to hold power in the Upper Realm.

Thus, moving to the Upper Realm was actually something he didn't want to do unless absolutely necessary.

"So be it." Tiangu said, "The dao in the Upper Realm is loose. I will be able to grow faster up there."

And then, he left.

There was no guarantee that he would be able to return in the future. Thus, he wanted to take a good look at the realm he had ruled for a hundred thousand years before leaving. Things might be calm if Su Yu weren't going to the Upper Realm as well, but that wasn't the case. And was Su Yu the type to sit silent and do nothing?

It was likely that the Upper Realm would be thrown into chaos...
