Chapter 1533: Grand Cycle Apertures (2) (Teaser)

After a while, Su Yu finally saw a black hole in front of him. It was floating below the Celestial River of Death. Some weak death spirits were roaming blankly in the vicinity.

He Tu pointed at the black hole and said, "That's the black hole. Previously, a whirlpool formed in it out of nowhere. When I came to check out of curiosity, I was transferred toward the mansion entrances." 

He looked at Dummy with an odd expression and said, "This guy seemed familiar with the way. Was he here before?"

Su Yu was surprised to hear that. Was Xia Chen here before? No way, right?

"Senior Xia Chen, were you here before?"

Dummy stared at the black hole blankly. After a long while, he said, "I...know...know this...place..."

Then, death qi covered his eyes, removing the tiny bit of clarity he had. Su Yu felt very helpless. This was annoying. Dummy did not have the ability to explain anything clearly. He didn't know what to say anymore.

So be it. He decided to give up and focus on the 360 apertures for now. He wasted no time and soared into the sky after a short pause.

He Tu and Xing Yue followed behind him.
