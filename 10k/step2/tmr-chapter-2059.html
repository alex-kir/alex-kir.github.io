Chapter 2059: Great Human Migration (1) (Teaser)

Human Realm.

Things were getting turbulent.

The biggest news in recent days was Su Yu's withdrawal to the Death Realm and the return of the human lord from six thousand years ago.

A large number of people started gathering in several predetermined points.

Great Xia.

A tired group was heading toward the Starfall Mountain. Some were flying, and some were walking. Many traveled with their entire families.

A group of people landed from the sky.

Looking at the elderly who were moving with their entire families, Xia Huyou focused on an old man in the group.

"Old Man."

Blocking the path of the family with a genial smile, Xia Huyou said, "Are you heading to Starfall Mountain?"

First, the old man looked at Xia Huyou. Then, he looked at the ones around Xia Huyou. Finally, his expression changed as he said, "I greet the lord prefect!"

Then, he attempted to kneel down.

Xia Huyou hurriedly stopped him and said, "Be at ease, Old Man. There is no need for this as a fellow citizen of Great Xia."

He asked, "Are you heading to Starfall Mountain?"

"Yes." The old man answered, "People are saying that Emperor Yu is heading to the Death Realm, right? There is an announcement that we can gather at Starfall Mountain if we wish to go with Emperor Yu, so we're here."
