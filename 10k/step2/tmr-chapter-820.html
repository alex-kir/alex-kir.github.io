Chapter 820: Xia Huyou's Request (3) (Teaser)

5th of June.

Great Xia Cultural Research Academy.

Today, the myriad race students would arrive at the academy. A lot of people were present to enjoy the bustle. Su Yu was also there, mixed among the crowd. The myriad race students had yet to arrive. He also saw a familiar face in the crowd.

He wanted to ignore that fellow, but alas, that person was too astute to simply dismiss. That person walked up to him and started a conversation, leaving him with no choice but to reply.

"Brother Cui!" Liu Hong had a wide smile on his face, "Are you here to see the myriad race students as well? Since you're not familiar with this place, do you want me to show you the way?" 

"Who are you?"

"Brother Cui, have you forgotten me? Back then, we had some interactions with each other. I'm Liu Hong. Do you remember me?"

Su Yu babbled a random reply, "Oh, yeah. Brother Liu. A few years ago, we visited the Red Pleasure House together. Are you the guy who promised to pay the bill and went back on his words?"

Liu Hong laughed awkwardly. Damn this bastard. Did he remember wrongly?

Then again, that wouldn't be too surprising. After all, the two had only met once before. He was only here to build a stronger relationship...
