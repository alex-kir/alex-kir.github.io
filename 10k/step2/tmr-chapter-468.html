Chapter 468: Whack-A-Mole (1) (Teaser)

Su Yu was unaware of what was happening outside the grotto.

After stepping through the grotto, his vision blurred before he arrived at a new place.

Not far away, Xia Chan was looking at him with a frown. The two had been transmitted to the same area. After all, they had entered at the same time. Su Yu flashed her a simple and honest smile.

The willpower in the grotto was dense, but the density level wasn’t too high. Since not everyone had entered, the grotto was not fully activated yet. The willpower he was seeing was merely what the grotto had accumulated passively.

Ignoring Xia Chan, Su Yu started looking around. The place was quite big. At the very least, it was big enough that he couldn't see the end of the grotto with his naked eyes.

Apart from Xia Chan, he saw nobody in their immediate surroundings. He wondered how big the grotto was. The Source Qi Secret Grotto was quite big. Even after entering so many times, he had only stayed around the Great Strength and Infinite Strength zones.

After looking around for a short while, Su Yu started walking away.
