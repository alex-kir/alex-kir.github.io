Chapter 2127: Su Yu's Accumulation (3) (Teaser)

Su Yu proceeded to ignore the giant. Even after reaching the level of a Venerable, the giant was still not his match. He could afford to focus elsewhere for now. He glanced at the Sealed Region coldly.

After taking in a deep breath, he grinned.

Death Imperator was staying out of the battle. What a surprise.

So be it.

This could have been his chance to kill his first Master of Law. Alas, the opportunity had not arrived.

Su Yu did not mind using up all his gathered trump cards. After all, they existed to be used. Wan Tiansheng was trying to save his trump cards for him, but it was unnecessary.

He took the Token of Sanctification. This would probably be its final usage. But that was enough for him. With a sneer and under the giant's frowning gaze, Su Yu tossed the token toward the Sealed Region.

As it descended from the sky, it enlarged countless times. It grew large enough to blanket the sky itself.

A saintly aura rippled out.
