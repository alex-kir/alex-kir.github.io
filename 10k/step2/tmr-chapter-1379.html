Chapter 1379: Aromatic Blood (1) (Teaser)

Inside the boundless void, hand after hand appeared and shot toward the forming tribulation.

They had to scatter the power of the laws forming this tribulation. Otherwise, Empyrean Cloud would die, the guardians would flip out, and the Death Realm would plunge the world into chaos.

Inside her city, Empyrean Cloud roared furiously and stared at the sky with indignance. 

Why? Why could others hit her yet she couldn't hit back?

She had guarded the world of the living for a hundred thousand years. For an ancient duty, she had served loyally, living like the dead.

Why was she deserving of punishment?

She could not accept this.

She roared furiously, "Boss, don't interfere. Let me take a good look at the laws. Do they kill me? I have served the Myriad Realms for a hundred thousand years. I have rendered great service to all life. I am no sinner!" 

She was no sinner.

She could not accept this. She was unsatisfied.

The old turtle ignored her. The laws were emotionless. They wouldn't care.

Numerous palms struck the tribulation. Rumbles rang out.

The world changed color. Space collapsed.

The tribulation resisted, growing in strength as the power of the laws continued pouring...
