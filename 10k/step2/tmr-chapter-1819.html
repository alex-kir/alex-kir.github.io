Chapter 1819: Bitter Fighting (2) (Teaser)

Su Yu looked at Cangshan Nether and coldly said, "You heard that. If you win, you might live. If you lose, you won't even have a corpse left."

After thinking about it, Cangshan Nether asked, "Where do you want me to go?"

"Luminous Domain Mansion."

"Luminous Domain Mansion?"

He didn't know that place.

Su Yu explained, "The palace of the ancient humans. It is now an abandoned palace. Of course, there is no escaping that place without me. You won't be able to find the exit. You can try if you doubt me." 

In life, Cangshan Nether was a supreme expert. Thus, he didn't take long to reach a decision, "Fine. I will accept the offer."

Staying here would guarantee death. No new experts were awakening from the river anymore. Even those who had awakened no longer dared to show themselves. At this rate, he would die. He wouldn't even get the chance to drag someone down before dying.

This was unacceptable. He would rather take the gamble. The mansion entrance wasn't too far away.

With the deal made, Su Yu started flying toward the entrance with the others behind him. The old turtle and South Heavenly King were in charge of watching over Cangshan Nether on their way there.
