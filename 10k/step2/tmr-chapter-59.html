10th of June.

Early in the morning, Chen Hao arrived at Su Yu's house in a rush. The moment Su Yu opened the door, he hastily said, "Yu, something happened!"

"Speak slowly."

Furthermore, he did not have much money left. With only around thirty thousand dollars, he had to be mindful and save enough for his expenses in the academy.
