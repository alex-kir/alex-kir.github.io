Chapter 1918: Chain Reaction (1) (Teaser)

Six dragon Daofuses were dead, numerous dragons were wiped out, and multiple dragon cities were flattened. News about this started spreading all over the Upper Realm. Everyone was shocked. Numerous people panicked.

Even a powerful race like the dragon race had suffered so badly. What about the other races? Apart from the divines, devils, and immortals, every other race was in danger.

To the south. 

Divine Fire Mountain.

Bajue's expression changed.
