Chapter 1376: Don't Offend Women (1) (Teaser)

Su Yu could feel his danger sense slowly fading away.

He did not dare to think too much as he was afraid that his thoughts would be sensed by someone. He didn't even dare to think of the fact that he was Su Yu. Like everyone else, he was fully focused on the distant gate.

It was massive. White-jade in color, it looked slightly transparent.

Luminous Domain (Xing Yu).

Was that a name? Or was that a title?

This mansion seemed to have some connections to the Human Emperor. Or to be precise, this could very well be the location of the so-called Myriad Race Assembly. According to the old turtle, the time projections of the ancient experts could be found on the ninth level.

The white-jade gate was pulsing slightly, as though it was breathing. Each time it pulsed, a burst of source qi would ripple out in all directions. It felt as though the gate was alive.

"How beautiful."

Those were not Su Yu's words. Rather, they were the words of a beautiful humanoid fish nearby. The humanoid fish had a pleasant voice, but one should refrain from looking at the head of the fish as that would ruin the beautiful image left by the voice...
