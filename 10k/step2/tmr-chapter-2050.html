Chapter 2050: I Wish To Pioneer A Dao (1) (Teaser)

Fusing and extracting the pen dao.

Finally, severing the dao, using the moment the dao snapped to extract even more from the dao.

Su Yu was having a lot of fun. But this was the kind of fun only he could enjoy. Everyone else would be stuck at the first step: fusing with the pen dao.

In any case, this wasn't something anyone would dare to do with their own daos, as that might result in the collapse of their daos. It was simply too risky.

To the side, Wan Tiansheng and Heavenbridge Marquis watched in silence. After a while, Heavenbridge Marquis said, "Emperor Yu, should we return? I can't last here any longer."

Only then did Su Yu depart the pen dao with a wide smile on his face.

The Mark of Civilization had gained a new page.

The arrow page.

He narrowed his eyes at Heavenbridge Marquis and said, "Ordinary strength, but great material. That is why you can survive so long in the river. I'm very curious. What were you made of? Who made you? Were you forged? Or were you born from nature?"

He had never cared about this before. But today, he was starting to grow...
