Chapter 1842: Three Marquises Fall (3) (Teaser)

Far away.

Immortal Realm.

The entire realm trembled violently. Tiangu, who was in the middle of giving Soy Bun a beating, blanked out slightly.

He was so distracted that he didn't even react when Soy Bun bit off one of his fingers. He pulled back in a hurry before turning to look at the Immortal Realm. Above the realm, a massive silhouette appeared before falling from the sky.

Natural disasters started erupting all over the Immortal Realm, killing countless immortals. Tiangu glanced at Talisman King by instinct, but Talisman King was still fighting Flatbun not far away. Even then, Tiangu was still blanking out. Talisman King was alive.

He wasn't dead.

Then...

Finally, Tiangu looked in Fate Realm's direction. He couldn't see the ongoing battle, but he saw the crimson clouds and the blood rain. He saw that burst of source qi. He saw the crimson beam. He saw the phenomenon of a dead expert.

He was completely stunned. He couldn't control himself. The battle had barely started. It might feel like a long time had passed, but some people were still on their way to join the various battlefields. All in all, the battle had only lasted twenty seconds thus far.

And then...a top marquis of the immortal race seemed to have perished. It wasn't Talisman King. It wasn't Tiangu...
