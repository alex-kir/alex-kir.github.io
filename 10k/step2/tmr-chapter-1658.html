Chapter 1658: The Crowning Of Su Yu, The Attack Of Celestial Chasm (1) (Teaser)

Nanyuan.

Mayor's residence.

The large square was already filled with distinguished guests. Each of them was an expert of respectable strength.

They came from both the myriad races and the various human prefectures.

Up in the sky, numerous Invincibles appeared.

Great Ming King and the others had arrived.

Just as Su Yu was about to greet them, Great Xia King gave him a cold stare before sending him a voice transmission, "Don't move!"

"..."

Su Yu rolled his eyes. Why was that guy being so serious? Was he sulking after learning about Great Zhou King's plan? Had Great Xia King forgotten about how he had kindly kept silent about the king's glorious past? How could Great Xia King talk to him like this?

Su Yu decided to be generous and forgive Great Xia King. It was normal for one to sulk after being lied to by Great Zhou King.

As he studied Great Zhou King and the other Invincibles in the sky, he could see that their auras felt completely normal, as though they were all really here. Their auras were rather subdued, but that was normal as no Invincible would run around blasting their aura all day.

After a while, he removed his gaze...
