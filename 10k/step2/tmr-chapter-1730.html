Chapter 1730: Battle Of Lanshan (3) (Teaser)

Humans.

As she fought, a set of armor appeared on Marquis Lanshan's body. Martial King had personally bestowed that set of armor upon her after her death.

Alive, she was a human marquis. Dead, she would dominate the dead.

That was the meaning behind that gift. According to Martial King, all of existence shall be the Human Realm.

As a human, everything he could see shall be his.

Everywhere he could reach shall belong to humanity.

Starting from the Human Realm, they swept through the various realms and defeated countless powerful races. A hundred thousand years had passed. The time to fight another war had arrived.

"Kill with me!"

Marquis Lanshan roared. 

With her sword, she faced three marquises alone, displaying boundless determination. Her fighting spirit instantly crushed the momentum of the three powerful marquises. These were all the marquises of the myriad races. And they were all the victims of the ancient humans.

And now, all of them were shaken by this familiar scene. Nearby, even more human death spirits ignited their death imprints as the battle grew intense.
