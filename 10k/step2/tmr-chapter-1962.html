Chapter 1962: Dao Painting (3) (Teaser)

For Su Yu, this painting was merely convenient to have around. After all, he had the Heaven Gate to show him the true nature of the myriad daos. But the painting was invaluable for everyone else. In fact, it was valuable enough to drive even Masters of Laws crazy. It contained Human Emperor's understanding of the myriad daos.

That was perhaps the third person to have ever created a world. The insight of such a person was invaluable. But just as Su Yu was about to take the painting down, he blanked out. His outstretched hand stopped moving.

An odd expression appeared on his face. Suddenly, he drew the little stone out. The stone was glowing faintly.

With a chuckle, Su Yu said, "I thought this painting here could be freely taken away since it was left on the wall, but no. There is a test."

"..."

Everyone was rendered speechless. It could be freely taken away just because it was left...
