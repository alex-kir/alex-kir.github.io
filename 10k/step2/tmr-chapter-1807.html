Chapter 1807: So Easy (2) (Teaser)

As they moved, faint traces of light swirled around Su Yu. That was not due to his anger. Rather, that was merely the power of laws.

He was dispersing the weaker power of laws in the area to strengthen the grasp of the human lord's token over this place. With this, those from his side would have an easier time fighting in the Death Realm.

"You are growing stronger and stronger, Your Majesty." Marquis Lanshan praised with admiration. She could feel that Su Yu had grown much stronger than before. 

Furthermore, Su Yu was feeling more and more like Culture King...no, at the same time, he was feeling more and more unlike Culture King. This was a very complicated sensation.

In the past, Su Yu greatly resembled Culture King. And now, he resembled Culture King even more. But at closer scrutiny, he would look completely different. His very disposition had taken a turn toward a different direction, one that was different from Culture King's disposition.

He felt more overbearing than Culture King, and less gentle than Culture King. Before long, the Celestial River of Death was visible before them. The gigantic river spanned across the Death Realm in a circle. But at a closer look, that wasn't the case. When Su Yu looked up, he had a feeling that the river was more like a tent than a circle.
