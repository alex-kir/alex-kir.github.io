Chapter 1541: Spirit Suppression General (1) (Teaser)

Heavendoom was completely dumbstruck to see Su Yu in his city.

Just a moment ago, he sensed something off about the visiting Grandstar. As a super expert, he could easily sense that something wasn't right. He was about to smash the impostor into mincemeat before the impostor spoke. But upon noticing that it was Su Yu, Heavendoom grew even angrier.

"Impudent! How dare you lie to me?"

Heavendoom suppressed the shock he was feeling and decided to give Su Yu a punch first. And thus, he threw a punch. After focusing on the incoming punch for a bit, Su Yu smiled and answered with a punch as well.

Naturally, Heavendoom was not using all his strength. He wasn't really trying to kill Su Yu. Thus, he merely used the strength of a Pseudo Invincible to test Su Yu. This was also a good opportunity for him to calm his nerves by giving someone else a beating.

The fists collided.

Boom!

Both fists faded away after the clash.

Heavendoom blanked out slightly before throwing another punch, "Impudent! There is indeed something wrong with you!"

How was this guy so strong?

This was definitely not Su Yu! Well...this might be Su Yu. But so what? Heavendoom had decided to give Su Yu a beating. Nothing...
