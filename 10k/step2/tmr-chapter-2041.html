Chapter 2041: Withdrawal (1) (Teaser)

Su Yu was in a great mood. Nobody understood why.

Regardless of whether it was Hundred Battle King, the chaos lineage, or Martial Emperor, all of them were humans. A victory for any of them would be a victory for the human race. He only needed to know that. He could finally remove the shackles on himself.

He only needed to prevent the victory of the myriad races. Humanity was no longer his to worry about. No matter what their intentions and goals were, they would not erase the regular humans. Not even Martial Emperor had said something like that.

He might have claimed that he did not care about humanity, but as a human lord, how could he not care? He had been living in caution and fear. The burden of humanity was too heavy. He had been living in constant fear that a single mistake would result in the extermination of humanity.

But today, he had shrugged that burden off. He no longer needed to worry about all that. The regular humans would be allowed to survive as long as they did not resist the new ruler. Recreating a new humanity? Easier said than done. With an existing humanity to rule, why would they bother with recreating an entire race?
