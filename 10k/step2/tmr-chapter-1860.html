Chapter 1860: Pacifying The Phoenix Realm (3) (Teaser)

Phoenix Realm.

The Phoenix Emperor Palace was a massive palace built atop a gigantic parasol tree. At the moment, the Phoenix Pseudo Emperor was feeling jittery and uneasy. That sensation terrified her.

She did not know if this was a result of the recent deaths. In any case, she hadn't been able to stay calm since the deaths of Nether Pseudo Emperor and the others. She was not the only one who was feeling jittery. The Eternals in the palace were feeling the same.

One could say that the myriad races had lost the battle in the Lower Realm. The epoch had started with an overwhelming advantage to their side, yet they lost. How laughable.

All in all, eleven Daofuses had died.

Even so, there were still twelve Daofuses among the myriad races. On paper, they were still capable of putting up a fight against humanity. But everyone knew that it would never work. Because the immortals, divines, and devils were not ready to fight the final battle.

After all, these were the races with more options available. In that case, why would they throw caution to the wind and fight humanity to the death? As far as they were concerned, humanity would be defeated the moment the Upper Realm opened.
