Chapter 1831: Fate Realm (1) (Teaser)

Su Yu's feelings aside, Old Wan was indeed showcasing the air of a grandmaster right now.

He seemed boundlessly talented. Countless Wan Tianshengs were reading. He had only lived for a hundred years, yet his life experience seemed even richer than that of someone who had lived ten thousand years. He seemed like a refined, scholarly gentleman. Perhaps this was the real Wan Tiansheng.

Su Yu also had the refined air of a scholar, but his was more fake. He had only spent ten or so years studying, and he had indeed learned a lot from books. But he could replicate Wan Tiansheng's scholarly aura.

Wan Tiansheng's dao pioneering had taught Su Yu some new things. Initially, he had thought that dao pioneering was simply a process of opening a tributary by force. But no. It was a process of expanding one's dao through one's knowledge and understanding. Brute force alone was not sufficient to expand a tributary.

As Su Yu observed, the human dao continued growing rapidly. Naturally, it was not as big as some of the more impressive daos Su Yu had seen. But this was a completely original dao, one Wan Tiansheng had created himself....
