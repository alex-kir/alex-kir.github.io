Chapter 1164: Meeting The Elder (2) (Teaser)

That day, numerous secret meetings were held.

That day, super experts started carefully going through the realm entrances to the Human Realm that were not under humanity's control. The realm entrance leading to the Allheaven Prefecture was the largest realm entrance, but that did not mean that it was the sole realm entrance.

The Myriad Race Cult had always been able to smuggle experts in and out of the Human Realm, and these unknown realm entrances were what they had been using.

Of course, it wasn't completely safe to use these realm entrances. For example, the entrance under the Heavenly Hunt Pavilion's control could easily deliver one right in front of a human Invincible. Some of the other realm entrances were filled with spatial cracks, making it an excessively dangerous experience to travel through them.

Because of the numerous issues, these alternative realm entrances were only suitable for smaller groups of people.

Naturally, none of that was something for Su Yu to worry about.

He was only in charge of stirring trouble, not taking care of the consequences. At the moment, he was busy meeting some higher-ups of the pavilion. These people were actual higher-ups of significance instead of some white masks.

They were none other than the third and eighth elders. The two top experts had arrived...
