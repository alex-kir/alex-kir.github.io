Chapter 2092: Come Kill Me (1) (Teaser)

"Everyone is helping me. Those worthy of the dao will receive numerous help. How can I fail? How can I fail?" 

This was usually how people started shackling themselves down.

Su Yu was no saint. He was not selfless. He was selfish. He once thought of living only for himself, allowing nothing to restrain him.

Dao pioneering?

After failing once, he could always try again in the future. He could take his time. He was in no rush. He could find some place to seclude himself for ten thousand or so years and not leave before he succeeded in his dao pioneering.

Pen dao? Human Emperor's dao? Those were powerful daos, but they weren't his. Culture King and Human Emperor could easily take those daos away from him in the future. All his hard work on those daos would be rendered pointless. He would essentially be cultivating those daos for others for free. 

That was his true thought. He did not mind the dao he cultivated so long as it was powerful enough. But he knew full well that those daos could never be truly his. They also weren't the most compatible daos for him. He would be at the mercy of others after cultivating those daos. They were too restrictive.

And of the two daos, he was most opposed to inheriting...
