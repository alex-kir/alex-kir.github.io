Nobody could stop him.

Then, instead of capturing his past or future body, he focused on the river for a bit before giggling, "I'll show you the proper dao of the myriad races. My various bodies have their own past memories. I will capture all of them in the past before fusing them with my present. That will turn all of us into one, past and present. With that, we will all be the same in the future as well."

He was trying to capture the past of others before fusing them with him. That was absolute madness. Capturing a past wasn't an easy feat. And the past of these bodies did not belong to Lan Tian.
