Chapter 1990: Mutual Suffering (1) (Teaser)

Were the Torchbearers still around? Nobody could say for sure. As for the recent attackers, they were clearly from the chaos race. But who could be sure that the Torchbearers hadn't been involved in the recent incidents?

Venerable Desolate and Venerable Nether had returned to the nether territory. Both looked gloomy and cold.

They naturally saw the characters hanging in the air. Venerable Nether gritted his teeth and said, "Chaos race!"

Venerable Scrape nodded. "But I am suspicious about something else. The ones who had attacked the Dao Origin Land...were they really from the chaos race?"

Nobody said anything.

Venerable Scrape continued, "During the battle in the Dao Origin Land, one of their pseudo kings died before our eyes. Her name was Zi Yan. And there were some rumors about her floating around. Is everyone here aware of those rumors?"

"Yes. It is said that she has been in the Lower Realm since the previous epoch."

"In that case, how did she die in the Dao Origin Land?"

The myriad race experts started worrying. A single chaos race was already annoying enough to handle. And now, they had to worry about the Torchbearers as well.  

Sacred Source Marquis said, "Things aren't looking that good in the Lower Realm either. Devilroil Marquis and Fallen Star Marquis are both dead. Tiangu and the others have been very silent recently. If I didn't know for sure that he's not dead, I would have thought that the Lower Realm has fallen."

"Things were going so well. Why did all this happen?"

Venerable Moon frowned. Things were indeed going well for them. They had defeated humanity during the ninth epoch....
