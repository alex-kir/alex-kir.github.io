Chapter 1682: Man, Dog, Plant, Book, Ball (1) (Teaser)

There was a guard dog inside the Culture King's Residence.

That was not an insult.

There was indeed a dog there. More importantly, the dog was unwilling to leave. Was the dog not bored of staying in here for so many years?

Su Yu racked his brain.

He said, "Senior, Culture King and Time Sage told you to watch over their home. But the Human Realm is the root of their home. Without the Human Realm, their home will no longer be a home. Do you want them to return one day to see all their neighbors turning into enemies?"  

The white dog said, "I must watch over the house!"

He was a guard dog. He couldn't leave. His master wasn't around. His little master wasn't around as well. He couldn't leave. What if the flowers all wilted away while he was away?

Su Yu felt very helpless. He couldn't convince the white dog.

"Why don't you discuss this with the tea plant and book spirit?"

Su Yu felt like this dog might be too stubborn. Ignoring the tea plant, the book spirit should be smarter as a book written by Culture King, right?

Su Yu said, "Senior, you might not know this, but a subordinate of Culture King has betrayed him. Do you know this? There is a traitor!" 

"Traitor?" The white dog asked, "Who?"

"Heavenly Overseer Marquis..."

"I don't know that person."

"The luck spirit. Do you know the luck spirit?"

The white dog nodded, "Yes!"
