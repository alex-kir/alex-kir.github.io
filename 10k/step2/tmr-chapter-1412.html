While everyone on the first level was fleeing to the second level madly.

Su Yu was still moving leisurely. He was not worried that the treasures on the level would all be harvested by these fleeing people. Some of the treasures in the mansion were not things just anyone could harvest.  

After all, the Luminous Domain Mansion had been around for countless years. If these treasures could be harvested so easily, they would have been harvested long ago. Some treasures could only be harvested by the fated ones or the smart ones.

Su Yu was confident he was the smart one. Or to be precise, he was the fated one. After all, he believed that one had to open at least 360 acupoints to become the fated one, right? As far as he was concerned, one would be nothing here without opening the grand cycle acupoints.

The Luminous Domain Mansion belonged to the Human Emperor. The builder was most definitely a human. Thus, some of the treasures here were only available for humans. This was yet another legacy from their ancestors. The other races would not be able to steal these treasures from the humans. The humans only needed to have the ability to harvest these treasures.
