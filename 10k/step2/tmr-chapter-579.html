Chapter 579: Arrival at the Starfall Mountain (3) (Teaser)

After taking his leave, Su Yu walked into his old neighborhood. Under the dark sky, he kept to himself and walked silently. Before long, he reached his home. A moldy smell welcomed him when he opened the door.

A lot of dust had accumulated in the house after being empty for almost half a year. With a burst of source qi, Su Yu cleared all the dust. He sat down on the couch and sank into thought. A short while later, someone knocked on the door.

He calmly said, "Enter."

"Am I not welcomed, Student Su?"

Someone laughed at the other side of the door. Telling a guest to enter without opening the door was quite a rude thing to do.

"You're welcome here. I'm simply too lazy to move."

The person outside laughed. The door swung open and an old man walked in. With a smile, he said, "Do I need to introduce myself?"

Su Yu stood up and said, "No. Greetings, Sir."

This was probably the commander of the Secret Regiment...
