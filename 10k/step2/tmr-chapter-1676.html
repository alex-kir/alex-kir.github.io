Chapter 1676: For The Future (1) (Teaser)

Even after looking around repeatedly, all Su Yu could see were singles. They still had a considerable number of Invincibles, but the combat strength they could muster had probably dropped by half.

But for now, Su Yu had something else to focus on. After consuming so much blood and flesh, his Mark of Civilization was in a state of rapid growth. Before long, the 142nd golden stripe appeared on it.

Its rapid growth was understandable since it had consumed so many Invincibles in one day. It was now a peak heaven weapon. Even the incomplete stripes were increasing as well. Including the incomplete stripes, the total number of stripes reached 169.

This signified that with enough replenishment, the Mark of Civilization would be guaranteed to reach no less than 169 golden stripes in the future. That would make it a high divine weapon. The growth of the Mark of Civilization would naturally be helpful for Su Yu.

As his book grew, his culture and radiance characters grew as well. Inside his sea of willpower, his willpower was also increasing in quality. After the intense battle, his 360 apertures had completed their fusion into nine apertures, signifying that his willpower cultivation had reached the peak of the Mountainsea Realm. He was only a step away from the Sunmoon Realm.

Even his body felt like it was on the verge of advancing and transforming further. It was showing signs of finally stepping into the Mountainsea...
