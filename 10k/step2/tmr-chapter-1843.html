Chapter 1843: Carnage (1) (Teaser)

Three ancient marquises had died. The sky was collapsing.

The Daofuses of the various races were fleeing for their lives.

Beside Su Yu, June, Hou Pseudo Emperor, and the others were feeling incomparably excited. They had never fought a smoother war than this before. It felt great. They couldn't believe this. The strength of Great Qin King and Great Xia King also caused them much excitement, but they were also feeling a slight sense of fear.

Su Yu seemed to have a never-ending supply of trump cards. It was as though he would never run out of trump cards. Who could have guessed that Great Qin King and Great Xia King were already Daofuses? There had been zero signs of this.

Su Yu's life force was surging after killing three ancient marquises. He glanced around. Everyone was fleeing. However, some were still heading toward the Human Realm. When he focused, it was only one fool. The Nether Pseudo Emperor. He could understand what the Nether Pseudo Emperor was doing.

Su Yu decided not to return and waited in Nether Pseudo Emperor's way back home. If he were to return, the Nether Pseudo Emperor would be able to move around them to escape.

"He's already dead. Just ignore him."

Su Yu did not pay the Nether Pseudo Emperor much...
