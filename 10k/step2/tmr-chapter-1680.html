Neither Culture King nor Time Sage had given him the mission to save humanity. He was merely a guard dog. Meant to guard their home.

Su Yu felt quite helpless. Even if he could get this dog to help, a single Eternal wouldn't be too helpful. Not even a ninth-degree Eternal would be enough to reverse the situation for humanity.

After forcefully calming himself down somewhat, Su Yu said, "Senior, did Culture King and Time Sage leave any treasures behind? Something capable of killing Daofuses? If there are, can I borrow these treasures for a bit?"

"Something capable of killing Daofuses?" The white dog shook his head and said, "The master and little master left no treasures behind. This is only a home. This is a place to eat, sleep, and read. There are no treasures here."
