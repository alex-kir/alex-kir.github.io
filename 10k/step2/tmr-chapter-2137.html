Chapter 2137: Peak Combat Strength (1) (Teaser)

The series of resurrections ended with Xing Yue. Su Yu returned through the hole. Before long, he was back in the Death Realm. The turbulent Celestial River of Death started calming down.

The Master of the Perished was nowhere to be seen. Perhaps he would return again in the future when someone else attempted to steal from the river again. The power balance in the Death Realm had changed completely.

North Heavenly King was dead. Dragonblood Marquis was dead. Fourteen top powerhouses of the Sealed Region were dead. Many of the powerful death spirits hiding in the river had either died or joined Su Yu.

Su Yu's world had grown significantly. Thanks to all the new people in his world, his strength had grown massively, and his dao had matured considerably. Xing Yue had left with Su Yu's life and death daos, but the two daos ultimately originated from Su Yu's world. Thus, his world would continue growing by association alongside her. There were pros and cons to this.

If Xing Yue were to die, the balance within Su Yu's world would be broken. The entire world might suffer from it. But she could also grow the two daos, growing his world in an indirect manner.
