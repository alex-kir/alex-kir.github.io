Chapter 2095: Dao Completion (1) (Teaser)

Darkness.

Boundless darkness.

Even the soul itself was engulfed in stillness. To experience death. This was an easy claim to make, but nobody could maintain their ability to think after death.

But Su Yu was still capable of thought. He felt himself looking at a gigantic dao. The death spirit dao. It was trying to bring him back, but he was in the middle of chaos. The influence of the death spirit dao did not extend that far.

Was this death?

Su Yu saw that dao. But the dao couldn't approach him. He was too far for the death spirit dao to reach. A pity.

What now?

He could feel his mind slowing down.

As though he was slowly losing his ability to think.

"What's the point?"

Even in such a state, Su Yu was still able to berate himself. What was the point of this? Why did he insist on pioneering his dao? Why did he insist on experiencing death? Were his daily deaths during his childhood not enough? In each of those deaths, he would return to life after waking up.

Right, he only needed to wake up to return to life. But how was he supposed to wake up from this?

"Life and death daos...dao pioneering? What dao should I pioneer?"
