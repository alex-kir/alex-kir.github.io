Two heavenly kings had been killed at the very start. Things were progressing smoothly.

They were slowly forming an encirclement around the four.

"Hahaha!"

Hundred Battle King had a feeling that the day for him to regain his freedom had arrived.

He roared madly, venting all his accumulated anger, "Kill them all! Kill one or two more and I'll be free! Kill these bastards!"  

After six thousand years of waiting, hope had finally arrived. These people were incredibly strong. There were even two pseudo kings among them. Thus, Hundred Battle King was quite surprised to see them.
