Chapter 1535: Return (1) (Teaser)

Shining clouds were appearing in the sky.

One after another.

"Grand cycle stars. The Human Emperor sends his blessings."

Su Yu opened his eyes and stared at the sky speechlessly. Was this something set up by a lazy subordinate of the Human Emperor? Previously, he had received a similar message after opening all his acupoints. Why was there only a small change in the message? It felt as though there was a template for all these messages.

Nearby, both He Tu and Xing Yue were blanking out as they stared at the sky. He Tu muttered, "Can the heavenly blessings really reach the Death Realm as well?"

He had never seen this before. However, Su Yu was already used to this. Since the blessings this time were related to his apertures, the rewards would either be willpower, characters, or power of laws.

Generally, he could only harvest some power of laws when a tribulation and a reward appeared at the same time. Most of the time, it wouldn't be easy to get some. The clouds started dropping upon Su Yu.
