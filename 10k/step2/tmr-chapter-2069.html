Chapter 2069: Shivering (2) (Teaser)

There was no denying that Liu Wenyan had implicated the entire Liu Family for the sake of protecting Ye Batian's legacy. Wan Tiansheng decided to lay the blame on Great Zhou King instead.

Right at that moment, Great Zhou King hurriedly vanished with a flicker. He could sense the malice directed toward him. He felt quite wronged. It wasn't like he had deliberately targeted Liu Wenyan back then. In fact, he had mostly ignored the matter. Yes, yes, that in itself was probably wrong as well. But intent mattered, right? He never intended to target Liu Wenyan.

It wouldn't be fair to punish him for non-participation, right? That was already an old matter. They should have forgotten about it long ago. Wan Tiansheng stopped looking at Liu Wenyan. There was nothing he could do about it. Instead, he looked at the various old guardians.

His eyes lit up as he shouted, "Guardians, try to reach the Daofuse Realm!"

All the old guardians had accumulated enough comprehension to reach the Daofuse Realm. Many of them had been ninth-stage Eternals for many years.

Of the thirty-six old guardians, five had withdrawn into retirement. Some were still living in the various ancient cities, and some were enjoying their retirement in the various minor realms. As for the remaining guardians, only ten had reached the Daofuse Realm. The rest were still stuck.

This could be their chance to make the breakthrough....
