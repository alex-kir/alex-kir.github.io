Chapter 1908: Chaos Power (3) (Teaser)

Su Yu and the researchers ignored the brutes and continued their research.

This wasn't a type of research any regular person could perform. Very few people had the ability to differentiate the differences between similar powers. For many, fire was simply fire. These were mostly people with a low level of dao comprehension.

Fortunately, Su Yu had gathered several individuals who were good at this around him.

Su Yu was also working on the chaos power endlessly, and he had discovered that the chaos power was mainly composed of several major powers.

Five elements, yin and yang, life and death. All were powers Su Yu had comprehended before. These concepts were the foundation for his domain character. Thus, he was able to sense these powers much more clearly than the others.

Of course, there were also many other minor powers mixed within the chaos power.

Fortunately, Su Yu had also experienced all sorts of powers while stealing from the many daos of the River of Time. That experience proved helpful now when he needed to identify all the powers in the chaos power.
