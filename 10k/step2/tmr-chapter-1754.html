Su Yu's face fell. What was this guy saying? Was he such a person?

He ignored the little furball and said, "Let's go. We have harvested enough today. I have collected over a hundred pieces of furniture from Culture King's place. I can't stay any longer in the level two area. It's time to plunder the other areas instead. We can leave the mansion after gathering a thousand or so carrier treasures."

He had essentially advanced massively in his path to financial freedom here. Both level two and one areas were incredibly dangerous. He did not have the courage to visit the other three Cardinal Human King residences since he was unrelated to them. It was simply too risky. After gaining so much, he had to pursue stability. It would be too big a loss if something bad were to happen due to his greed.
