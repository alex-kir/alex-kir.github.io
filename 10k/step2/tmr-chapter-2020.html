Heavenly Measure was gone.

But not before giving Su Yu a knowing smile.

Clearly, he was mentioning Endless Passage and September to show that he was aware of Su Yu's group in the Upper Realm. Since Endless Passage had returned to the fate race, what about September?

***

Su Yu maintained the same expression. So what if they knew? What could they do if he insisted on feigning ignorance? Soon, he tossed the matter to the back of his mind. 

He would be cooperating with the myriad races for now. At worst, he would withdraw upon exposure. With the chaos lineage around, the myriad races would not be willing to sacrifice too much to take him down.

***

Once again, he returned to the iron devourers.

They had settled down on the third level.

The three major races had settled on the second level. In truth, the second level was better for only one race as it would be too cramped otherwise. And it wasn't like there weren't better locations other than the second level. But none of them was willing to take a lower level when one of them was taking a higher level. That would give the other races the impression that the race at the higher level was superior to the others.

And thus, all three of them had decided to settle down on the second level.
