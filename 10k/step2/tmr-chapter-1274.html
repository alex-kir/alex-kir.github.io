Chapter 1274: Eve Of Alliance (1) (Teaser)

The Myriad Realms were at peace.

In the proceeding month after the mass dao affirmations, numerous battles had erupted among the various races. However, not a single one of them involved those at the Mountainsea Realm and beyond.

Sunmoons became exceedingly rare while Invincibles had all but disappeared. But as time passed, things started getting busy in the Allheaven Battlefield again.

***

"Have you heard? Su Yu has called for an assembly of the ancient cities. It is happening in a few days." 

"Yeah."

"The Luminous Domain Mansion will open soon. The ancient cities wish to get a share as well. But I doubt uniting the ancient cities will be easy. Don't forget that some of the mayors are actually backed by relatively powerful races."

Each ancient city mayor was considered the overlord of a region.

It was only natural that such a position would catch the eye of the myriad races. Of the thirty-five mayors, some were backed by these races. In truth, even Mayor Tian He was not entirely independent. He had a slight connection to the human race. Or at the very least, he had a connection to Xia Longwu. The myriad races wanted to see how Su Yu would bring them...
