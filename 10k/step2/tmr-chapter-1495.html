Chapter 1495: Kill An Invincible (3) (Teaser)

Su Yu waited.

He was creating the perfect opportunity to act.

Just then, an attack struck Frost Divine King, causing the entire Illusory Heaven Mirror to shake. Even those whose six senses were sealed were jolted awake. Some opened their eyes blankly while some exclaimed in alarm.

With a fearful expression, Su Yu yelled, "What is that?"

Everyone followed his gaze and noted a spatial crack not far away. It looked as though the Illusory Heaven Mirror was on the verge of shattering.

Yu Sheng was greatly alarmed.

The crack wasn't too far away from Su Yu. Noticing the crack, Yu Sheng flew over and said, "Calm down! This is a small matter. I'll deal with it..."

He could see that the crack wasn't too big. He was sure he could handle it. Zhan Wushuang was just about to fly over as well before the look in his eyes changed.

Danger.

A vague sense of crisis enveloped him, causing his entire scalp to turn numb. The sensation wasn't too strong, but it was clearly there. Something dangerous was awaiting him.

"Lord..."

Yu Sheng looked back upon hearing Zhan Wushuang's roar....
