Chapter 1759: Changes (2) (Teaser)

"So what instructions do you have for me?"

The old turtle appeared uninterested. After all, they were not on the same side. Both were among the oldest beings in existence, but that did not mean that they were friends.

"No, I dare not instruct you." Heavenly Overseer Marquis said, "I only want to know if Su Yu has entered Culture King's Residence."

"I don't know."

"Are you unwilling to tell me even this?" With a bitter tone, Heavenly Overseer Marquis said, "Not long ago, I saw something similar to the dog Culture King raised back then. Brother Grand Dream, is Culture King really dead? If he's not dead, why hasn't he returned? Things wouldn't have developed to this point if he had returned earlier. Also...Su Yu...is he...Culture King?"

Fear flickered in his eyes as he said, "Why are there so many belongings of Culture King in his hands? Culture Tomb, the white dog, and even you are standing on his side. The iron devourers are supporting him as well." 

He desperately needed an answer. Was Su Yu Culture King or not? Looking at the fearful expression on Heavenly Overseer Marquis' face, the old turtle frowned. He knew that he would be able to sink Heavenly Overseer Marquis...
