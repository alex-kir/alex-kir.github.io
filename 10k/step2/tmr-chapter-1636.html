Chapter 1636: Don't Laugh, Must Resist! (3) (Teaser)

Ten years. Twenty years. Thirty years...

As they traversed the river, they found that Silk Destroying King's life had indeed been rather monotonous. He had spent most his life either fighting or traveling to another fight, with a majority of them being fights with the Silkwyrm King.

And finally, they went over fifty years.

A massive wave appeared ahead of them.

When they entered the wave...

Su Yu and Great Zhou King were rendered speechless. It was yet another fight with the Silkwyrm King.

Yes. During the major battle over fifty years ago, Silk Destroying King was fighting the Silkwyrm King. Was he not bored of fighting only one person for so many years?

Su Yu suspected that Silk Destroying King's true love was the Silkwyrm King, not Lan Tian.

They had no choice but to keep going ahead. Eventually, they surpassed a hundred years. A majority of the waves were the same: they were filled with Silk Destroying King's battles with the Silkwyrm King. Eventually, even Great Zhou King was starting to sweat from exhaustion. He asked, "Nothing suspicious in a hundred years. Is this enough to prove his innocence?"

Apart from Silk Destroying King's battles with the Silkwyrm King, the most intense wave contained his memory with Lan Tian.

His past wouldn't be so clean if he was really the traitor.

Su Yu frowned and said, "We continue. I think I see a much bigger wave ahead of us..." 

Great Zhou King said while gasping for breath, "I can't last much longer. If you want to go further, you'll help instead."

Su Yu did not protest.

"Lord Flat, can you help us stabilize the river?"

"Again? Do you have any food?" 

"You can eat Silk Destroying King if he's the traitor."

"Okay!"
