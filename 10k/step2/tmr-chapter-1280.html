Chapter 1280: To Be Shameless Is To Be Invincible (1) (Teaser)

Grandstar City.

People were still streaming into the city.

Everyone was terrified that Su Yu would seal the city and kill everyone again, but there were very few safe locations one could stop and rest these days. Apart from Grandstar City, the only alternative was Red Clouds Island, the island under clearbright bird race's control.

But that race was too weak. Thus, nobody was confident in their ability to enforce peace in their city.

Meanwhile, it had been a month since Su Yu was last sighted in Grandstar City. The city had not been sealed during that time. Additionally, those from the major races were also trying to spy on him. Thus, people had been entering the city nonstop.

As for the nine realms in the vicinity, they had been quite lucky. Of the six new minor race Invincibles, one of them came from one of the nine realms. That one advancement elevated the said race into a relatively powerful race. A large number of people from the nine realms could be seen in the city as well. Some were even considering rebuilding their island.

But they were still worried so they decided to send a representative to discuss the matter with Su Yu. No matter how safe an ancient city could be, it wasn't a place one could stay for more than three days. Having an island in the area would make things easier for ever...
