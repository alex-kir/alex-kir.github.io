Chapter 1503: Tai Shan (2) (Teaser)

Su Yu was left quite speechless.

He could only say that the department head had truly gone mad.

Everyone had been labeling him, Lan Tian, and Wan Tiansheng lunatics. But this human from the first epoch was also mad. Sure enough, Old Wan was right. At times, the three bodies would be in conflict with each other.

This was most likely the situation the department head was in. Did he blame the department head for this? Not really. A master and a race. That was indeed a hard choice. For example, Su Yu's teachers were Liu Wenyan and Bai Feng. If they told him to kill Great Qin King, what would he do? His teachers and his race. How should he choose? 

Su Yu was sure he would struggle with the decision as well. However, he had never expected to see a supreme expert struggle with himself to such an extent. He found this sad, and he felt helpless.

That was such a powerful human. His assistance would be enough to deal a major damage to the myriad race experts here. But he also rejoiced that this was a human. That was why the department head had struggled to obey Heavenly Overseer Marquis' order to kill him.

Suddenly, He Tu asked, "What is your name?"

"Tian Yue."

He Tu blanked out...
