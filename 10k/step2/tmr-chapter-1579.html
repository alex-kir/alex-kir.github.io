Chapter 1579: Catalog Page (1) (Teaser)

When Su Yu left the Death Realm, night had arrived yet again.

The crimson clouds in the sky were starting to disperse. The world did not seem to care even after the deaths of so many Invincibles. No matter how strong one was, one would be reduced to nothing after death. Grandstar had also returned to his usual position.

Suddenly, Senior Wang called out from outside the hall, "Mayor."

"Enter."

Shortly after, Senior Wang entered and said, "Mayor, the West Spire head of the Heavenly Hunt Pavilion is requesting an audience with you."

"West Spire head?"

Su Yu raised his brow. He had thought that the West Spire head would either leave or return to the Human Realm. Surprisingly, he was here.

"Invite him into the city."

The West Spire head was a top expert as well. He was a seventh-degree Eternal. During the previous battle, he had stopped the South Chamber head, saving Su Yu and Wan Tiansheng the effort of handling yet another seventh-degree Eternal. At the time, it was unlikely that they would be able to handle another opponent...
