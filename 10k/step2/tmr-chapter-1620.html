Chapter 1620: Su Yu The Narcissist (3) (Teaser)

Great Ming King said, "I'll be in charge of building the foundation. Silk Destroying will imbue the power of time onto the formation. Heavenly Seal will add the power of sealing into the formation. Tianyuan, you will control the power of time in the formation."

"Sure."

The Invincibles got ready. Instantly, the area was sealed, forming a secluded space independent from the rest of the world. Numerous jade talismans were tossed out by Great Ming King to serve as the foundation of the formation. As for Su Yu, he tossed a book out. Countless projections emerged from the book.

"Seniors, allow me to trouble you to use the book as the base and forge a formation with ten thousand foundations."

"Cough, cough, cough!"

Great Ming King nearly choked on his breath. The others had odd expressions as well.

Was this a joke?

Su Yu smiled and said, "Thank you. A formation forged this way will be a lot more compatible with my book." 

Great Ming King said, "The success rate will drop considerably if we do this."

Su Yu said, "I know. But...I only need you to try your best. You will get what I promised even at the event of failure. At most, we'll redo everything. I will pay you the same price for the second attempt."

They gasped in shock.

Fuck!

It sure was nice being rich! Was this for real? The likelihood of failing at the first attempt was extremely high. Fine, fine. Since Su Yu was willing to pay for everything, so be it.

Boom!
