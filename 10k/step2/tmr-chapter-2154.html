Chapter 2154: Upper Realm Again (3) (Teaser)

A short moment later, they reached the tunnel of the Torchbearers.

Looking at the tunnel that had appeared from a wave of Su Yu's hand, everyone was shocked. Was he not keeping this tunnel secret anymore? Wasn't this the same as exposing the location of this tunnel to the myriad races of the Upper Realm.

Even Thunderstorm was frowning. This did not feel appropriate for him, but he was not in the position to say anything. However, his mood was solemn. Su Yu had exposed this tunnel so easily.

Tiangu and the others grew even more worried at the sight of the tunnel. With the exposure of this tunnel, the tunnel in the Fate Realm woul decline in importance. Would Su Yu silence them after showing them this tunnel?

"Giant Axe and March will go first. Pacify all threats."

The two looked at each other before stepping into the tunnel. Just as Thunderstorm was about to follow, Su Yu said, "There is no need to trouble you for this, Venerable Thunderstorm. You need to remain and protect the others. How are we supposed to explain ourselves to the myriad races if these people die in the process of traversing the tunnel?"

Thunderstorm had no choice...
