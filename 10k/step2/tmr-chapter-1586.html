Chapter 1586: I Understand Yet Again! (2) (Teaser)

Su Yu asked, "What is the name of this method?"

"Weapon Fusion Method." Grandstar explained, "Forge a powerful weapon and become one with it."

Su Yu said, "This reminds me of someone."

"Zhao Li?" Wan Tiansheng nodded, "Are you thinking of him?"

"Correct." Su Yu smiled, "Him."

Grandstar, who had seen Zhao Li before, nodded and said, "Yes. He seems to be walking a similar path. That was one of the mainstream cultivation methods in the ancient past." 

Su Yu asked, "Lord, do Eternals need to follow specific power of laws during their cultivation?"

After some hesitation, Grandstar answered, "I can't answer that question well as my cultivation is not high enough. But I can say that most of your guesses are right. The process of Daofuse might really be the process of increasing one's compatibility with a certain dao. But you need to ask the boss to be certain. He should know the answer."

Grandstar looked at them with complicated expressions. There was not one Eternal among them, yet they were discussing the process of Daofuse and transcendence here. Grandstar felt lost. He didn't know what to think.

Was this how cultivation was supposed to be? Wasn't one supposed to only worry about the cultivation level one was in? Why were these people who weren't even Eternals exploring the possibilities of Daofuses...
