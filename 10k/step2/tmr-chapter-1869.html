Chapter 1869: Hierarchy Of Contempt (3) (Teaser)

Su Yu had only discovered this realm by following the dao. Otherwise, it would be much harder for him to discover a realm so weak. The race ruling this realm resembled gorillas, and they had also developed a crude civilization.

Ruins from the previous or earlier epochs could be found all over the realm. This must have been a prosperous realm once upon a time. However, the original rulers had gone extinct, and these gorillas rose to become the new rulers of this realm. It was unknown if these gorillas were a new race or an existing race that had existed even when the previous rulers were around.

Naturally, Su Yu wasn't too interested in the history of this realm. Perhaps the same thing would happen in the Human Realm if the humans were to go extinct. A new race would rise, ruling the realm unaware of its past glory.

Civilization would restart from scratch. The discovery of some ancient ruins might accelerate the development of this new civilization, pushing it into an era of cultivation.
