Chapter 1907: Chaos Power (2) (Teaser)

"What do you think?" Su Yu thought about it and said, "Forget it. For now, I need to learn more about our neighbors. When I'm doing scouting, we can proceed with our research. I'll try to add a fake chaos dao to my Mark of Civilization and see if it works."

He could forge a new page and use it to bear the chaos dao temporarily. It would be even better if he could use a weaker beast for research. Or perhaps he could even devour a weak beast with his Mark of Civilization and learn directly from the memories of the beast.

Killing a stronger beast such as a Daofuse would create too many fluctuations. But a mere Sunmoon beast wouldn't pose such an issue, right? With Su Yu's strength, he wouldn't even need to utilize his dao to kill a regular ninth-degree Eternal.

At the moment, Su Yu was feeling quite regretful. He was regretful that his teachers, such as Bai Feng and the others, were too weak. Their comprehension of the dao was too weak as well. Otherwise, they would be the best for conducting such research. It wouldn't even take them long to figure out the composition of the chaos dao.

Everyone specialized in something. Su Yu was strong. He was also good at a lot...
