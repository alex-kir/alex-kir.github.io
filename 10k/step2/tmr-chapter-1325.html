Red Clouds Island.

Day after day passed.

For the myriad races, a month was not long. 

For one month, numerous people arrived at Red Clouds Island, including the humans. Nobody rejected the invitation. The Myriad Realms was still a world ruled by multiple major races. As strong as the humans were, they would not receive any slot if they were arrogant enough not to join the assembly.

Humans, divines, devils, immortals...

The buildings erected by these major races were incomparably large. Also as large were the buildings erected by the major organizations of the Allheaven Battlefield, such as the Thousand Region Alliance, Heavenly Hunt Pavilion, and the Ancient City Alliance.
