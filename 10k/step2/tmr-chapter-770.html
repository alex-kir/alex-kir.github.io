Beilian City.

This was a large city of Great Ming near Starfall Mountain. After going past the Starfall Mountain and traveling a few hundred kilometers, one would reach Nanyuan City of Great Xia.

Teahouses and restaurants were always the places where rumors and information were exchanged. Great Ming did not lack storytellers. Even with the existence of televisions, this form of entertainment was still popular in Great Ming. After all, the shows in television were boring and repetitive. Meanwhile, these storytellers were much more interesting.

In Great Ming, storytellers shouldered a heavy responsibility as well: dissemination of gossip.
