Chapter 1455: Genius and The Lunatic (2) (Teaser)

Dao Cheng's expression changed drastically. Mo Duona? Why would Mo Duona kill Pan Hu and Fourth Heaven?

Taihe cursed furiously, "Audacious! How dare he kill Pan Hu?"

Su Yu said, "He's probably not dead. I didn't notice any falling moons. He has probably been captured. Are they trying to negotiate with us?" 

He looked at Taihe nervously and said, "Martial Uncle, this...I didn't want to flee. But I thought it would be important for someone to inform the two of you about this as well. I did not flee to save myself!"

He was showing an extreme level of fear. Fleeing from battle was not acceptable regardless of race.

Taihe had an unsightly expression, but Dao Cheng consoled him, "It's fine. You wouldn't be able to help much against a Pseudo Invincible anyway. You did the right thing."

Su Yu said, "No, I'm not that smart. But I never intended to give up on Senior Brother Pan Hu. I only fled because that was the only choice. I was worried that you would fall into Mo Duona's trap if you didn't learn about this!"

Taihe didn't even know what to say anymore. Why was this guy making so many excuses? He fled because he was scared.  That was obvious.

However, Taihe merely frowned and said nothing. There were fewer and fewer of their people in the mansion anymore. It was pointless to punish Ling Heng. In any case, Dao Cheng was right....
