Chapter 1965: Ninth Level Gains (3) (Teaser)

Su Yu was still working on the book.

His hammer dropped repeatedly.

Attaching the dao painting to the Mark of Civilization was not an easy task. Unlike the past, he was no longer hammering with brute strength alone. Rather, each hammer strike carried the power and his comprehension of a different dao.

The painting corresponded to Human Emperor's understanding of the myriad daos. The hammer strikes corresponded to Su Yu's understanding of the myriad daos. He had traversed the River of Time before. He had stolen from the myriad daos before.

He also had his own interpretation of the myriad daos.

A fiery hammer strike. A hammer strike wrapped in a miniature gale. A hammer strike rippling with spatial undulations. A rapidly vibrating hammer strike.

The tempering continued.

As Su Yu hammered his comprehension into the book, the dao painting slowly became one with the book. Great Zhou King and the others listened to the rhythm of the hammer-strikes in silence.
