Chapter 1575: First Close Encounter With Xia Chen (3) (Teaser)

"Senior Xia, do you know the cultivation method of the 360 apertures?"

Xia Chen smiled and said, "Don't you know the location of that cultivation method? You might have missed it this time, but you only need to wait ten years." 

"Ten years? I'm better off deriving it myself."

He sounded very arrogant.

Xia Chen said nothing about that. Perhaps Su Yu could really do so. Of course, the premise of success was surviving the process itself. At worst, he could activate the formation after ten years. At the time, he might have grown enough to do so without external help.

Suddenly, Su Yu lost all interest in Xia Chen. This guy did not seem to know too much. He had not received a proper answer for any of his more important questions.

Having guessed Su Yu's thoughts, Wan Tiansheng was left quite speechless. After a short pause, he said, "Senior Xia, is there nothing left from the previous epochs in the Human Realm?"

"Hmm..." Xia Chen thought about it...
