Chapter 2051: I Wish To Pioneer A Dao (2) (Teaser)

Grand Dream smiled and said, "Soy Bun, give it a try. At times, we can easily confuse our own daos. Previously, I believed that my dao is defensive. Clearly, I was wrong."

It turned out that his dao companion was an aggressive female turtle. Thus, the dao he inherited from her was actually an offensive dao.

Soy Bun was still confused, but he didn't mind giving it a try. Instantly, his dao power erupted. Grand Dream did not resist, allowing the dao power to envelop him. At a speed visible to the naked eye, Grand Dream started turning younger.

Su Yu paid close attention to everything that was happening. He noticed that Grand Dream was indeed looking younger, but his strength remained the same. And after some time, Grand Dream actually dropped from a heavenly king to a tier-two Daofuse.

At that point, Soy Bun was already gasping in exhaustion. He said, "See! I told you I can reverse time! Look! The old turtle is dropping in strength!"

He withdrew his dao power. After a while, the effect on Grand Dream faded, returning him to his peak strength.
