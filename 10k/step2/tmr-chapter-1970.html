Chapter 1970: Catching Up With Martial Emperor (2) (Teaser)

Seventh level.

Martial Emperor was still floating in the sky in his willpower body. Not far away, a gate appeared and a familiar figure stepped out of it.

Martial Emperor's expression changed.

Su Yu!

What a terrifying speed of growth. It hadn't even been that long yet Su Yu's grasp over the pen dao had reached a rather high level.

"Been a while, Martial Emperor."

A while? No?

Martial Emperor said nothing. It hadn't been that long. They last clashed on the day of Lan Tian's dao pioneering. At the time, he had suffered a minor defeat. Su Yu was simply too overbearing. He had smashed the token down without bothering to say anything.

One could say that Martial Emperor had witnessed Su Yu's rapid growth with his own eyes. Su Yu had first entered the mansion about a year ago. At the time, even ninth-stage Sunmoons were challenging opponents for him. But now, he had grown to the level of a pseudo king.

Back then, Su Yu had claimed that he wouldn't fear a free Martial Emperor. Naturally, Martial Emperor viewed that as a stupid claim by a clown.

But after seeing Su Yu again, Martial Emperor could no longer take that claim lightly. He couldn't help but wonder if Su Yu would really grow enough to contend against him by the time...
