Chapter 1669: Endless Slaughter (3) (Teaser)

Cheng Mo of Great Tang said loudly, "Holy Lord, we won't be of much help staying here. Those Mountainseas and Sunmoons are scattering everywhere. Please give your order and allow us to slaughter these enemies!"

Su Yu looked at them, exhaled, and asked, "Do you understand why you're all gathered here today?"

The look in their eyes changed slightly.

"Great Qin King and the others believed that with me here, I can protect all of you. That way, if your fathers were to die out there, you would still be around..."

Yes. That was what Great Zhou King and the others had intended. They wanted Su Yu here for protection. To protect these people.

These were all descendants of Invincibles. Outside, the Invincibles were fighting with their lives on the line. Some had perished. After losing some to Wan Tiansheng's slaughter, these Invincibles did not have too many descendants left. Great Qin King and Great Zhou King did not want to see the lineages of these Invincibles ending here after all they had done for humanity over the years.

The Great Yue prefect roared furiously, "Holy Lord, please allow me to fight! My father has died fighting! I don't want to stay here like a piece of trash! I want to avenge my father!"

Su Yu solemnly said, "If you stay here, I can keep you alive..."

"I don't need that!"

The Great Yue prefect gnashed his teeth so hard that blood started dripping down his lips. Then, he knelt down and said, "Please issue your orders, Holy Lord! Great Yue is willing to fight!"

Su Yu exhaled and said, "Fine. Go. Kill those Sunmoons. I'll await your good news." 

"Yes!"

With a roar, the Great Yue prefect flew away. Behind him, the Great Yue experts at the Mountainsea Realm and above hesitated slightly before flying away as well. Nobody had expected there to be so many hidden enemies within the Human Realm.
