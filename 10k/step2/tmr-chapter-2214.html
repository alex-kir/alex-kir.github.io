Su Yu laughed again. "Did I? I recall you asking for it from me. You said that with such an appointment, you can throw your weight around when you meet the future generation after my death. But I don't recall agreeing to your request."

Heavenbridge Marquis was left speechless. Was that so? But why did he feel like Su Yu had agreed at the time?

One of them must have remembered wrongly. Su Yu was unbothered as he said, "Sure. I'll assume I did make you a promise. But you need to be more reliable and talk less. Otherwise, I will appoint you the Shit King."

Heavenbridge Marquis stared blankly. Was there a need for such pettiness?

That was such a terrible title!

"I will definitely talk less!"

Heavenbridge Marquis nodded solemnly as he reminded himself to say less in the future. In any case, he didn't have much he wanted to say right now. Su Yu chuckled and started moving toward the chaos. He still had a lot of things to do. He was very busy.

Hundred Battle King sure was lucky to have so much free time. Su Yu started lamenting his tiring life.
