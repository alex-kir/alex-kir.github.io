Chapter 1329: Fight For The Slots (2) (Teaser)

The white-haired divine king said, "Of course the agreement still counts. With the myriad races serving as the witnesses, how can we renege on our words?" 

The dragon king said, "Previously, we promised 200 additional slots to the human race. In that case, only 610 slots are available for allocation?" 

Only 610 slots for the rest of the races. Any race with an Invincible would be able to get a few slots just by being here.

Moge said indifferently, "That's your problem to solve. The human race will get the 200 slots we promised. As for how many more they can get, it's up to them."

Finally, Great Zhou King spoke, "This isn't very fair."

Moge coldly said, "And how do you propose we make it fair?"

Great Zhou King said, "I think it's better to allocate the slots based on the number of Eternals. Each race can show all their Eternals. These Eternals don't even need to be here personally. A mere projection will do. Then, we can split the slots accordingly."

That was not acceptable.
