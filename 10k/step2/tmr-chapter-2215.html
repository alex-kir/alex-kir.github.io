Chapter 2215: Set Off (1) (Teaser)

Su Yu had his plan against the myriad races.

Naturally, the myriad races might not act according to his plan. Thus, he had two choices in his plan. First, eliminate them. Second, expel the wolf to swallow the tiger. If people like Venerable Moon still led the myriad races, Su Yu would most likely pick the first option.

But at present, Tiangu and Consort Emeritus were running things. With them around, the second option was possible. It wasn't that these people were stupid. Rather, they knew Su Yu too well. They knew just how terrible an opponent he could be.

The old fossils were not able to stop thinking that Su Yu was still a youth. He wasn't a Master of Law. They had seen countless Masters of Laws with their own eyes. Su Yu was nothing special. His rapid growth was not indicative of what he could be in the future. 

But people like Tiangu had suffered too many losses in Su Yu's hands.  They had personally witnessed how Su Yu had grown from a Skysoar to his current level in the blink of an eye. Even when he was still a Skysoar, he could already survive Sunmoons and Eternals. It was as though he was omnipotent.

Deep inside, they were terrified of Su Yu.
