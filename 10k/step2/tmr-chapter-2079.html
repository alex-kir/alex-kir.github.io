Chapter 2079: Drama King (3) (Teaser)

At the moment, Su Yu was feeling quite relaxed.

Inside the River of Time.

Holding onto the weakened Hou Pseudo Emperor, he asked, "Wasn't that fun?"

Hou Pseudo Emperor felt like crying. Sure, sure.

It was so fun he nearly died.

For a moment there, he even thought that Su Yu was really going to kill him. Fortunately, he survived.

"Thank you for your favor, Emperor Yu."

This whole performance was done for the sake of the Upper Realm hous.

Su Yu smiled. "It's no big deal. I'm only doing what's right. I will try my best to overturn the Upper Realm before the myriad races learn that you're still around."

Hou Pseudo Emperor said, "I'm confident you can do that, Emperor Yu."

It felt as though Su Yu was capable of anything. Any casual gesture of his was seemingly capable of upending heaven and earth.

For example, he had killed a Daofuse before summoning a bunch of death spirit experts to force the myriad races to remove all their collected death qi. If Su Yu hadn't faked Hou Pseudo Emperor's death, the threat might not have worked against the myriad races.

With a smile, Su Yu brought Hou Pseudo Emperor back to the dao they found earlier. Then, he waited outside as Hou Pseudo Emperor entered the tributary.

Hou Pseudo Emperor had cultivated the wrong dao for so many years yet he was able to grow...
