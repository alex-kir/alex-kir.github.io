Chapter 2017: Human Mountain Pact (1) (Teaser)

Human Mountain.

Or perhaps this mountain should be called Myriad Race Mountain now. Su Yu was unbothered about the name change. This was the eighth day after the second assembly of the myriad races.  The iron devourer race had arrived after eight days. The mountain was filled with people.

The Upper Realm wasn't as big as the Lower Realm. That wasn't surprising. After all, the Upper Realm was incomplete. It might be known as the Upper Realm, but its laws weren't as complete as the laws of the Lower Realm. Apart from the thicker ambient power of laws, there was nothing good about the Upper Realm.

Most of the inhabitants of the Upper Realm came from the Lower Realm. Initially, only a small number of experts had migrated over. Thus, the number of people in the Upper Realm was still quite low even after a hundred thousand years of reproduction.

That was the case when the various races were separated. But when they came together, the number was actually quite respectable.

Even the races that were extinct in the Lower Realm had shown themselves. There weren't a lot of them, but they still existed in the Upper Realm. Su Yu mused to himself that perhaps the Upper Realm could serve as some sort of endangered species protection land.
