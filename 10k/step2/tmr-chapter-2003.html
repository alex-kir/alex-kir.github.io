Chapter 2003: First Self-Detonation (2) (Teaser)

Su Yu smiled and said, "The question has been asked. We only want to know about the number of humans still in seclusion in the Dao Origin Land. That is all. Are we supposed to ask you about the return of Human Emperor and Culture King instead?" 

The skeletal figure had nothing to say to that.

He sank into thought. After a long while, he said, "There might be some. Back before I entered deep seclusion, there were rumors about someone cultivating deep inside the Dao Origin Land. But at the time, humanity and the myriad races were equal in power. Nobody bothered to search for these hidden experts to avoid rocking the status quo."

In short, he knew nothing.

Just as Su Yu was about to store the skeletal figure in the book again, the skeletal figure said, "Can you allow me to stay here for a bit longer? You can put me away when you're about to fight." 

He wanted to spend more time outside. It had been too long. To the point where the world felt completely alien to him. Su Yu smiled. He was fine with this request. So be it. No matter what, this was a heavenly king. He would also try to help this fellow recover. If that was possible, he only hoped that this would be an obedient expert. That was all.
