Chapter 568: Caught Up (1) (Teaser)

Su Yu’s killing intent had indeed been very intense during the journey. This was an opportunity for him. A chance to fight this much was very rare in the Human Realm. He would need to travel to the Allheaven Battlefield if he wanted to fight and slaughter to his heart's content like this in the future.

Since his enemies were still unsure of his true strength, they would continuously send people his way to test his strength.

After about half an hour of rest, Su Yu announced, "Let's resume our journey."

"I—"

Su Yu ignored the sky horse youth and leaped onto the back of the suanni. Once again, the team set off.
