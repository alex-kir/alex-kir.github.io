Chapter 1861: Four Realms, Pacified (1) (Teaser)

Countless phoenixes were captured.

Fear, anger, and resentment filled the air.

However, there was nothing they could do.

Su Yu did not spare them much look. He only stared at the sky for a bit before saying, "I won't kill all of you. In fact, I have not killed anyone during the entire attack. Killing is never the way to solve problems."

As he said that, he released the seas of willpower of the phoenix Eternals and Pseudo Emperor.

Or to be precise, he slightly loosened the seals on them, allowing them to hear him.

With his saint character active, he flashed them a gentle smile and said, "I am here to end the endless wars of the myriad races. I am not here to kill. I know that talk alone won't do anything. Thus, I can only defeat everyone before talking about peace. If I want to exterminate the phoenix race, this would be the perfect chance for it. But that is not what I want.

"Therefore, I am giving you a choice. I can release an Eternal and have this Eternal supervise the Phoenix Realm. But the other Eternals and your Pseudo Emperor must remain imprisoned. You know very well that all of you will become my enemies again if they are not locked away." 

Great Zhou King and the others wanted to say something, but they hesitated. As for the Eternals and Pseudo Emperor, they were surprised...
