Chapter 2224: It Broke (1) (Teaser)

Rumbling sounds continued ringing out.

Finally, Su Yu was done fusing all the pages into his world. As a kind man, he had naturally left something for Time Sage. He wouldn't consume everything.

"Cook Book."

Yes. He had kindly preserved the cover for Time Sage. He was too kind to devour everything.

When they met in the future, and when Time Sage asked for her cookbook, he would naturally hand the cover with "cook book" written on it to her.

"Time Sage... Radiant Cook Sage?" (Time = Shi Guang, Radiant Cook = Shi Guang as well)

Su Yu blinked. Time Sage was such an impressive name. That was a name he heard from Xing Yue, but back when Xing Yue was alive, Time Sage should still be quite weak, right? Would she call herself the Time Sage when she was still weak?

Perhaps it was Radiant Cook Sage instead? A sage who would eat everything clean until the plate itself sparkled.

Su Yu sank into confusion. So was she the Time Sage or was she the Radiant Cook Sage? The name of the book suggested that she should be the Radiant Cook Sage instead. Then again, this didn't matter.

Su Yu did not think too much about it. If he was still curious in the future, he could always ask her about it.
