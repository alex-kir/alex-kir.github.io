Chapter 2049: Collapsing Mentality (3) (Teaser)

A certain term appeared in Su Yu's mind.

Lan Tian cultivated all life. Old Wan cultivated the expressions of life. But Su Yu himself cultivated something similar as well, especially if he considered the pages of the Mark of Civilization as his clones. He, too, cultivated all life and the expressions of life to assemble his myriad daos.

Both the demonic and the holy existed within his path.

"Sure enough, when all daos are brought together again, the result will be similar."

Just a simple look at the human dao gave Su Yu numerous inspirations.

There were many facets to an entity referred to as a person. Evil. Kindness. Good. Bad.

That was life. What one was as a person depended on how much one tilted toward these facets.

Nobody was a saint at birth.

Even someone like Old Wan harbored evil within him. That was Su Yu's thought as he looked at an evil Wan Tiansheng who was busy bullying the weak.

Then, he saw a kind Wan Tiansheng who was saving all living beings.

He even saw a brutish Wan Tiansheng who was charging forward in a battlefield.

What the fuck?

Su Yu was rendered quite speechless. Was he truly looking at Wan Tiansheng's history? Or were these scenes imagined...
