Chapter 1527: Nice Guy He Tu (2) (Teaser)

Meanwhile, Su Yu was roaming around randomly behind Xing Yue.

As she walked, Xing Yue said, "He Tu might be dead. I think we should return to my kingdom."

"No. I need to look for that place."

"Hmph!"

Xing Yue snorted unhappily. She did not feel like doing that. The Death Realm was so big. How were they supposed to find that place? But without that place, Su Yu would continue absorbing her death qi. That would be annoying as well. She could only hope that He Tu wasn't dead.

Su Yu said nothing else and continued following Xing Yue around. Their surroundings were bleak and empty. He felt as though he was taking a stroll in the cemetery with the repressive atmosphere in the Death Realm.

He had not encountered even one death spirit after entering the Death Realm. According to Xing Yue, the Death Realm should be filled with mindless death spirits.

These were the weaker death spirits. Some had awakened from the Celestial River of Death while some had taken form automatically in the Death Realm. As for the true experts, most were still in slumber, awaiting for the right moment to awaken.

The immediate area near the death tunnel was completely empty....
