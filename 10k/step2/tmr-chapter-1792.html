Chapter 1792: Lower Realm (2) (Teaser)

Five Elemental Realm.

Su Yu was still devouring the crimson clouds in the sky.

He knew that excessive extraction of power from the laws would bring forward the restoration of the connection to the Upper Realm. But what could be more important than the start of his dao fusion?

Shortening ten years to five or even three years? Nothing was as important as his dao fusion. As for the power he might need even after his dao fusion, he couldn't do this anymore. He intended to extract the power inside the Luminous Domain Mansion instead.

The eighth level was filled to the brim with the power of laws. That still wasn't possible with his current strength. Fortunately, the tribulations of the five elemental race was just nice. They weren't too strong, but they weren't too weak either.

If he needed more power after this, he could also have the death spirits invade the world of the living to generate more tribulations. In short, he wouldn't stop milking the heavenly laws. While he was working on his pen dao, he sensed the arrival of a faint power alongside the path of the power of laws.

Su Yu blanked out slightly. Who was this? It was quite an impressive feat. Just as he was about to scatter that newcomer, it approached him, seemingly having sensed his presence....
