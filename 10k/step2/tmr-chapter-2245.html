Chapter 2245: Unworthy (1) (Teaser)

Su Yu was akin to the villain of a show, giving chase to the chosen main character while killing the loyal protectors of the main character. Then again, Su Yu had never viewed himself as a good person. Who could truly call themself a good person in this era? What was a good person, and what was a bad person?

It was merely a matter of perspective. The hero of one party would be the villain of the enemy party. That was all. For people like Longbrow and Evergreen, Su Yu was the greatest villain. Su Yu did not have that much of a hatred toward them. As Longbrow said, he was merely serving his master. Unfortunately, his master was not reliable enough.

After fusing the Book of Time with his world, Su Yu was roughly a tier-three Master of Law inside his world. With the infusion of all those Masters of Laws, Su Yu's strength had skyrocketed to a level near the second tier.

And with the boost from Human Emperor, Su Yu was finally a tier-two Master of Law within his world. Of course, this strength was temporary.  Human Emperor's power was still borrowed power, in the end. Nevertheless, that did not change the fact that at the moment, Su Yu was incredibly powerful.

It wasn't like this was his first time borrowing the strength of others. This was something he had been doing...
