He was further swayed.

But Marquis Xia was telling him he only needed the legitimacy. He could even ignore everything in the Human Realm after getting the position. He could ignore all the responsibilities attached to that position.

Right now, he did not have the courage to kill a human Invincible rashly. That would only trigger a war between him and humanity. But when he became the leader, he would have the authority to do so.

Everyone might be unhappy, but they would need to first dethrone him before doing anything. They would need to get really creative to punish him.

"Marquis, I must admit that you're really good at talking."
