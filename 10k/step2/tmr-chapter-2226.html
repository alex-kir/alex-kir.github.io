"Of the myriad daos, speed is undefeatable. Move faster. Move a tiny bit faster..."

As Su Yu's toying laughter rang out, the two beasts felt the world spinning around them. Countless living beings appeared. Fire elementals, wood elementals, earth elementals… 

The powerful living beings swarmed the two.

"Let there be a nine-winged tiger."

And then, a nine-winged tiger appeared out of thin air. With a roar, the nine-winged tiger charged toward the eight-winged tiger.

"Let there be a two-tailed dragon."

A two-tailed dragon appeared and pounced on the chaos dragon. The two chaos beasts didn't know how to react anymore.

With a rumble, the chaos dragon struck the two-tailed dragon with a tai-whip. Instantly, the two-tailed dragon crumbled apart. The chaos dragon exclaimed with joy, "They only look strong!"

How scary.

Fortunately, these beings only looked strong. They weren't really that strong.
