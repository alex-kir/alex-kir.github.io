Chapter 1716: Iron Devourers (1) (Teaser)

Unlike Xia Longwu, Su Yu was uninterested in playing peacemaker.

As far as he was concerned, the issue between Silk Destroying King and Lan Tian was not something he wanted to get involved with. From what he saw, he found it hard to believe that Silk Destroying King would actually kill Lan Tian.

Instead, the two might rekindle their love upon their next meeting. This was not something outsiders should get involved with.

Furthermore, Su Yu had not left the Human Realm to handle a quarrel between lovers. Rather, he was out here to visit the various Daofuses. During the previous battle, the iron devourers, hous, spatial beasts, primordial giants, and furballs had helped humanity greatly.

They had left after the battle without even collecting the spoils. And they had all suffered to different degrees from the battle. Thus, he had to visit them and further nurture his relationship with them.

He did not know what agreement Great Zhou King had reached with them, but he had to show them that he cared. Great Zhou King's agreement with them didn't matter since he was the human lord here.

It was important that he had a strong relationship with them as well. Even a relationship built by Great Zhou King should be his. That was his approach....
