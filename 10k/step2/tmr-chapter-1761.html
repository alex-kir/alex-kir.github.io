Chapter 1761: Submit (1) (Teaser)

Great Zhou King stood blankly for a long while.

It looked as though Su Yu had really vanished in the River of Time. His traversal was unlike the others, where they would traverse the tributary. Meanwhile, Su Yu was following the flow of the main river before vanishing into the distance. There was a big difference between the two.

The Heaven Gate was open. This was an extremely rare feat since ancient times. And Su Yu had opened it for real. He had seen the true face of the world.

Great Zhou King had mixed feelings about this. He sighed. Su Yu had transformed too much in such a short time. When they forced Su Yu into the position of the human lord previously, both he and Great Qin King had expected Su Yu to remain a nominal leader for now. They did not think that he would be able to do much anytime soon.

In Great Zhou King's opinion, it would be quite difficult for Su Yu to gain true control over humanity. By the time he grew enough to gain control, the connection to the Upper Realm would have been restored.

But now...

Great Zhou King stopped thinking. He looked up at the sky and blanked out again. After a long while, he left. He had a job to do. Su Yu had called for a gathering at the Starfall Mountain. The purpose was unknown.
