Chapter 1917: Chaos Race (3) (Teaser)

Despite his heavy injuries, Stable Army Marquis felt incomparably excited. He couldn't stop staring at Su Yu and the others with fervor.

Ignoring the excited marquis, Su Yu said, "Let's go."

Boom!

With a flicker, all of them vanished.

As they departed, the spatial fluctuations around that area were calmed forcefully.

During the teleportation, Great Zhou King was breathing so heavily that it felt as though his mouth had turned into a fan. His exhaustion was clear for all to see. Instead of teleporting in a straight line, they made a circle and only reached Chaos Mountain after more than ten teleportations.

There, Su Yu said, "You guys can return to Dao Origin Hill first."

"What about you?"

"I'll walk a different path and leave some traces behind."

He gazed into the depths of the mountain range and grinned, "I'll provoke a few ancient beasts. Stop wasting time. Go back first. When the myriad races are here, follow my path and go deeper into the mountain range."

Great Zhou King said, "Be careful."

"Don't worry."

Their cooperation had gone well, so Su Yu's tone was softer when he spoke to Great Zhou King. He started moving into the depths of the mountain range while emitting traces of chaos power from his body.
