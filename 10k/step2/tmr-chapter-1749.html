Chapter 1749: Opening Doors, Harvesting Treasures (1) (Teaser)

Some people would die after several internal explosions. Some people would get numbed after several explosions. Pain was eternal. One only needed to get used to it.

As Su Yu attempted to perfect the cultivation method, he came to realize one thing. The Grand Cycle Method was not something to be taught lightly.

Yes. He had finally understood it. Luck played no role here. The Grand Cycle Method itself refused to be imparted. This was an extremely terrifying cultivation method. Or one could say that it was a perfect cultivation method. A method to unify the acupoints and apertures. 

However, everyone was born imperfect. Even when one opened acupoints and apertures, completion was hard to grasp. After opening all of them to reach completion, the difficulty of fusing the completed acupoints and apertures into one would be unimaginably high. Thus, the Grand Cycle Acupoint Method was hard to obtain. The Grand Cycle Method was also hard to obtain.

Su Yu couldn't help but suspect one thing. Was the Grand Cycle Method really allowed to exist?

Back then, he wrote a cultivation method of 360 acupoints for Bai Feng. Ultimately, the heavens had rejected it as the written cultivation method had eventually been destroyed by a lightning tribulation.
