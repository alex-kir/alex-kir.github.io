Chapter 1735: Schemes Are Useless, Battling East Heavenly King (2) (Teaser)

Seventh level.

The entire seventh level had been turned into a vast flatland. All the buildings had been flattened when Old Zhou raged previously. That included even Devote King's Residence. Not a single plant survived as well. In fact, there wasn't even a blade of grass left after Old Zhou's rampage.

Without any obstructions covering their vision, the death tunnel could be seen right after they emerged from the entrance. The Source Return Blade was no longer there.

However, the tunnel still couldn't be opened randomly. They could feel some power of laws left around the death tunnel. In the past, all the power of law would gather in the Source Return Blade. With the blade missing, the power of laws could only float about near the tunnel. They even saw a death sovereign attempting to enter the mansion through that tunnel.

Su Yu ignored that and shot straight ahead. He needed to check the status of the entrance to the eighth level first. He had to make full preparation to flee at any moment. He intended to wake Old Zhou up the moment East Heavenly King arrived. And he believed that the eighth level would be safe from Old Zhou's wrath.

The headquarters of the assembly and the residences of the various ancient big shots were there. Old Zhou's influence would be quite limited there. It was likely that even at his peak, Old Zhou wouldn't be a match to all those experts during the ancient past.
