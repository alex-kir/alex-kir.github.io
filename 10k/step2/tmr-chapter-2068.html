Chapter 2068: Shivering (1) (Teaser)

Celestial Chasm Realm.

As more and more threads were severed, the powers within the realm were no longer as pure. With the various powers mixed together, absorption was no longer as easy. However, this was never Su Yu's goal. He only needed them to help manage the various powers. Wan Tiansheng's expression was a lot more solemn than before. People were advancing one after another.

But he could no longer pay any attention to them. Suddenly, he roared, "All Eternals, manage the various powers with me!"

At that order, the various Eternals stopped absorbing the dao powers and soared into the sky. This was a task assigned to them by Su Yu. They were here mainly to manage the powers leaking out of the river. Or to be precise, they were meant to keep the various powers orderly so that Su Yu could turn them back into the dao of time upon his return.

"We will handle the five elemental daos!"

Topsoil Spirit volunteered.

"We will handle the spatial dao."

The spatial beasts received their mission as well.

"I'll deal with the devour dao."

One expert after another stepped forth to deal with the various daos.

As for Wan Tiansheng and Lan Tian, they were in charge of managing the overall situation and handling the daos nobody else could...
