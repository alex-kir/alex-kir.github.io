Luminous Domain Mansion.

Eighth level.

The world was shaking. 

The moment Su Yu left the ninth level, he was faced with a large amount of power of laws. He was here before, so he knew how powerful this place was. Ninety-nine projections appeared, while the projection of a human appeared above them all. Ninety-nine beams of light shot toward Su Yu.

"Impudent!"

That roar did not come from Su Yu. Rather, it came from the human projection above them. It was not a real human. It was merely the manifestation of the power of laws. The massive human projection suppressed the ninety-nine beams.

Su Yu looked around. On one side, there was the manifestation of humanity. On the other side, there was the manifestation of the myriad races. He did not waste too much of his time here. He took out two tokens nonchalantly.

His human lord's token and Xing Yu's token.

With the inclusion of the two tokens, the aura of the human projection surged. Initially, the ninety-nine beams were pushing it back. But now, it was able to push back.

This was a clash of wills. A clash of fortune.

Prior to this, Su Yu wouldn't have the ability to participate in it.
