Chapter 2029: Unyielding Torchbearers (1) (Teaser)

The whole guess about Hundred Battle King was only a speculation, but Su Yu made his plans on the assumption that his guess was the truth.

It didn't matter if his guess ended up wrong. A defeat would still be beneficial for him. Before growing further, and before the final battle, a defeat would not necessarily be a bad thing for him. Everyone on his side was getting too confident that he would win forever.

Blind faith was not a good thing. They were so confident in him that they no longer considered the strength of their opponents. They had also stopped wondering if there would be any traps waiting for them. He did not even have anyone capable of correcting him when he was wrong anymore.

Yes, Su Yu was an overbearing person. But that did not mean that he was unwilling to accept meaningful and good opinions. For example, not a single person had warned him about Hundred Battle King after his escape. Not even people like Great Zhou King, Wan Tiansheng, and Lan Tian.

Perhaps they had simply not suspected him, or perhaps they simply didn't care enough about him. As far as they were concerned, they only needed to put their trust in Su Yu.
