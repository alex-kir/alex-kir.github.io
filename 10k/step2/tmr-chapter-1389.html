Chapter 1389: Su Yu's Shocking Discovery (2) (Teaser)

While the heavily injured challengers were withdrawing from the tunnel, Su Yu was facing his second round of challengers. There were nine of them. There was even an immortal and a devil among them.

Su Yu was both surprised and unsurprised to see them. Even among the major races, some people would be excluded during the slot allocation. This was perfectly normal. None of the nine was a weakling, but most of them were only early Cloudbreaches.

Su Yu was unsure if this was because the door had the ability to determine the cultivation levels of the challengers. They weren't surprised to see that someone was already waiting inside.

Some of them knew who Langtu was. A large black bear looked at Langtu coldly and said, "Langtu? Looks like you killed everyone else in the first round. How lucky. I had been curious about the survivor in this door. So it was you?"  

Langtu wasn't weak, but the black bear was confident he was stronger. Of course, the other challengers were no weaklings either. This time, things were a lot calmer. Unlike the previous round, a battle did not break out immediately.
