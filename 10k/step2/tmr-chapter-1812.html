Chapter 1812: Awakening, Advancement (1) (Teaser)

The encirclement of North Heavenly Domain continued.

Su Yu spent his time throwing threats at them, but he didn't attack for real. As time passed, those inside started calming down.

How fortunate.

Clearly, the new human lord was incapable of doing anything to the North Heavenly King for now. His threat to strip the heavenly king's title had not happened. It had clearly failed.

After a while, Su Yu said, "North Heavenly King, I'm going back to the world of the living to spend some time right above you. Leave while you so can or a stomp will come down at any time."

After leaving those words behind, Su Yu vanished.

***

North Heavenly Palace.

North Heavenly King was frowning.

Su Yu was gone. But why couldn't he leave silently? Why did he leave that threat before leaving? Could he really do that?

A devil marquis said, "Heavenly King, he must be going to gather reinforcement. Why don't we kill our way out while he's not around? At worst, we can hide in the Celestial River of Death..."

None of them considered the possibility of victory. Yes, both sides seemed even. Each side had one heavenly king and ten or so marquises. They shouldn't be at a disadvantage. But the sight of that stomp was still clear in their minds. They all knew that they wouldn't have the final victory. At most, they would...
