Nanyuan.

As Su Yu waited, one genius after another arrived. Some were accompanied by their Sunmoon dao protectors, but only the geniuses would enter and sit down. The Sunmoons remained outside the room.

There weren't that many of them. Including Su Yu, there were only thirteen people in the room. The representatives from the immortal, devil, divine, dragon, nether, five elemental, primordial giant, heavenly spirit ape, celestial chasm, and some other races were here.

Each of them had only deployed one Pseudo Invincible into the Human Realm. The Heavenly Hunt Pavilion, on the other hand, had deployed two Pseudo Invincibles. Each representative was the cream of the crop from their respective race. Mo Duona was the undisputed representative of the devils. As for the immortals, Dao Cheng was sent instead of Xuan Wuji.

As for the five elementals, Topsoil Spirit was naturally the representative. Soulcurse of the celestial chasm race, Long Yingyue of the dragon race, Ming Yue of the nether race, Ancient Forge of the primordial giant race...

All of them were geniuses on the Heaven Index. Of the eighteen Heaven Index geniuses, more than half of them had arrived....
