Chapter 2182: Chaotic Battle, Chaotic Slaughter (1) (Teaser)

"Su Yu!"

The moment Su Yu ran off with his people, someone roared in fury. Su Yu again! The two Venerables weren't even dead! Why was he running off with them?

At that, Su Yu stopped and looked at Venerable Phoenix in confusion. "What is it?"

That further infuriated Venerable Phoenix.

What was it?

Just as she was about to rage further, Su Yu took Venerable Dragon's sea of willpower out. Everyone believed that he was about to kill the dragon, but he suddenly gathered the chaos power in the air before reconstructing a body for the dragon. In the blink of an eye, a confused Venerable Dragon reappeared.

Su Yu said, "I lack physical body power so I can only use some chaos power for this. A recovery is better than no recovery. You nearly died, you fool. Consider the Coiled Dragon Mountain your payment for this. The debt between us is cleared. I don't like owing debts." 

Everyone watched blankly as Su Yu rebuilt Venerable Dragon's body. The new body was weaker than before, but this was still much faster than what the dragon could do through his own efforts. In the blink of an eye, his body had recovered...
