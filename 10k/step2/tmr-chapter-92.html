"This test is unrelated to weapons, techniques, or stances. It mainly measures one's source qi and willpower control."

Now that he had reached the ninth-stage, he had gained the ability to connect with external source qi. His control over source qi had improved considerably as well.

If I use my fist, no, if I use my finger, I can gather my source qi to a single point. That will be easier to accomplish with a finger.

Su Yu made a choice. Even though he was still uncertain if that would work, he had still decided to not use a weapon or martial technique. He would use all his effort to gather source qi in his finger instead. Maybe he could get a higher score that way.
