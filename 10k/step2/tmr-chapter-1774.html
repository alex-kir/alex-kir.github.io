Chapter 1774: War Of Living And Dead (2) (Teaser)

The intense battle raged on.

And the Celestial River of Death was indeed trembling.

At the bottom of the Celestial River of Death, a bottom that no senses could reach, countless slumbering death spirits were stirring. Finally, some opened their eyes. Doubt and confusion filled these eyes.

But some of them differed from the others. Some eyes would shine blindingly right after opening. These were the eyes of the top experts. Even after awakening, they did not move. They did not leave the river. Rather, they waited. They recovered. They observed.

Over the countless years, numerous experts had been brought into this river. Since the creation of this river, almost all dead experts would be brought into its embrace. However, many had remained in slumber.

Somewhere in the Death Realm.

Inside a certain residence.

Liu Hong looked outside, staring into the distance. Noticing several massive figures in combat, he inhaled in shock. Why was the battle so intense?

Were those Daofuses? No, that one guy felt stronger than even a Daofuse! Was that...a heavenly king? Good heavens! It hadn't even been long since he entered the Death Realm. Previously, the strongest combatants of the various battles would only be Daofuses. Why did such an intense battle break out...
