Chapter 1233: Fiery Sea Fall (2) (Teaser)

After inhaling deeply, Wan Tiansheng roared as the sword in his hand turned into a sword of light. The radiance of the sword illuminated the entire Human Realm.

Next, the sea of flames was cut apart.

The sky itself was cut apart as well.

In response to the attack, Fiery Sea King roared and gathered source qi from all directions before throwing a punch. All current human cultural researcher Invincibles were actually warrior Invincibles. Thus, most of them were stronger physically than mentally. Thus, it wasn’t surprising that Fiery Sea King would resort to his fists when he grew desperate enough.

The sword exploded alongside about seven or eight characters. Wan Tiansheng was pushed back as a stream of blood flowed out of his mouth. However, Fiery Sea King wasn't any better off as he was also carrying numerous wounds on his body. He gritted his teeth and asked, "Disassembly method?

"No!" Wan Tiansheng said, "My character technique. I have no need for the disassembly method since I formed it the old-fashioned way."

"Freak."

After coughing another mouthful of blood, Fiery Sea King charged forth as the battle resumed.
