Chapter 1569: Suppressing Myriad Races (3) (Teaser)

Fate Pseudo Emperor's voice rang out with a sigh, "Su Yu, why must you do this? Things have not escalated to a point where you need to drag everyone down with you. By summoning an uncontrollable existence like this, the entire Myriad Realms might fall." 

Far away, the old turtle also said in shock, "Su Yu, stop the summon. You're trying to die with everyone. This existence...has surpassed the level allowed in this world. Just look at how his appearance is triggering the laws. You...what are you doing?"

Su Yu was confused. What was the meaning of that? Everything seemed really complicated.

Tiangu's expression was also changing repeatedly at the realization that Su Yu was really capable of summoning such an existence. He coldly said, "Su Yu, if you want to die together, remember that I've lived a far longer life than you! This is not a loss for me! At present, the Allheaven Battlefield is not yet capable of holding such an existence. If you really want to fight, wait. The thousand-year-limit will end soon. This backer of yours...might not really be unequalled." 

Su Yu's confusion deepened. However, he did not show it. Smart as he was, he managed to catch some of the information in Tiangu's words.

Thousand-year-limit.

Holding...yet capable of holding…

Over the years, the Allheaven Battlefield had been closing and opening itself to the various realms repeatedly, as though it was operating in accordance to a certain set of rules. And according to Tiangu, the Allheaven Battlefield was yet capable of holding an existence at Old Zhou's level....
