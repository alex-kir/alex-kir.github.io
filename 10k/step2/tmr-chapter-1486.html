Chapter 1486: Death Tunnel Opens (3) (Teaser)

Xing Yue maintained her indifferent expression, but she was greatly enjoying this feeling inwardly. What an odd sensation. So this was how it felt to master the art of persuasion. So she could be a leader as well. Only some lies were needed and these people would all trust her over He Tu.

Xing Yue started wondering if she should lead these people and kill Su Yu instead? But...she found herself slightly unwilling to part with Su Yu anymore.

After all, Su Yu was the one who had allowed her to enjoy this sense of superiority only someone intelligent could feel. She had easily become their leader. This was temporary, but it still felt amazing.

As for He Tu's feelings, why should she care?

With a sneer, Xing Yue looked into the distance and said, "In that case, let's start. Kill the immortals."

"Sure."

Nine death sovereigns vanished with a flicker.

Only He Tu and Dummy were left behind. Dummy stared at He Tu blankly while He Tu gnashed his teeth...
